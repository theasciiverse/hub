Rain streams down the terminal in cyan strokes and dots. Switch on the
thunderstorm and it pours harder and brighter, and now and then a bolt of
lightning forks down from the sky, flares and fades. With sound on, the
rain patters along and thunder rolls in a moment after each strike.

## Controls

- **t**: thunderstorm on or off (Asciiverse starts in the storm)
- **s**: speed: fast, medium, slow
- **m**: sound on or off (needs `ffplay` from FFmpeg)
- **v**: volume: quiet, normal, loud
- **q** or **Esc**: quit

## Options

From a terminal: `terminal-rain --sound` starts with sound,
`--rain-color` and `--lightning-color` take black, red, green, yellow,
blue, magenta, cyan or white, and `--speed` and `--volume` set where they
start. `terminal-rain --help` lists them all.
