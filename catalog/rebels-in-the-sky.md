**SPACE PIRATES. BASKETBALL. WHY NOT.**

You are the captain of a crew of space pirates, and in this galaxy disputes are settled on the court. Recruit players from across the planets, each with their own skills and quirks, fit out your spaceship and fly it between worlds to trade, explore and go on space adventures. When you meet another crew, challenge them to a match and watch it play out, play by play, with your tactics deciding the game.

Other crews can be bots or other players: Rebels in the Sky finds other players online and lets you challenge their crews, or join tournaments.

- **Build a crew** of players with their own stats, skills and personalities
- **Fly between planets** to trade, explore and find new players
- **Basketball matches** played out on the court, with your tactics
- **Online play** against other players' crews, and tournaments

## Controls

- **Left** / **Right**: switch tabs
- **Up** / **Down**: choose in a list; **[** / **]**: change the selection
- **Tab**: cycle the view
- The keys for each action are shown on its button, and the mouse works too
- **?**: help; **Esc**: back or quit

## Options

- `-l`: play offline, without looking for other players
- `-a`: no audio
- `-f`: no local bot crews, only other players'
