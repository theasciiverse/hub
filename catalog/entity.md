**ONE NINJA. ONE HUNDRED ROOMS. ONE SECOND IS A LONG TIME.**

A switch. A door. And between you and them, a room that wants you dead!

Mines line the floors. Drones patrol the halls, zapping, chasing, firing lasers and chainguns. Gauss turrets wait for a clear line of sight. Thwomps hang overhead, waiting to slam down. Homing rockets come screaming after you. And the clock is ticking: every five rooms share ninety seconds, and every speck of gold you grab buys you two more.

You are fast. You are very fast. Sprint, leap, kick off a wall, kick off the other one, slide down the shaft with your hand on the brick and fling yourself across the gap at full tilt. Momentum is everything, and the only fall that kills is a hard landing, so a ninja who keeps moving keeps living. Touch the switch, the door opens, dive through. Miss, and you are in pieces... and back at the start in the blink of an eye, ready to try again.

- **A hundred rooms in twenty episodes,** each one teaching one trick and then throwing them all at you together: first light, minefield, hall of thwomps, the last door, ski jump, crush hour, stepping on heads, gun deck, citadel, siege, and at the very end, a room called entity, the whole game in one level
- **Every kind of trouble:** mines, zap drones, chasers, laser and chaingun drones, gauss turrets, floor guards, thwomps, homing launchers, launch pads, bounce blocks, one-way platforms, locked doors and trap doors that slam shut behind you
- **N's physics, to the number:** momentum, wall jumps and wall slides on the very values of the original
- **The whole room on one screen,** filling your terminal, with the ninja drawn in fine Braille dots
- **Build your own:** a level editor, and a route bot that finds a way through your level to prove it can be done

Ninety seconds. Five rooms. **HOW FAST CAN YOU BE?**

## Controls

- Arrows, WASD or HJKL to run; Up, W, Space or Z to jump, held for height
- Down or S to stop, R to try again, N and P for the next and previous level
- Esc asks before you quit
- Gamepad: d-pad or stick to run, A or B to jump, Y to try again, A, B or
  Start for the next level

Best in a terminal that reports key releases (kitty, foot, Ghostty,
Alacritty), or with a gamepad.

## History

In 2004 Metanet Software released N, a little Flash game about a ninja with ferocious momentum and a very short life. Entity set out on 3 October 2026 to bring that feel to the terminal: the ninja runs on the same physics numbers as N version 1.2, all thirteen kinds of object are here, and the campaign is a hundred rooms of its own, built to teach one thing at a time and then test you on all of it.
