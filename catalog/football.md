**FOURTH AND SHORT. WHAT'S THE CALL?**

Four fifteen-minute quarters against Granite Falls. Each down, call a run up the middle, a sweep, a short pass, a long pass, a screen, a punt or a field goal try; on defence, stack the line, play it balanced, drop back in coverage or blitz. Downs and distance, touchdowns and the kick after, punts and touchbacks, sacks, interceptions, fumbles and safeties, on a hundred-yard field with the line to gain marked.

- **The ball in flight** down the field strip, with play-by-play.
- **The field goal's distance** shown before you try it.
- **Your record:** wins, losses, ties and the biggest margin.

## Controls

- **1** to **7**, **↑ ↓** and **Enter**, or a click: call the play
- **Enter**: the second half, and a new game at the end
- **N**: a new game; **Tab**: the rules; **M**: sound on or off; **Esc**: quit
