**THINGS ARE FALLING.** Spheres, boxes, tumbling crates, green diamonds,
cyan bonuses, the odd red mine, all of them dropping out of the sky toward
a little grid below. You have two machine guns, a mine emitter and the
mouse. Most of what reaches the grid explodes, and your health goes with it.

Short, precise bursts bring things down, and the ammunition runs out
faster than you think. Shoot a crate for more rounds,
or a blue one for rapid fire that turns your bullets red. Shoot a green
diamond to patch your armour, a cyan one for two thousand points. Drop a
mine into a crowd and hit it with a bullet: the blast takes everything
near it. The longer you last, the faster it all falls.

And all of it is real 3D: a lit, shaded scene drawn many times a cell and
turned into characters, ` .,:;=+tIYVRBM` from dark to bright, in the
sixteen colours of the Windows console it was made for. Debris tumbles,
blasts fade, guns recoil, and the aiming box follows your mouse with its
scales and ammunition bars. A narrator warns you when the armour runs low.

At the end: your score, a remark about it, a page of statistics, and the
Hall of Fame, where the author's own 100,000 is still waiting to be
beaten.

## Controls

- **Mouse:** aim; **left button** fires both machine guns, **right
  button** drops a mine
- **Arrows** aim, **Space** fires, **M** drops a mine
- **Escape:** back to the menu

The original's letters still work after the command: `a` sharpest
antialiasing, `n` none, `f` frames per second, `m` male narrator, `c` no
debris, `p` twice the debris. `--truecolor` colours each character with
its real colour instead of the sixteen.
