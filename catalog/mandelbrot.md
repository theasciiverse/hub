**ZOOM IN. THEN ZOOM IN AGAIN. A HUNDRED TIMES.**

The Mandelbrot set, drawn in characters and colour bands. It opens on
the whole set, fitted to your terminal. Steer anywhere along its edge
and zoom, and the spirals, seahorses and tiny copies of the set keep
coming, a hundred levels deep, until the view is magnified about five
billion times. In a 256-colour terminal the bands run from black through
red, orange and yellow to white.

Every point is worked out live as you move, from Ken Perlin's famous
one-line Mandelbrot, with more detail the deeper you go.

**Pick a spot on the edge. Dive.**

## Controls

- **Arrow keys**: move the view
- **+** or **=**: zoom in
- **-**: zoom out
- **q**, **x** or **Ctrl+C**: quit

The most detail lives along the boundary of the black shape: try the
valley between the two big bulbs, or the tips of the small buds around them.
