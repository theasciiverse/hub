**GO INTO THE SEWERS AND ELIMINATE THE SOURCE OF SLIME!**

In the not-too-distant future, THE YEAR 1999, fallout from THE WAR has caused RADIOACTIVE MUTANT SLIMES to appear in the sewers of THE CITY. You are a GENETICALLY-MODIFIED PRECOG SUPER-SOLDIER, whose free will was in part traded for the power to PREDICT THE OUTCOME OF COMBAT ENCOUNTERS. Six floors down, the Source of Slime is waiting.

slime99 is a roguelike with no dice. Down the side of the screen you can see your future: the stack of every attack you will make (Hit 5, Hit 7, Cleave 3, Skewer), every defence you will put up (Armour 2, Dodge, Revenge, and somewhere down there, Die), and every tech you have ready. Each fight takes the next card off the top. The whole game is knowing what is coming and deciding what to do about it.

- **Bend fate.** Abilities let you stash the top of a stack for later, swap the top two, or discard one outright, so the Die waiting three defends down never comes up
- **Techs:** blink across the room, attract slimes to you or repel them, make your next hit a crit or your next enemy miss, teleport whatever you hit next, or skip a slime's attack entirely
- **Slimes with tricks**, and every one shows it when you hit it: divide slimes split in two, swap slimes trade places with you, teleport slimes vanish and take you with them, goo slimes turn the floor beneath them to sludge, curse slimes slip misses into your futures, and upgrade slimes, struck, deal you better attacks, defends or techs
- **Toxic sludge** in the sewer channels, bridges over it, and doors to close behind you
- **A reward on the way down:** "Good work soldier," says the commander, and you choose a new ability

## Controls

- **Arrows**, **WASD** or **HJKL**: move, and walk into slimes to fight
- **t**: use the top tech (aim it with the movement keys); **Escape**: cancel
- **1** to **8**: your abilities; **Space**: wait; **x**: examine
- **Escape**: the menu

## Options

- `--rgb`, `--ansi`, `--greyscale`: for terminals without true colour

## History

Stephen Sherratt, who makes games as gridbugs, wrote slime99 in seven days for the 7DRL Challenge of March 2020, on chargrid, his own Rust library for games drawn in characters, and released it that summer. It is part of a line of his seven-day roguelikes, one most years since 2018.
