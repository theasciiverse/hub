Can you hear me? Two people have just been synthesised into one body to escape the island of Anglophone Atlantis, and you are both of them. In your pocket is a letter-remover: point it at a thing and take a letter out of its name, and the thing changes. A pear becomes a pea, a crate becomes a rate. A big, generous puzzle game built entirely on wordplay.

## How to play

Type what you want to do, in plain words, and press Enter:

- **look**, **inventory** (or **i**), **examine** something (or **x**)
- **north**, **south**, **east**, **west**, **up**, **down** (or **n**, **s**, **e**, **w**, **u**, **d**)
- **take**, **drop**, **open**, **push** things; **help** or **about** in most games
- **save** and **restore**; **quit** to stop

Your saved games are kept in `~/.local/share/counterfeit-monkey`.
