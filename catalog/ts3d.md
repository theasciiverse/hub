**KILL THE ENEMIES BEFORE THEY KILL YOU. LUCKILY, THEY AREN'T VERY SMART.**

You start on the sand beside a pond, under a wide cyan sky, with lily pads on the water and two frogs coming your way. They are not here to be friends. Swing round with Q and E, line them up and fire: your shots are slow red bolts that burst where they land, and whatever you kill leaves a pale spirit rising into the air.

Thing Shooter 3D is a first-person shooter drawn entirely in your terminal. Walls, sand, sky and every creature are coloured characters, rendered in real 3D by a ray-casting engine its author wrote himself. Ten levels wait in a row, each opened by winning the one before:

- **The Pond**, then **Entrance**, a cave mouth in the dunes with a goblin and a bat waiting inside
- **Gobstopper** and **Halls**, whose walls spell out the game's name in big letters, with bats in the halls and green arrows of health to run into
- **Columns** and **All Sides**: goblins among the pillars, then goblins and frogs on every side, and goblins are a touch faster than you are
- **Dance Off** and **Double Trouble**: demons that hurl flaming skulls, and when one falls, its own skull comes for you
- **Ensemble**: frogs, goblins, bats and demons, all at once
- **Medley**: a giant eye that backs away as it spits frogs at you. Bring it down and it comes back changed, spitting bats, then goblins, then demons, and each time it changes it showers you with health

Your bullets are slow, so lead your shots, or shoot theirs out of the air. And your legs keep going on their own: the walking keys switch walking on and off, so you can set off sideways and keep both hands for aiming. The bigger your terminal, the easier the game gets.

## Controls

- **W**, **A**, **S**, **D**: walk forward, left, back or right; press again to stop
- **Q** and **E**: turn left and right
- **Space**: shoot; hold **Shift** or turn on **Caps Lock** to shoot while you walk and turn
- **P**: pause; **X**: leave the level
- In the menus: **W** and **S** or the arrows to move, **D** or **Enter** to choose, **A** or **Esc** to go back

## History

Jude Melton-Houghton began Thing Shooter 3D in May 2019, in C, and wrote nearly everything it stands on himself: the 3D renderer, the JSON parser that reads its levels, even the test framework. "In making this game, I set out not to make a game, but to design a system," he wrote in its About screen. So every texture is a text file in which each character is a pixel, and every creature, level and menu is plain data anyone can open and change. Version 1.0 came that November, and he kept polishing until 1.16.2 in October 2022, calling it, honestly, mostly finished.
