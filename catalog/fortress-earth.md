The first one comes down twenty days in. Nobody knows what they are. HQ
calls them the Arrivals, which is the polite word for it.

They land near the great cities and spill out across the land, red and
hungry, tile by tile. You have ten armies (Marines, Infantry, a couple of
Squads, the Air Force and HQ itself) and a map of the whole planet that
breathes. Throw them at Cairo. Hold Paris tight. Turn the Air Force loose
and let it hunt.

Every patch of ground is fought over. Your units pour into the gaps like
water, the enemy shoves back wherever it is strong, and the world goes
white or red one tile at a time. Your armies can fly; the war on the
ground stops at the shoreline.

Ten landings are coming, each one sooner than the last. Smash a landing
site outright, or ring it in once it runs dry and let it starve. Lose six
cities at once, or every last army, and that's it: Earth has fallen.

Your generals will keep you posted. Mostly with bad news.

## Controls

- **0** to **9**: pick an army, then:
  - **G** and a city's letter: go there
  - **T**: hold tight around the city
  - **E**: spread out further
  - **D**: go after the enemy anywhere
- **Space**: pause
- **F1**, **?** or **H**: how to play
- **Q** or **Esc**: leave (it asks first), **Ctrl+C**: leave at once

Fortress Earth shows the whole world at once and needs a terminal of at
least 186 columns by 55 rows: a big window, or a smaller font.

## Options

- `--demo`: watch the computer fight the war
