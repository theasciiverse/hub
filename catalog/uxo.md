**BLAST YOUR WAY OUT TO THE REAL WORLD!**

3:14 in the morning. One key pressed, and the machine pulled Dot in!

Every night at 3:14 a cleaning program called **purged** tidies the disk. Tonight it has decided the tidiest disk is an empty one. Its charges are planted all through the system, every last one set to wipe everything at four o'clock. Dot included!

Dot comes down in an old garden screensaver, where purged has rewritten every creature to guard its charges. Grubs, ladybeetles, hedgehogs that pretend to be hedges, frogs, rams, crows... and something big and prickly guarding the way out.

In here every bomb is a kill, and purged's creatures can't start one. So drop a bomb, run, and BOOM: the blast rips out in a cross and flattens everything in its path. Line them up right and one sets off the next, and the next, and the next... Set off purged's charges, and watch out, because they blow for real! Caught in your own blast? That one's on you.

But not every kill goes off. Some hang, and lie there grey and smoking, live... until a blast finds them, or you kick one, or it just decides it's time. Every bomb is unexploded ordnance. Every single one.

- **Bombs AND brains!** Push crates for cover, or onto switches to hold the gates open. Hunt down keys for locked doors, lost parts for the sealed sockets. Some stages are a race, some are a puzzle, most are both!
- **The surge!** Out in the open, your bombs flood outward every way at once, round corners. Mind where it spreads!
- **Ride your enemies!** Beat a Hopper or a Ram and it might leave an egg. Hatch it, climb on: hop clean over hedges, or charge. And when a blast catches you, your mount takes the hit.
- **Adventure, alone or with a friend:** nine stages through the Screensaver for Dot alone, or Dot and Caret together, from Windfall Lane, past the Hedge Maze and the Cider Press, all the way to the Bramblehog.
- **Battle for up to four!** Six arenas, from the Ice Rink to the Catacombs. Blown up? Ride the edge and lob bombs back in for revenge. Let the clock run down and the walls close in.
- **Eighteen power-ups, and the dreaded skull.** Catch a disease and you might drop bombs nonstop, run backwards, turn invisible, or watch every bomb you drop fizzle. Bump into a rival and it's theirs instead!
- **Every gamepad is a player,** and two can share the keyboard.

Can you stop purged before four o'clock? **IF YOU SEE ME RUNNING, TRY TO KEEP UP!**

## Controls

- **Move**: stick, d-pad, **W A S D** or the **arrow keys**
- **Bomb**: A or X on a pad, **Space** (W A S D player) or **Enter** (arrows player); press again standing on your bomb with the line bomb to lay a row
- **Ride** (your mount's hop or charge): Y, **E** or **.**
- **Action**: B, **F** or **/**: throw what you carry, else punch the bomb ahead, else pick up the bomb underfoot, else set off your oldest remote bomb
- **Push a crate**: walk into it and lean on it a moment
- **Start** or **P**: pause (in the adventure, bomb then starts the stage over, for a life), **+** and **-**: zoom (**0**: automatic), **Esc** or Select: back to the title
