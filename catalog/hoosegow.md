You blew up a railroad tunnel, the sheriff got word ahead of time, and now you and Muddy Charlie are in the jail cell of a frontier town. There is a broken stool, a barred window, a stranger snoring on the bench and a deputy watching through the bars. Muddy has his harmonica; you have a pocketwatch and a scrap of paper. Time to plan a jailbreak.

## How to play

Type what you want to do, in plain words, and press Enter:

- **look**, **inventory** (or **i**), **examine** something (or **x**)
- **north**, **south**, **east**, **west**, **up**, **down** (or **n**, **s**, **e**, **w**, **u**, **d**)
- **take**, **drop**, **open**, **push** things; **help** or **about** in most games
- **save** and **restore**; **quit** to stop

Your saved games are kept in `~/.local/share/hoosegow`.
