**THERE'S GOING TO BE A HANGING. YOURS.**

You blew up that railroad tunnel just the way Muddy Charlie planned it.
He just didn't plan on the sheriff getting word ahead of time. Now the
silver dollars of the Missouri, Kansas & Texas Line are evidence, you
and Muddy are locked in Sheriff Cheney's sturdy brick jail, and a
federal marshal is coming for you at eight o'clock tomorrow morning. The
sheriff's news for his deputy: "There's going to be a hanging!" And on
his way out the door: "Jimbo, don't screw up."

"Why are you not surprised to have landed right back in the hoosegow
after another one of Muddy's dubious plans? Will you ever learn?" Look
around. A broken stool. A small window above your head. A stranger in a
black suit, snoring on the bench. Muddy, tapping his harmonica on his
arm. And Deputy Jimbo, on the other side of the bars. Somewhere in all
that is a way out before eight.

Ben Collins-Sussman and Jack Welch's comic Western, "A Wild West Wreck",
won the Casual Gameplay Design Competition #7. Type "hints" when you're
stuck, "or just roll into town guns a-blazin'."

**Bust out by eight. Or hang.**

## How to play

Type what you want to do, in plain words, and press Enter:

- **look**, **inventory** (or **i**), **examine** something (or **x**)
- **north**, **south**, **east**, **west**, **up**, **down** (or **n**, **s**, **e**, **w**, **u**, **d**)
- **take**, **drop**, **open**, **push** things; **help** or **about** in most games
- **save** and **restore**; **quit** to stop

Your saved games are kept in `~/.local/share/hoosegow`.
