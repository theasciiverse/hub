**YOU'VE BOOKED FIVE DAYS AT A CABIN IN THE FOREST.**

You arrive close to midnight, worn out, looking forward to falling asleep to the sound of rain. Through the trees to the south you can see the lights of the cabin. It is raining, lightly for now. Somewhere there is a forecast for the next five days, if you can find it.

There is nothing here to fight. No monsters, no combat, nobody to kill. The only thing you can lose is your motivation, and the weather is quietly working on it. Every step out in the rain wears it down a little. So does wading through flood water, and stumbling about in the dark. Run out of it and you give up, pack your bags and go home early.

So you look after yourself. Shelter under a tree until the shower passes. Hop from stone to stone across the water instead of splashing through it. Go looking for the things that make the stay better: an umbrella, a pair of gumboots, a lantern for the dark paths, a shovel to dig a ditch and drain a flooded clearing, a map of the forest, and that weather report, so you know whether tomorrow is a day for walking or for staying in.

And then, once a day, the small pleasures. Sit in the cozy chair and just listen to the rain. Brew a cup of tea, if you have found tea leaves for the pot. Leave a flower at the long-abandoned altar in the ruins. Stand at the edge of a lake so vast it puts your whole life into perspective. Then back to the cabin for eight hours of sleep, with the rain still falling outside.

Keep your spirits up for five days, and the break turns out to be just what you needed.

## Controls

- **Arrows**, **WASD** or **HJKL**: walk; **Shift** with WASD or HJKL: walk quickly
- **Space**: wait two minutes; **.**: wait an hour
- **g**: pick up or put down; **x**: examine
- **m**: map; **r**: weather report; **f**: lantern; **p**: push rocks; **e**: dig
- **Escape**: the menu, with save and quit

## Options

- `--rgb`, `--ansi`, `--greyscale`: for terminals without true colour

## History

The 7DRL Challenge asks for a roguelike in seven days, and in March 2022 Stephen Sherratt answered it with one that has nothing to kill. Spend a few days in the forest, in the rain, his page for it says, and above all, sit inside and enjoy the rain. A roguelike as gentle as they come.
