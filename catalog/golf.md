**NINE HOLES. MIND THE WATER.**

A nine-hole course, par 36, seen from above: tee, fairway, rough, trees, sand, water and a green with its pin. Choose a club from driver to putter, the aim and how hard to swing. Wind changes from hole to hole, rough and sand cost distance, and a hard swing can hook or slice. On the green the view closes in, and the green has a slope to read.

- **A caddie** suggests every shot; change any of it, or click the map to aim there.
- **A scorecard** with par, your strokes and how far over or under you stand.
- **Your best round** and your holes in one, kept between games.

## Controls

- **↑ ↓**: club; **← →**: aim (**,** and **.** for fine aim); **+** **-**, **PgUp** **PgDn** or **1** to **0**: swing
- **Enter** or **Space**: swing; a click on the map: aim there
- **C**: ask the caddie again; **N**: a new round; **Tab**: the rules; **M**: sound on or off; **Esc**: quit
