**SOMETHING IS HIDING. FIND IT.**

Three small hunts in one, each on a ten by ten field, each with its own kind of clue:

- **Hurkle:** one shy hurkle. Every guess tells you which way to go: north, south-west, and so on. Five guesses.
- **Mugwump:** four mugwumps. Every guess tells you how far away each hidden one is, but not which is which. Ten guesses to find them all.
- **Depth Charge:** a submarine somewhere in a sea ten deep. Every charge tells you which way it lies and whether it is deeper or shallower. Five charges.

Press **H** to shade every spot that still fits the clues, and watch the field shrink as you close in. Your finds, misses and fewest guesses are kept for each hunt.

## Controls

- **← → ↑ ↓**: aim; **PgUp** and **PgDn** (or the mouse wheel): set the depth
- **Enter**, **Space** or a click: guess
- **H**: shade what is still possible; **V**: choose another hunt; **Tab**: the rules
- **M**: sound on or off; **Esc**: back to the choice of hunt, then quit
