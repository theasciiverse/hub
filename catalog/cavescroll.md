**THE CAVE NEVER ENDS. NEITHER DOES THE GAP.**

You fly `>` through a cave that keeps coming at you from the right. Its
roof and floor wander up and down, sometimes leaving room to spare and
sometimes barely a slot to squeeze through. Steer up and down and stay in
the gap.

Pick up money `$` for points and ammunition `+` for your gun, and watch
for the enemies `@`, which drift towards you as they come. Shoot them for
extra points, or get out of their way. You start with five shots.

## Controls

- **↑** / **↓**: steer up and down
- **Space**: shoot
- **q** or **Esc**: quit
