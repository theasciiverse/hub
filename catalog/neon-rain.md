A city street at night in the rain, seen from straight above. Neon signs
hum over the shopfronts, buzz off now and then or lose a letter, and their
colours run down the wet road in shimmering streaks. Cars, trucks and
scooters swish past with their headlight beams sweeping the tarmac, and
now and then a police car flashes red and blue. People under umbrellas
walk the pavements, wait at the crossing while the traffic stops, or mill
about in a lit lobby. Steam drifts from the manholes, rain streaks pick up
the colour of whatever light they fall through, puddles ripple, and once
in a while lightning flashes over the rooftops.

Every start lays out a new street to fit the window.

## Controls

- **Any key**: quit

## Options

From a terminal, `neon-rain -s 7` lays out the same street every time.
It draws in full RGB colour, and in 256 colours on terminals that do not
report truecolor.
