**AN AQUARIUM. IN ASCII. THAT'S IT, AND IT'S WONDERFUL.**

Put it on a spare screen and leave it there. Fish in every colour drift by, blowing bubbles that rise to the surface. Seaweed sways in front of a little castle. Every so often something bigger comes along: a shark that will happily eat one of your fish, a whale spouting water, a sea monster rising through the waves, a sailing ship, a family of ducks, a swan, a pod of leaping dolphins, or a fishhook that comes down and catches someone.

Asciiquarium has been a favourite terminal toy since 2005.

## Controls

- **p**: pause
- **r**: redraw (the aquarium starts over)
- **q**: quit

## History

Kirk Baucom wrote Asciiquarium in Perl, on top of his own animation module. This edition is a faithful port to C: the same art, the same creatures and the same behaviour, in one small program with nothing else to install.
