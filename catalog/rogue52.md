Rogue as it was in 1982, a year after 3.6. You still go down through
the Dungeons of Doom for the Amulet of Yendor, a new map on every level,
monsters from A to Z, magic you learn by trying it, death for good and
gold for a score; but the stairs now lead up as well as down, you can
ask what a trap is, and **D** lists everything you have identified.

## Controls

- **hjkl yubn**: move, the last four diagonally; with **Shift** run that
  way
- **f** and a direction: move until something turns up; **t**: throw,
  **z**: zap a wand in a direction, **^** and a direction: name a trap
- **i**: inventory, **d**: drop, **D**: what you have discovered
- **e** eat, **q** quaff, **r** read, **w** wield, **W** wear, **T** take
  off armour, **P** put on and **R** remove a ring
- **s**: search for traps and secret doors, **.**: rest
- `>` and `<`: stairs down and up
- **?**: all the commands, **/**: what a symbol is
- **S**: save and quit, **Q**: quit

## Saving

**S** saves the game to your data folder. To carry on, start it from a
shell as `rogue52 -r`; a saved game is deleted once it is restored.

## History

Rogue 5.2 is Michael Toy, Glenn Wichman and Ken Arnold's Berkeley
version of 1982, between the 3.6 of BSD and the 5.4 that most later
ports grew from. Nicholas J. Kisseberth (Yendor) restored it in the
Roguelike Restoration Project, releases 5.2.1 to 5.2.3, and keeps it at
rogueforge. This edition keeps its files in your data folder and fixes an overflow in the top
ten. It is part of the Roguelike Restoration Project in the Asciiverse
catalog.
