The party parrot, the dancing kakapo of a thousand chat rooms, in ASCII
art in your terminal: it bobs and spins in a loop, changing colour every
frame, for as long as you let it.

## Controls

- **Any key**: quit

## Options

From a terminal, `-delay 25` makes it a fast parrot (the default is 75
milliseconds a frame), `-orientation aussie` turns it upside down, and
`-loops 5` stops after five dances. `-path` points at a folder of your own
`.animation` files, and `terminal-parrot <name>` plays one of them.
