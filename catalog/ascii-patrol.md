"Be careful, they come with mega bombers fleet. Their bombs make huge
craters in the ground. Good luck cadet!"

So says the Commander, and off you roll: one cadet, one moon buggy, and
a whole planet's worth of aliens between sector @ and sector Z. The
ground never stops coming. Craters open in front of your wheels,
boulders sit in the road, saucers swing overhead dropping bombs that
punch fresh holes where the road used to be. Your cannon fires two ways
at once, straight ahead to crack the rocks and straight up to pop
whatever is hovering, and your jump has to land on all three wheels or
the suspension goes and so do you.

It is Moon Patrol, lovingly rebuilt in ASCII art by someone who grew up
on it: a parallax sky of mountains and cities, a thumping tracker
soundtrack, and between sectors a radio drama with your Commander about
minefields, missiles, alien eggs and plants that may not be as friendly
as they look. Two full missions, Mission One and The Lowering, five
sectors each. Make it through the minefield and the Commander may even
call you sergeant.

And it is your face on the radio: build an avatar from hair, eyes, nose
and chin in the Player Profile before you set off.

## Controls

- **Right** or **D**: speed up (hold)
- **Left** or **A**: brake (hold)
- **Up** or **W**: jump
- **Down** or **S**: duck (hold)
- **Enter** or **L**: fire, forward and up at once
- **Esc** or **Q**: leave the level
- **Tab**: save a screen shot to the data folder

The letters can be changed under Keyboard Input in the menu. Holding
keys needs a terminal that reports key releases (kitty, Ghostty, foot,
Alacritty, the Linux console); elsewhere the keys are sticky: tap Right
to speed up, Left to slow back down. The Control Panel sets music and
effects volume and the colour mode.
