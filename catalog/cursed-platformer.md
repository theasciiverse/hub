**THE PHARAOH HAS THE PRINCESS. YOU HAVE AN @.**

The evil pharaoh Tutankhascii has carried a princess off into his underground pyramid, and somebody has to go down after her. That somebody is you: one brave `@`, who enjoys jumping very much and will tell you so.

Cursed Pl@tformer is a classic side-on platformer drawn entirely in text. Run, jump twice your own height, climb, flip switches that open doors somewhere else, ride moving platforms across pits, and keep out of the way of munchers, bats and spikes on the way down.

- **The pharaoh's ten levels**, from Muncher Meadows and the Railway to the Pit of Bats and the Pyramid of Tuthanascii
- **Four more by Ian T. Jacobsen**, quiet and strange, behind EXTENDED on the title screen
- **Then across the sea**: after the pyramid, the pharaoh's map leads to a jungle, along a road in the treetops, down a sacred cenote, through a ball court and up the steps of a Maya pyramid, eight levels more
- **Music and sound**: Kevin MacLeod's "Midnight Tale" and a jump with real spring in it
- **Progress is saved** at every level exit; Continue picks up where you left off
- **Make your own levels**: they are plain text files, a map and its colours

## Controls

- **Left** / **Right**: walk; **Space** or **Up**: jump
- **R**: restart the level; **M**: music on or off; **C**: colours on or off; **Q**: quit
- Gamepad: d-pad or left stick to walk, **A** or d-pad up to jump, **Y** restarts, **Back** quits

Holding an arrow to walk works in a terminal with the kitty keyboard protocol, such as Ghostty, kitty, WezTerm or foot. In other terminals a tap starts walking and the next tap stops.
