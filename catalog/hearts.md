**DON'T TAKE THE QUEEN. OR TAKE EVERYTHING.**

Thirteen cards each, and before the first trick three of them go to a neighbour: left, then right, then across, then hold. Choose well. That queen of spades you just passed on may come straight back to haunt you. The two of clubs leads, you follow suit if you can, and every heart you take costs you a point. The queen costs thirteen.

Unless you dare to take them all. Every heart and the black lady too, and you've shot the moon: the whole hand's points land on the other three instead!

Ada, Bo and Cyd make it hard. They pass you their most dangerous cards, duck under the winning card, and the moment you lead a suit they've run out of, down comes the queen. The match ends when someone reaches 100, and the lowest score wins.

- **Three computer players** who know exactly where the queen should go: to you
- **The full rules:** passing, hearts broken, no points on the first trick, shooting the moon
- **A match to 100,** hand after hand

**WHO'S GOT HER?**

## Controls

- **Left** / **Right**: choose a card
- **Space**: mark a card to pass; **Enter**: pass the three marked
- **Enter**: play a card
- **Enter**: deal the next hand, when it's yours to deal
- **Esc**: leave (asks first in a game)
- **Enter** on the setup screen: deal; **Q**: quit

## History

Pieczasz began tty.cards in April 2026 with one bold idea: type `ssh tty.cards` and you are sitting at a card table, with no download, no browser and no sign-up form, your SSH key your only ticket in. Over seven months and more than eight hundred commits it grew five games, lobbies, a turn clock and rated tables. Asciiverse dealt each of the five out as a title of its own and filled every table with computer players, so a game is on whenever you are. Hearts always seats four: you and three rivals.
