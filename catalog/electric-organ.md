**A CORRUPTION IS TAKING OVER RETRO-FUTURE CITY, AND ONLY YOU CAN STOP IT.**

You step out of the evac zone with a pistol, one heart, two lungs, a stomach, a liver and an appendix that does nothing. Four levels down, under the streets, waits the CORRUPTOR. To reach it you will fight, scavenge, and above all swap out your insides: harvest organs from the dead, carry them in containers, have them installed at the clinic, and hope the cybernetic one you bought doesn't need a CyberCore you can't afford.

Electric Organ is a traditional roguelike with an emphasis on ranged combat, where your character sheet is your body.

- **Organs are your levels.** Each heart raises your health, each lung brings back more oxygen, each stomach makes more of your food. Organs come radioactive, vampiric, damaged, embedded, transient, or prolific: a tumour that copies itself
- **Cronenberg weapons.** Grow a biological pistol or shotgun into your body and fire it with your own health; trade a hand for a claw and hit like a truck, as long as you never want to hold a gun again
- **A city that fights back:** zombies that rise again unless you destroy the corpse, climbers that come over the debris, trespassers that open doors, boomers that explode, snatchers that run off with your things, poisoners, dividers that split when you hit them, glowers that pour out radiation and venters that fill the streets with smoke
- **Radiation adds up.** Let it fill and you gain a mutation, wanted or not
- **CyberCoinz™** to spend at the gun vendor, the item shop, the organ trader and the clinic
- **Synthesised music.** Every note of the soundtrack and every sound effect is generated live by a synthesizer written in code (`--mute` turns it off)

Get to the fourth level, destroy the CORRUPTOR, and make it back to the evac zone alive. Or take its heart for yourself.

## Controls

- **Arrows**: walk, and walk into enemies to fight
- **f**: fire your weapon; **c**: fire every Cronenberg weapon (costs health); **r**: reload
- **g**: get; **a**: apply; **d**: drop; **u**: unequip
- **o**: your organs; **m**: the message log; **?**: help
- **Escape**: the menu, with save and quit

## Options

- `--mute`: no sound
- `--rgb`, `--ansi`, `--greyscale`: for terminals without true colour

## History

Stephen Sherratt, who makes games as gridbugs, wrote Electric Organ in seven days for the 7DRL Challenge of March 2024, on chargrid, his own Rust library for games drawn in characters, with its music played by caw, a synthesizer library he wrote too. He came back to it in 2025 and 2026 to bring it up to date. It is part of a line of his seven-day roguelikes, one most years since 2018.
