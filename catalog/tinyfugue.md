**SEVERAL WORLDS. ONE KEYBOARD. TOTAL CONTROL.**

TinyFugue, "tf" to its players, has been connecting people to MUDs since the early 1990s. Type `/connect` with a host and a port and you are in. Then make the game yours.

Tell tf what to watch for in the text the MUD sends, and it will light it up (a hilite), make it vanish (a gag), or fire off a macro of your own (a trigger). It keeps a history of every line, so you can `/recall` what scrolled past an hour ago or `/log` the whole session to a file. And it will hold several worlds open at once, switching between them as the action moves.

- **Macros**: from one-word abbreviations to whole programs in tf's own language; its library even solves the Towers of Hanoi, "not very useful except as an example of recursion"
- **Triggers, hilites and gags**: react to, highlight or silence any line
- **Many worlds at once**: name them with `/addworld` and connect with a word
- **A split screen**: output above, input below, and a status line between them
- **Debian's patches**: this edition carries the fixes that have kept it building

## Getting started

- `tinyfugue <host> <port>`: connect straight away
- `/connect <host> <port>`: connect from inside tf
- `/help`: the built-in manual
- `/quit`: quit
