TinTin++ is the console MUD client many players have used for decades:
connect to any MUD, then make it yours with its own scripting language,
triggers and aliases, a mapper that draws the rooms you walk through, and
split screens.

## Getting started

- `#session <name> <host> <port>`: connect, for example `#session bat batmud.bat.org 23`
- `#help`: the built-in manual
- `#end`: quit
