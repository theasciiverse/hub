You wake in the delivery room of a sealed facility, and a voice over the speakers tells you your name is Prey. The Predator is hungry and coming for you. Seven pieces of an environment suit are scattered around the place: collect them all and you can survive the poison gas and escape. A tense game of hiding, listening and running, a little different every time. Its characters swear.

## How to play

Type what you want to do, in plain words, and press Enter:

- **look**, **inventory** (or **i**), **examine** something (or **x**)
- **north**, **south**, **east**, **west**, **up**, **down** (or **n**, **s**, **e**, **w**, **u**, **d**)
- **take**, **drop**, **open**, **push** things; **help** or **about** in most games
- **save** and **restore**; **quit** to stop

Your saved games are kept in `~/.local/share/i-am-prey`.
