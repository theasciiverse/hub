**THE HEARTSTONE IS DOWN THERE SOMEWHERE. BRING IT UP INTO THE DAYLIGHT.**

Every miner in the valley has heard of the Heartstone, and nobody has
seen it. It lies in a cave at the very bottom of your claim, a hundred
tiles of earth below the grass, and the only way down is the pickaxe in
your hand.

Swing it and the dirt gives way, three blows a tile. Coal, iron and copper
glint near the top; push on and the dirt turns to stone, and the stone
gives up silver, then gold, then rubies, and finally diamonds. Fill your
pack, climb back up your ladders, sell the lot at the shop on the surface
and spend it: a stronger pickaxe, a bigger backpack, a brighter lamp, a
sword, a crossbow, a visit to the doctor. Then go down again, deeper than
before.

The ground fights back. Dig under a boulder and it drops on you. Fall more
than four tiles and you feel it. Run out of stamina underground and every
swing and every rung costs you health instead. Cockroaches and worms
wriggle through the upper earth, termites eat the ladders you are counting
on to get home, and far down in the stone the goblins are digging too,
with tools of their own, carrying off the ore you came for.

- **A whole claim to mine:** dirt, stone and bedrock in a side view, your
  lamp lighting a ring of it at a time, every shaft and tunnel your own
- **Everything a miner needs:** ladders, supports to hold the roof up,
  rope to let down a shaft, crossbow bolts, and dynamite on a three-turn
  fuse (light it, then run)
- **Four kinds of trouble:** cockroaches, tunnelling worms, ladder-eating
  termites and ore-stealing goblins
- **Riches by depth:** seven ores and gems, each worth more than the last,
  and the Heartstone waiting under them all
- **Dive after dive:** the claim keeps between sessions, and your best runs
  and every Heartstone brought up go on the scoreboard

## Controls

- **Arrows** or **hjkl**: walk; walk into the ground to dig it
- **.**: wait a turn
- **a**: at the shop (=), sell your findings and buy upgrades and supplies
- **w**: put down a ladder, **s**: put up a support, **r**: let down a rope
- **f** then a direction: fire the crossbow
- **d**: light a stick of dynamite
- **?**: help, **S**: save and quit

## History

In March 2014 Whales, the maker of Cataclysm, took ten days off from
Cataclysm II and dug out a little side project: a side-view mining
roguelike after Boulder Dash and the many small mining games found
online. On the development blog Whales sketched what it should become:
coal, iron and copper to bring up and sell, ladders, supports, dynamite
and crossbow bolts to buy, and enemies from the humble cockroach to worms
building their own tunnels, termites eating your ladders and goblins
racing you for the ore. The blog post ended simply: "It is pretty fun
though!" Twenty-eight commits in, Whales went back to the bigger game.

In 2026 the Asciiverse picked up the pickaxe and built the rest of that
list: the deep layers and their gems, health and its dangers, the rope,
the dynamite, the crossbow and the goblins, and the Heartstone at the
bottom to give every dive somewhere to go.
