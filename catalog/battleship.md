Hide your fleet on a 10x10 sea, then take turns firing at the other
side's sea, one shot each. The first to sink every ship of the other
fleet wins. The game tells you when you have sunk a ship.

Your fleet is a carrier (five squares), a battleship (four), a
destroyer and a submarine (three each) and a patrol boat (two). To set
it up, move to a square and press Enter to lay the ship from there,
press R to turn it, and any other key to keep it. In play, your own sea
is on the left and your shots on the right: `*` is a hit, `~` a miss.

At the start you choose single player, against the computer, or two
players on one keyboard. Two players set up their fleets in turn, with
a screen in between that the next player clears by typing a given
letter three times; in play neither fleet is shown, only the hits and
misses.

## Controls

- **Arrow keys**, **hjkl** or **wasd**: move
- **Enter**: place a ship, or fire
- **R**: turn the ship while setting up
- **Mouse**: left click to place or fire, right click to turn
- **F1** or **?**: help on the controls, **F2** or **!**: how to play
- **q** or **Esc**: quit

## History

Battleship is one of the games abakh wrote for New BSD Games, a collection
of small curses games started in 2018 and named after the classic BSD
games. The collection is packaged by Debian, Ubuntu, openSUSE, Alpine,
Nix, Homebrew, FreeBSD and pkgsrc, and is in the AUR.
