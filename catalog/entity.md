**ONE NINJA. ONE HUNDRED ROOMS. ONE SECOND IS A LONG TIME.**

Run, jump and wall-jump through rooms full of mines, drones, lasers,
turrets, thwomps and homing rockets. Touch the switch, the door opens,
reach it. Gold buys you time, and every five levels share one clock.
Death is instant, and so is the next try.

## The campaign

- 100 levels in twenty episodes of five, each episode teaching one thing
  before it mixes them: jumps and slopes, doors and keys, mines, launch
  pads and bounce blocks, drones, thwomps, lasers, turrets, rockets and
  chainguns, one-way gates, then everything at once
- N's momentum, wall jumps and wall slides, and its rule that only a hard
  landing kills
- The whole room on one screen, filling the terminal, the ninja drawn in
  Braille dots
- A level editor and a route bot that finds a way through your own levels

## Controls

- Arrows, WASD or HJKL to run; Up, W, Space or Z to jump, held for height
- Down or S to stop, R to try again, N and P for the next and previous level
- Esc asks before you quit
- Gamepad: d-pad or stick to run, A or B to jump, Y to try again, A, B or
  Start for the next level

Best in a terminal that reports key releases (kitty, foot, Ghostty,
Alacritty), or with a gamepad.
