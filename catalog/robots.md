Evil robots fill the field, and every time you move, each of them takes
one step straight toward you. They are not very bright: when two robots
run into each other they crash into a junk heap, and any robot that
walks into a heap is destroyed too. You have no weapons, so the only way
to win is to move so that they wreck each other.

Each robot destroyed scores a point. Clear the field and a new one
starts with more robots, up to 64, and a bonus for the level. When you
are cornered, teleport to a random empty spot: it can save you, or drop
you right next to a robot. When a robot finally reaches you the game is
over, and your score goes on the table of the top 16.

## Controls

- **h**, **j**, **k**, **l**: move left, down, up, right
- **y**, **u**, **b**, **n**: move diagonally up left, up right, down
  left, down right
- **.** or **Space**: stay where you are for a turn
- **t**: teleport
- **q** or **F10**: quit

The keys are also shown beside the field while you play. Robots are
`+`, junk heaps `*` and you are `@`.

## History

Robots is one of the oldest BSD games, first written at the University
of California in 1980. Mike Sharov rewrote it from scratch in 2016 for
his bsd-games, the modern Linux port of the BSD games, keeping the play
and the look of the field from Christos Zoulas's 1999 NetBSD version.
Robots is part of BSD Games in the Asciiverse catalog, under the BSD
licence.
