The second part goes deeper into the Empire, to the realm of the Wizard of Frobozz, a wizard who has seen better days and still likes to cast a spell on passing adventurers. There are new treasures, a dragon, a princess and a bank vault to deal with.

## How to play

Type what you want to do, in plain words, and press Enter:

- **look**, **inventory** (or **i**), **examine** something
- **north**, **south**, **east**, **west**, **up**, **down** (or **n**, **s**, **e**, **w**, **u**, **d**)
- **take lamp**, **open mailbox**, **put sword in case**
- **save** and **restore**; **quit** to stop

Your saved games are kept in `~/.local/share/zork2`.
