**PULL UP A CHAIR. THE DEALER IS WAITING.**

A hundred chips, one dealer, one card face down. Bet 10 to 50, look at what you've got, and make the call: hit, stand, double down on that eleven, or cut your losses and surrender half your bet. Then the hole card turns over and the dealer draws to the house rule.

Before the first hand you set the table. Pay blackjack at a generous 3:2, or at 7:5 or 6:5 like the stingier casinos. Make the dealer hit or stand on soft 17. Play from one deck, two, or a six-deck shoe, and choose how deep into the shoe it goes before a reshuffle: card counters, take note. The count of cards dealt is always in the corner.

Had enough for tonight? Save and quit, and your bankroll and table rules are waiting when you come back.

- **Your house rules:** payout, soft 17, decks and penetration, all chosen at the start.
- **Double down and late surrender,** the moves that make blackjack a game of decisions.
- **Saved games,** so a good run doesn't end when you close the terminal.

Blackjack is MarcusBilo's one-on-one casino blackjack in Go.

**HIT OR STAND?**

## Controls

- **Up** / **Down**: move between choices
- **Enter**: choose
- **Backspace**: go back while setting up the table
- **Ctrl+C**: quit (Save and Quit keeps your game)
