Steal a Silmaril from Morgoth's iron crown and escape alive. You are an
elf, a dwarf or a man of the First Age who has found a way into
Angband, the Dark Lord's fortress, unarmed and with little food, and
you must go down a thousand feet to his throne room. Sil-Q is the
continuation of Sil, a roguelike that stays close to Tolkien: no
classes, no wizards or priests, no magical scrolls and no town, but
eight skills to train, songs of power, smithing at the forges, stealth,
and careful, tactical combat with few easy escapes.

Experience comes from meeting new creatures, going deeper and
identifying things as much as from killing, so sneaking past a sleeping
troll is a real choice; the game can be won without ever attacking.
Every level is made afresh, and as time passes you can no longer go back
up past a minimum depth, so you must keep going down. Death is final.

## Controls

- **Arrow keys** or the **number keys** (1 to 9): move; walk into a
  monster to attack it
- **Z**: rest, **z**: stay in place, **.** and a direction: run
- **<** and **>**: take stairs up and down
- **g**: pick up, **i**: inventory, **e**: equipment, **w**: wear or
  wield, **r**: take off, **d**: drop
- **E** eat, **q** quaff a potion, **u** use an item, **a** use a staff,
  **f** and **F** fire from the first or second quiver, **t** throw
- **s**: sing a song, **S**: stealth mode, **Tab**: abilities,
  **0**: smithing at a forge
- **o** and **c**: open and close doors, **T**: tunnel, **D**: disarm
- **x**: examine an item, **l**: look around, **M**: map of the level,
  **@**: character sheet
- **?**: help, **Esc** or **m**: the menu, with options (a keyset with
  hjkl movement is there too)
- **Ctrl-S**: save, **Ctrl-X**: save and quit

## Saving

**Ctrl-X** saves your character and goes back to the title menu; choose
"Open saved character" and give the character's name to carry on.
Saved characters, the high scores, character dumps and options are kept
in your data folder (`~/.local/share/sil-q`).

## History

Sil was made by half and Scatha and released in 2012; its last version,
1.3, came out in 2016. It grew from NPPAngband, a variant of Angband,
but kept little of it. Quirk took up the Sil 1.3 code in 2017 as Sil-Q,
rebalancing abilities, smithing and the enemies with Scatha's feedback,
and released 1.5.0 in 2022 with tiles and a new manual by MicroChasm.
Michael G. Noll has carried it on since 2026, fixing the bugs reported
against 1.5.0 without changing the play.

This edition keeps your files in your own data folder and saves a new
character under its own name. Sil-Q is under the GNU GPL, version 2.
