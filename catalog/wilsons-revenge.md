**THIS TIME IT'S PERSONAL.**

Wilson the chicken is back on the road. Three lanes, traffic coming the
other way, and it only gets faster: switch lanes, jump the barricades
and the people in your path, and grab a shotgun to blast through
whatever you can't get round.

The road is full of pick-ups, and not all of them are friends:

- **~-=**: a shotgun and five shells; **[A]**: three more shells
- **[P+]**: five seconds invincible, double points
- **[P-]**: lose a hundred points
- **[XJ]**: no more jumping, until you find **[OJ]**

Everything you get past is points; everything you shoot is more.

## Controls

- **↑** / **↓**: change lane
- **→**: jump
- **Space**: start, then shoot
- **P**: pause; **R**: run again after a crash; **Q**: quit

## Options

- `--demo`: watch Wilson play himself; any key quits
