**26 LEVELS. ONE AMULET. NOTHING WASTED.**

Brogue strips the roguelike down to what makes it great. There are no classes and no experience levels: you grow by what you find, enchanting the items you choose to trust. Every level is new, with lakes, chasms, bridges, traps and secret doors, and every monster has a way it wants to fight. Free a captive and it fights at your side, learning as it goes.

It is turn-based, with one life and a dungeon that is different every time, but it is also easy to pick up: the mouse-free terminal version is fully playable from the keyboard, and **x** explores for you until something interesting happens.

- **26 levels** to the Amulet of Yendor, and more below for the bold
- **No grinding**: every item and every ally is a choice
- **Allies** you can free, who learn new abilities
- **Seeded games** and recordings you can replay

## Controls

- **Arrow keys**, the number pad or **h j k l y u b n**: move and attack
- **x**: explore; **>** / **<**: travel to the stairs
- **i**: inventory; **z**: rest; **s**: search
- **S**: save and quit; **Q**: give up
- **?**: every command

Your saves, recordings and high scores are kept in `~/.local/share/Brogue`.
