Fifteen years of champagne and carriage rides, and you are in debt. You come back to the Count's chateau hoping for an advance, and find it empty: no Count, no Marie, not even the cook. Somewhere in the house is what you need, and you have a magic for getting it, linking two similar things so that what happens to one happens to the other.

## How to play

Type what you want to do, in plain words, and press Enter:

- **look**, **inventory** (or **i**), **examine** something (or **x**)
- **north**, **south**, **east**, **west**, **up**, **down** (or **n**, **s**, **e**, **w**, **u**, **d**)
- **take**, **drop**, **open**, **push** things; **help** or **about** in most games
- **save** and **restore**; **quit** to stop

Your saved games are kept in `~/.local/share/savoir-faire`.
