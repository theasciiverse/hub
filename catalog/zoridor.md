**WALL THEM IN. NEVER SHUT THEM OUT.**

Quoridor, the board game of pawns and fences, at your keyboard against
the machine. Your pawn starts on one side of a 9 by 9 board, the
machine's on the other, and the first to reach the far side wins.

Each turn you either step your pawn one square or put down one of your
ten fences, two squares long, to lengthen the machine's road. It does the
same to you. A fence may cross nothing and must always leave both pawns a
way through, so the board turns into a maze you are building together,
and the shortest path is never where it was a turn ago.

- **One move or one fence**: every turn is a choice between racing and
  blocking
- **Pawns jump**: face to face, you can hop over the other pawn
- **The machine counts paths**: it weighs every move by how much it
  shortens its road and lengthens yours
- **Two at one keyboard**: hand the other side to a friend

**FIND THE WAY THROUGH.**

## Controls

- **Arrows**: pick the square for your pawn, or move the fence
- **Tab**: switch between pawn and fence; **Space**: turn the fence
- **Enter**: make the move (a green mark is legal, red is not)
- **Q**: quit

## Options

- `-2 human`: two players at one keyboard
- `-r 50`: shake up the machine's choices, 0 to 100 (the higher, the
  more it errs); `-s 12345`: fix its seed
- `-f`: play game after game, counting wins
- `-m`: a smaller board for 80x24
