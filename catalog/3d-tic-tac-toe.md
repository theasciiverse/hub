**FOUR IN A ROW, IN THREE DIMENSIONS.**

Tic-tac-toe grown into a cube: four levels, each four by four. Take turns with the computer placing your X; the first to get four in a line wins. A line can run along a level, straight down through all four levels, across a level on the diagonal, or from corner to corner through the middle of the cube: 76 lines in all, and far more to watch than on the little board.

- **The four levels side by side,** with the winning line lit up across them.
- **Three strengths:** easy forgets to block now and then, hard sets traps of its own.
- **Turns to start alternate,** since moving first is worth a lot.
- **Your record** of wins, losses and draws at each strength.

## Controls

- **← → ↑ ↓**: move on a level; **PgUp** and **PgDn** (or **[** and **]**): change level; **1** to **4**: jump to a level
- **Enter**, **Space** or a click: place your X
- **L**: the computer's strength for the next game; **N**: a new game; **Tab**: the rules
- **M**: sound on or off; **Esc**: quit
