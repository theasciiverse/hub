**PIG LOST! BOSS SAY IT GRUNK FAULT.**

"Boss say Grunk go find pig, bring it back. Him say, if Grunk not bring
back pig, not bring back Grunk either." So Grunk, an orc with a torch
and a pair of pants, sets off after the pig into the dark woods, and
before long under the ground, where a gnome and a lot of old things are
waiting.

Every word of it is Grunk's own, and Grunk has thoughts. On the pig: "Pig
pink and chubby. Look tasty. Pig maybe smart, but Grunk catch pig. So
that mean Grunk more smart." On temptation: "Pig maybe tasty, but Grunk
have job. That job: not eat pig."

Admiral Jota's Lost Pig (And Place Under Ground) won the 2007
Interactive Fiction Competition and Best Game at the 2007 XYZZY Awards.

**Find pig. Bring pig back. Not eat pig.**

## How to play

Type what you want to do, in plain words, and press Enter:

- **look**, **inventory** (or **i**), **examine** something (or **x**)
- **north**, **south**, **east**, **west**, **up**, **down** (or **n**, **s**, **e**, **w**, **u**, **d**)
- **take**, **drop**, **open**, **push** things; **help** or **about** in most games
- **save** and **restore**; **quit** to stop

Your saved games are kept in `~/.local/share/lost-pig`.
