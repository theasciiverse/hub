**THIS CLASS TAKES THE BISCUIT.**

"You've heard about them from others. You've even had them yourself: classes that seem to last forever. But this one takes the biscuit."

Celtic Elements in Early Teutonic Settlements: "a field of study so narrow and boring, you'd wonder they would award doctorates in it." The professor drones on about the settling instincts of early German nomad tribes, your elevenses are finished, and the large and heavy double doors at the top of the hall are calling you. Surely there is something better to do with your time?

There is. Slip out, and you find a world of magic that has gone badly wrong. The title means "Renaissance Repressed": Michael J. Coyne wanted to capture a Wizards Guild suppressing a magical society on the verge of breaking through into the age of reason.

- **A long, good-humoured puzzle adventure**, written for the 2003 Interactive Fiction Competition and updated in 2006 and 2017.
- **British to the bone**: "carcase" is spelled correctly, the author assures you, and the ground floor is not the first floor.
- **Help built in**: type **HELP** if you are new to text adventures, or **ABOUT** for the author's notes.

## How to play

Type what you want to do, in plain words, and press Enter:

- **look**, **inventory** (or **i**), **examine** something (or **x**)
- **north**, **south**, **east**, **west**, **up**, **down** (or **n**, **s**, **e**, **w**, **u**, **d**)
- **take**, **drop**, **open**, **push** things; **help** or **about** in most games
- **save** and **restore**; **quit** to stop

Your saved games are kept in `~/.local/share/risorgimento-represso`.
