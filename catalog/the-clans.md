**FOUR WARRIORS. ONE VILLAGE. AN EMPIRE TO TAKE.**

You start with nothing but a name. Choose four clansmen from nine races
and eleven classes, a Dwarf sorcerer, an Elf healer, a Halfling assassin
who strikes before anyone else moves, and send them down into the mines
to fight goblins, werewolves, ogres and worse. Every swing earns
experience, and every battle won draws followers who want to march
under your banner.

Then the real game begins. Train your clan, buy arms in the market, run
for the town hall and set the taxes. Raise an army from your followers,
build an empire, form alliances, and break them when the moment is
right. Twenty quests wait beyond the village, from The Orcs and the
Caravan to Drakuul's Legacy, the Wyvern Lord and the Dark One.

The Clans was a BBS door: on a dial-up board in the late 1990s you got a
handful of fights a day, and the whole village played against each other
while you were offline. That rhythm is still here. Each new day brings
new battles, the news tells you who rose and who fell, and your village
waits for you tomorrow. Share it with friends on the same machine: each
of you logs in with your own name and runs your own clan.

- **Command a party of four**: in battle every clansman acts in turn, by
  agility; choose attacks, skills, spells and scrolls for each.
- **Nine races, eleven classes**: from Barbarian and Knight to
  Necromancer, Pirate and Samurai, each with its own skills.
- **More than a dungeon**: the market, the church, the training hall,
  the town hall, alliances, empires and village wars.
- **Twenty quests** with their own stories, from a lost king to the
  Dragon Lord.
- **One village, many clans**: hot-seat play by login name.

Allen Ussher wrote The Clans in 1997, and it spread across InterBBS
leagues of boards that traded clans and armies by night. He released the
source in 2002, and the Synchronet developers keep it going today.

**Raise your clan. Take the village. Destroy everything.**

## Controls

- **Letters** pick from every menu: E enters the mines, M the market,
  T the town hall, H the training hall, Q goes back
- **In battle**: a attacks, k uses a skill or spell, f fights to the end,
  r runs
- **Enter** continues past a pause
