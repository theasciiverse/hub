**AHOY AN' YE BE WELCOME TO THE ROGUE SEA!**

This is yer ship, the Bloody Betty. Be not she nice? Start with a small
one on a small sea, an' collect four coconuts from the islands before
movin' to a bigger sea and a bigger ship. Every island has a name, and the
one ye be sailin' for is always shown as yer target.

The sea is drawn in square tiles, and so are the ships: several tiles
long, they turn in steps of forty-five degrees, and their sides are where
the cannons are. Other ships sail these waters too. Some islands give
coconuts, some cannonballs, some repairs; ships sunk by yer broadsides
leave gold behind. Mind yer hull, an' mind the shallows.

A short game made in seven days, with a tutorial at the start (Escape
skips it for experienced pirates) and a nontrivial amount of piratey talk.

## Controls

- **Arrows**, **A W D** or **H K L**: turn the ship and sail
- **Space**, **Enter** or **.**: wait a turn
- The other keys are shown in the game as you need them
- **+** and **-**: zoom the sea in and out
- **Ctrl+Q** or **Ctrl+C**: quit
