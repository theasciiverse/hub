**THE FRACTAL ZOOMER. IN YOUR TERMINAL.**

Hold the mouse button and fly into the Mandelbrot set, smoothly and
without stopping, as fast as you dare. XaoS reuses the points it worked
out for the frames before, so the zoom never waits for a picture to be
drawn. Here it all comes out as ASCII art, each character standing for
four points of the picture.

Twenty-nine kinds of fractal, from the Mandelbrot set and its higher
powers to Newton, Barnsley, Phoenix, Magnet and the Sierpinski carpet,
with their Julia sets, plus a formula of your own. Watch a Julia set
change as you move the pointer, swap the colouring, run the colour
cycling, put on filters, or hand the controls to the autopilot and watch
it hunt for the interesting edges.
The built-in tutorials explain fractals as they play.

**Pick a spot on the edge. Hold the button.**

## Controls

- **Left mouse button**: hold to zoom in towards the pointer; drag to steer
- **Right mouse button**: hold to zoom out
- **Middle button** (or both): drag to move
- **Arrow keys**: up and down for zoom speed, left and right for detail
- **a**: autopilot
- **m**: from the Mandelbrot set to the Julia set under the pointer, and back
- **j**: fast Julia mode, a small Julia set for the point under the pointer
- **1** to **0**: fractal types
- **c** and **f**: outside and inside colouring; **p**: a random palette;
  **y**: colour cycling
- **e**: filters; **u**: undo
- **Enter**, or the pointer on the top row: the menu bar; **h**: help, with
  every key and menu explained
- **q**: quit

The Help menu's tutorials start with an introduction to fractals; try it
first.
