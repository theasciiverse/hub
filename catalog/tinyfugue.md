TinyFugue, "tf" to its players, has been a MUD client since the early
1990s. It connects to several worlds at once, and its macro language,
triggers and hooks let you shape every line the MUD sends and every
command you type. This edition has the patches Debian has kept it
building with.

## Getting started

- `tinyfugue <host> <port>`: connect straight away
- `/connect <host> <port>`: connect from inside tf
- `/help`: the built-in manual
- `/quit`: quit
