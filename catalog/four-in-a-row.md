**DROP. CLACK. FOUR IN A ROW!**

Seven columns, six rows, and a stack of red and yellow discs. Drop yours in at the top, watch it fall to the lowest free place, and try to line up four of your own: across, up, or on a slant. Your opponent is trying to do exactly the same, and every disc you drop is also one they can stand on.

It looks simple. It is not. Make three with both ends open and nothing can stop you. Miss the one they are building on a slant and it's over.

- **A computer with four levels.** Easy never misses a win or a block right in front of it, but that is about all. Normal looks five moves ahead, Hard eight. Master thinks as deep as it can and does not forgive.
- **Two players at one keyboard,** or with a gamepad each.
- **Four boards:** Classic 7 by 6, Wide 8 by 7, Grand 9 by 7, and Five, a 10 by 8 board where it takes five in a row.
- **Take it back.** Dropped in the wrong column? Press U.
- **Watch** the computer play itself.
- The mouse works: point at a column and click.

Who starts alternates from game to game, and your record against each level is kept. **ONE MORE GAME...**

## Controls

- **← →** or the mouse: choose a column
- **Space**, **Enter**, **↓** or a click: drop the disc
- **1** to **9**, **0**: drop straight into that column
- **U** or a right click: take your move back
- **Gamepad**: d-pad to choose, A to drop, X to take back, Start for the menu
- **M**: sound on or off, **Esc**: back to the menu, **Q**: quit from the menu

## Options

- `--level easy|normal|hard|master`: the computer's level
- `--board classic|wide|grand|five`: the board
- `--two`: straight into a two-player game
- `--watch`: the computer plays itself
- `--mute`: start with the sound off
