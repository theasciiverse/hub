**HOT, FOUL, AND DARK.**

High summer, an indoor theatre, and you crammed in neck by neck with
your sweaty fellow citizens. On stage Rito has just found out about
Imita's affair, one of your favourite plays, and you cannot follow a
line of it. Aessa was supposed to meet you here. She has made excuses
before, and you are trying not to think about what that might mean. Was
that a flash of her copper hair by the aisle?

Get up. Push past the knees ("Sit down, horse-head"). Leave the play
behind, and the way out leads further than any theatre door should:
into world after world, each with its own logic and its own price.

- **"An Interactive Catharsis"**, as the title screen calls it, and a
  story about distance and what it takes to close it
- **Rated "cruel" by its own author:** you can make mistakes that stop
  you winning, so save often and keep your old saves
- **Andrew Plotkin's 1996 game**, nominated in that year's XYZZY Awards

**The play is in its final act. Yours is just starting.**

## How to play

Type what you want to do, in plain words, and press Enter:

- **look**, **inventory** (or **i**), **examine** something (or **x**)
- **north**, **south**, **east**, **west**, **up**, **down** (or **n**, **s**, **e**, **w**, **u**, **d**)
- **take**, **drop**, **open**, **push** things; **help** or **about** in most games
- **save** and **restore**; **quit** to stop

Your saved games are kept in `~/.local/share/so-far`.
