**THE STARS ARE GOING OUT.**

Every night the Lamplighter climbs into the sky and lights the stars. But not any more. The Mason has walled the Lamplighter up at the very top of his tower, the Brickspire, and now, one by one, the stars are going dark.

Five towers stand between you and the top. Fifty floors. One ball. Smash your way up!

Clear a floor and the whole tower turns, swinging the next one round to face you. Up through the Old Keep, where a Gargoyle spits fire from its balcony and a Storm Cloud rumbles over the roof. Up through the Ivy Tower, where vines slide along their rows and a giant crow guards its twig nest. On through the Bell Tower, where clockwork bricks tick back into place and the Great Bell sends shock rings rolling across the roof. Then the Sea Tower, lashed by storms, with a serpent rising beside the balcony and the Weathercock whirling on top. And every tower you climb, there are fewer stars left in the sky...

Here's the twist: your paddle changes its path as you climb! On the floors it runs straight. On the balconies it swings along an arc. On the roofs it circles the whole arena, and every angle you thought you knew goes out of the window.

Catch the capsules as they fall: a Plank for a longer paddle, Mortar to catch the ball, Chisels to fire, an Hourglass to slow things down, a Flock of three balls, a Spare life, or a Stair straight up to the next floor.

- **Five towers, fifty floors, ten bosses,** each tower with its own look and its own trick brick.
- **Two looks:** chunky pixel art, or pipe graphics drawn in box lines. Press G to switch.
- **Keyboard, mouse or gamepad.**

At the top, the Mason is still laying bricks, as fast as you can break them. Break through, free the Lamplighter, and light up the sky again. **HURRY, BEFORE THE LAST STAR FADES!**

## Controls

- **Left** and **Right** (or **A** and **D**, **H** and **L**), or the **mouse**: move the paddle
- **Space**, **Enter** or **Up**: launch the ball, fire Chisels, let go of a caught ball
- **P**: pause, **R**: restart, **Q** or **Esc**: quit
- **G**: pixel art or pipe graphics, **M**: sound on or off
- **Gamepad**: stick or d-pad moves (the stick aims round the roof), bottom or right button or right trigger fires, Start pauses

## Options

- `--floor N`: start on floor N (1 to 50)
- `--glyphs`: start in pipe graphics
- `--mute`: no sound
