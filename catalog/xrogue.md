The last of the Bell Labs Rogue line. You share out your attribute
points, choose one of nine classes (fighter, ranger, paladin, magician,
cleric, thief, assassin, druid or monk) and begin on level 0 in Friendly
Fiend's Equipage, a shop full of arms, armour and magic, before taking
its stairs down. More than 200 monsters live below, many of them
intelligent, and the game sets you a quest for one unique item.

## Controls

- **hjkl yubn**: move, the last four diagonally; with **Shift** run that
  way
- **f** and a direction: move until something turns up; **t**: throw,
  **z**: zap a wand or staff
- **i**: inventory, **P**: pick up, **d**: drop
- **e** eat, **q** quaff, **r** read, **w** wield, **W** wear, **T** take
  off
- **C**: cast a spell, **p**: pray, **c**: chant, **a**: affect the
  undead, **D**: dip something in a pool
- In the shop: **$** price, **#** buy, **%** sell
- **s**: search for traps and secret doors, **.**: rest
- `>` and `<`: stairs down and up
- **?**: all the commands, **/**: what a symbol is
- **S**: save and quit, **Q**: quit (type yes)

## Saving

**S** saves the game to your data folder. To carry on, start it from a
shell as `xrogue -r`; a saved game is deleted once it is restored.

## History

Robert Pietkivitch wrote XRogue between 1989 and 1992 for the AT&T UNIX
PC, building on Advanced Rogue 7.7. He sent the source to Nicholas J.
Kisseberth (Yendor) in 1996 and worked with him to bring it back; he
died in 2002, before sending the enhancements he had made in 2000.
Kisseberth's Roguelike Restoration Project released 8.0.1 to 8.0.3, and
rogueforge carries it on. This edition keeps your files in your data
folder and fixes saved games that
would not restore and a score file that crashed on 64-bit machines. It
is part of the Roguelike Restoration Project in the Asciiverse catalog.
