**HIDE YOUR FLEET. FIND THEIRS. SINK THE LOT.**

A carrier, a battleship, a destroyer, a submarine and a patrol boat, on a
10x10 sea. Lay them out where nobody would think to look. Then take turns
firing blind into the other side's waters, one shot at a time: `*` is a
hit, `~` is a splash. Sink a ship and you hear about it ("You have destroyed
my destroyer!!"), and the first fleet on the bottom loses.

Take on the computer, which will crow "Hahaha! I won!" if it gets there
first, or a friend on the same keyboard. For two players there's even an
anti-cheater screen between turns: type the given letter three times before
you may look.

- **Single player or two on one keyboard**
- **Keys or mouse**: click to place and fire, right click to turn a ship
- **Help built in**: F1 for the controls, F2 for how to play

## Controls

- **Arrow keys**, **hjkl** or **wasd**: move
- **Enter**: place a ship, or fire
- **R**: turn the ship while setting up
- **Mouse**: left click to place or fire, right click to turn
- **F1** or **?**: help on the controls, **F2** or **!**: how to play
- **q** or **Esc**: quit

## History

Salvo is one of the games abakh wrote, as Battleship, for New BSD Games, a
collection of small curses games started in 2018 and named after the classic
BSD games. The collection is packaged by Debian, Ubuntu, openSUSE, Alpine,
Nix, Homebrew, FreeBSD and pkgsrc, and is in the AUR. Here it plays under the
pencil-and-paper game's old name: Salvo was what the paper game was sold as in
the 1930s, decades before plastic pegs and trays.
