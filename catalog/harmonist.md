**YOU ARE A MONKEY. YOUR FRIEND IS IN A CELL EIGHT FLOORS DOWN. DON'T GET SEEN.**

Shaedra was caught by the Dayoriah Clan while trying to win back a stolen magic artifact, and now she sits in a prison deep in their underground domain. You are Syu, a gawalt monkey. You don't really understand why people complicate their lives over artifacts, but one thing is clear: you are going in to get her. You are small, you see well in the dark, and you are no fighter, so this is a game about not being noticed.

Every guard, dog and mage has a cone of view, and you can see exactly where it falls. Lamps throw pools of light you will want to skirt; your footsteps make noise; doors close behind you and block sight. Duck into dense foliage, slip under tables, crouch in barrels, squeeze through holes in the walls. When someone does spot you, jump off a wall to spring clear, or step into their blind corner, because monsters never look behind them diagonally.

- **Magaras**: magical tools with a few precious charges each, from levitation, digging and teleportation to fog, shadows, disguise and swapping places
- **Magical stones** set into the floors: teleport stones, night stones, tree stones, seal stones and more, to shake off a pursuer or reveal the map
- **Underground creatures** with their own habits: blinking frogs, haze cats, satowalga plants, tree mushrooms, vampires, earth dragons
- **Lore** scattered through the tunnels: diaries, ballads and poems written for the game
- **Coffee-break length**: a whole run is short, every dungeon is new, and each game is saved as a replay you can watch back

## Controls

- **Arrow keys** or **h j k l**: move, and jump off walls or over creatures
- **Shift** with a direction, or **H J K L**: run
- **o**: explore automatically (only while nobody is in view)
- **.** or **Enter**: wait a turn
- **e**: interact: equip, take the stairs, rest
- **v** or **z**: evoke a magara; **i**: inventory
- **x**: examine with the keyboard; **>** jumps to the stairs, then **.** travels there
- **Mouse**: click to move or travel, right click for the menu
- **Space**: the menu; **?**: help; **S**: save and quit; **Q**: quit without saving

## Options

- `-t`: true colour, in the game's own selenized palette
- `-x`: the xterm 256 colour palette
- `-n`: no animations
- `-r _`: watch a replay of your last game

## History

anaseto (Yon) began Harmonist in 2019, growing it out of the code of Boohu, his earlier roguelike set in the same world. The diaries, ballads and poems in its tunnels were written by kaoseto, author of the Ciclo de Shaedra novels, where Syu and Shaedra come from. After seven years of play and polish it reached version 1.0 in 2026.
