**EVERY RACE. EVERY CLASS. EVERY MONSTER. ONE DUNGEON TOO FEW.**

FroxComposband is the Angband family's great everything-included variant. Pick from dozens of races, among them monster races you play as a dragon, a vampire or a beholder would, and from classes from warrior and mage to necromancer, mystic and possessor. Walk out of town across a wilderness, take on the dungeons and the guardians at their bottoms, and go as deep as you dare.

It plays like Angband: turn-based, a single life, a level made new every time you take the stairs, and more items, spells and monsters than you will ever see in one game. FroxComposband carries FrogComposband on with many fixes and quality-of-life improvements, and loads Frog saves.

- **Dozens of races and classes**, including playable monster races
- **A wilderness and many dungeons**, each with its own boss
- **Beginner mode** for a gentler start
- **Huge variety**: artifacts, egos, spells, pets and more

## Controls

- **Arrow keys** or the number pad: move and attack; **`**: travel
- **g**: pick up; **i**: inventory; **w**: wield; **q**: quaff; **r**: read
- **<** / **>**: stairs; **?**: help, with every command
- **Ctrl-X**: save and quit

Your saves and scores are kept in `~/.local/share/FroxComposband`.
