**PASS THEM ALL.**

The road runs to the horizon, bending left and right, with the mountains
sliding past behind it as you turn. Somewhere ahead is traffic, small
dots at first, then cars, then cars right next to you. Pass as many as
you can. Touch one, and it is over.

Speed up and the road comes at you faster; slow down and the cars ahead
pull away. The score counts the cars you have left behind.

## Controls

- **← / →** or **a / d**: steer
- **↑ / ↓** or **w / s**: faster and slower (shown in km/h)
- **q**: quit

## Options

From a terminal: `racing-game -c` without colours, `racing-game -a`
with colour blocks only, no characters.
