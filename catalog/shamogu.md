**THE MOUNTAIN IS SICK. YOU ARE ITS GUARDIAN. TAKE THE SPIRITS AND GO DOWN.**

All life on your mountain is being corrupted. Beasts are losing their minds and turning on everything around them, and it all began when a dungeon portal opened at the summit. As the mountain's shaman guardian you step through it, to find the source of the corruption and destroy it.

You don't fight alone. Before you go, you bind a primary spirit: the **Four-Headed Hydra**, which strikes every foe around you at once; the **Rampaging Boar**, which digs straight through walls; the **Jumping Frog**, which leaps clear of trouble; the **Wind Fox**, whose gale blows foes away; the **Temporal Cat**, which stops time. Deeper in you find totems that lend you more of them, from the thunder porcupine and the barking hound to the walking tree and the stomping elephant, each with a few charges of a power that can turn a fight. Then there are the comestibles growing in the dungeon: firebreath peppers, clarity leaves and stranger things, every one good for more than one purpose, if you eat it at the right moment.

Every move counts. Monsters charge from a tile away, ranged beasts are worse at a distance, rats hunt you by smell, and footsteps in dense foliage can bring trouble or keep it away. Killing everything earns nothing, so a wise guardian takes what the level offers and slips through the portal to the next.

- **Seven primary spirits**, two of them for experienced players, and many more to find on totems
- **A short run**: a whole game is a coffee break or two, and every dungeon is new
- **Mods** to change the game: Corrupted Dungeon, Totem Conditions, Healing Combat, No Recharges, Small Inventory, Advanced Spirits
- **Replays** of every game, and a Wizard Mode that brings you back to life while you learn

## Controls

- **Arrow keys** or **h j k l**: move or attack
- **H J K L**: run
- **o**: explore automatically (not while danger is near)
- **.** or **Enter**: wait a turn
- **i**: your spirits and comestibles: invoke a spirit or eat
- **e**: interact: take a totem, use a menhir or a portal
- **x**: examine with the keyboard; **>** finds the portals, **!** the totems, **%** the comestibles
- **Mouse**: click to move or travel, right click for the menu
- **Space**: the menu; **?**: help; **S**: save and quit; **Q**: quit without saving

## Options

- `-t`: true colour, in the game's own selenized palette
- `-x`: the xterm 256 colour palette
- `-r _`: watch a replay of your last game

## History

anaseto (Yon) released Shamogu in 2025, built on gruid, the same library as his stealth roguelike Harmonist. It keeps growing with its players: one of them, mlochbaum, wrote the Totem Conditions expansion that is now one of its mods.
