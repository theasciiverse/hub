**THE STARS ARE GOING OUT.**

Every night the Lamplighter climbs into the sky and lights the stars. But not any more. The Mason has walled the Lamplighter up at the very top of his tower, the Brickspire, and now, one by one, the stars are going dark.

Five towers stand between you and the top. Fifty floors. One ball. Smash your way up!

Clear a floor and the whole tower turns, swinging the next one round to face you. Up through the Old Keep, where a Gargoyle spits fire from its balcony and a Storm Cloud rumbles over the roof. Up through the Ivy Tower, where vines slide along their rows, a giant spider spins webs to gum up your paddle and a giant crow guards its twig nest on the roof. On through the Bell Tower, where clockwork bricks tick back into place, the Jack, the clock's iron striker, climbs down off its bell to ring chimes at your paddle, and the Great Bell sends shock rings rolling across the roof. Then the Sea Tower, lashed by storms, with a serpent rising beside the balcony and the Weathercock whirling on top. And every tower you climb, there are fewer stars left in the sky...

Here's the twist: your paddle changes its path as you climb! On the floors it runs straight. On the balconies it swings along an arc. On the roofs it circles the whole arena, and every angle you thought you knew goes out of the window.

Catch the capsules as they fall: a Plank for a longer paddle, Mortar to catch the ball, Chisels to fire, an Hourglass to slow things down, a Flock of three balls, a Spare life, or a Stair straight up to the next floor.

- **Five towers, fifty floors, ten bosses,** each tower with its own look and its own trick brick: steel and gilt, sliding vines, clockwork, and ice the ball skids off.
- **Climb again!** Beat the Mason and watch the sky fill with stars, then go round a second time.
- **Two looks:** chunky pixel art, or pipe graphics drawn in box lines. Press G to switch.
- **Keyboard, mouse or gamepad.**

At the top, the Mason is still laying bricks, as fast as you can break them. Break through, free the Lamplighter, and light up the sky again. **HURRY, BEFORE THE LAST STAR FADES!**

## Controls

- **Left** and **Right** (or **A** and **D**, **H** and **L**), or the **mouse**: move the paddle
- **Space**, **Enter** or **Up**: launch the ball, fire Chisels, let go of a caught ball
- **P**: pause, **R**: restart, **Q** or **Esc**: quit
- **G**: pixel art or pipe graphics, **M**: sound on or off, **B**: music on or off
- **Gamepad**: stick or d-pad moves (the stick aims round the roof), bottom or right button or right trigger fires, Start pauses

## Options

- `--floor N`: start on floor N (1 to 50)
- `--glyphs`: start in pipe graphics
- `--mute`: no sound
- `--no-music`: no music

## History

Brickspire began on 9 October 2026 with one question: why should the paddle always run along a straight line? The answer came from the turning towers of the late-80s climbing games. Swing the camera round to a balcony and the paddle bends into an arc; look straight down onto a roof and it wraps into a full circle, just as the bosses get tough. Before the first line of code we went looking for a brick breaker that had done it already, from the Acorn Archimedes to the iPod, and found paddles on rings and towers with flat paddles, but never a climb from one to the other. Later that month the Mason walled up the Lamplighter, and the climb found its reason.
