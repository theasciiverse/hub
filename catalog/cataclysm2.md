**SHIPWRECKED, FIFTY YEARS AFTER THE END OF THE WORLD. FOUR FACTIONS WANT THE SAME ROOM.**

You were born after the cataclysm, in a coastal farming and fishing town
that kept its walls up and its people mostly safe. Then the walls broke.
You ran for the docks and put out to sea. Days adrift, then a storm. Now
you wake on the beach of an island, beside the wreck of your boat, with
your supplies scattered around you and night coming.

Find water, food and somewhere to sleep. Inland lie fields, forests,
swamps and salt marsh, and towns: the towns hold what you need, and the
zombies. Out in the wild there are mutant rabbits, demented dogs and giant
crabs, and at the island's heart a sealed Lab where the aliens came
through.

You are not the only one left. Walk up to people and talk. Farming camps
behind their stockades trade food and tools and gossip, and send you off
on errands across the island. Raiders hold camps of their own, full of
what they took, and shoot first. And four factions are racing each other
for the Lab:

- **The Cultists** worship the fungus and the aliens, paint a spiral on
  your forehead when you join, and walk unharmed among the things that
  hunt everyone else.
- **The Military** want the island under orders, and its seams aimed like
  guns. They arm their own and shoot their rivals on sight.
- **The Naturalists** vote on everything, share their beans and their
  rope, and mean to starve the seams until nothing comes through again.
- **The Scientists** watch from their station behind bulletproof glass,
  with eyebots and a GPS unit for anyone they come to trust.

Join one, do their errands, rise through their ranks, and you are their
enemies' enemy too. Every dawn the factions creep closer to the Lab, and
the radio and anyone you meet will tell you who is winning. When one takes
it, the clock starts.

The Lab will not open for just anyone. It wants the Power Plant running,
its substation passing the power on, the remote release thrown at the
Control Station where the aliens nest, and a passcard: one in an old
bunker, one aboard a wreck on a far shore, two with Lab staff who fled
into the towns. Ask around. People know where.

There is more than one way off this island:

- **Sail home.** Patch the wreck, rig a mast and sail, find a GPS unit to
  follow the mainland relay, stock the boat and go.
- **Close the seam.** Open the Lab yourself and shut down the core that
  everything on the island came through.
- **Win it for your faction.** Hand them the Lab and hold it three days,
  and the island is theirs, and yours.
- **Or lose it.** Let a rival hold the Lab for seven days, unless you
  reach the Power Plant first and cut the power out from under them.

- **Whales' own sequel** to Cataclysm, built around a game you can win,
  carried on from his notes and his development blog
- **Story or sandbox:** the race and every ending, or the island to
  yourself, with or without factions, with fewer or more monsters
- **Errands that take you places:** scout, clear a raider camp, fetch a
  supply case, carry a sealed letter, bring a corner substation back to
  life, its target flagged on your map
- **Hunger, thirst and sleep** that press on you, slow healing, and death
  that is permanent
- **Make a survivor** from stats, traits and a profession (Survivalist,
  Medic, Farmer, Mechanic, Hunter...)

## Controls

- **hjkl yubn** or the numpad: move, the last four diagonally; walk into a
  person to talk, a monster to attack it, a door to open it
- **,**: pick up, **d**: drop, **i**: inventory
- **w**: wield, **W**: wear, **T**: take off, **e**: eat, **R**: read,
  **a**: apply (the radio, the GPS unit, a flashlight)
- **t**: throw, **f**: fire, **r**: reload
- **o** and **C**: open and close, **s**: smash, **x**: examine (the wreck,
  a trade counter, panels, switches, doors), **;**: look
- `<` and `>`: stairs up and down
- **$**: sleep
- **:** or **M**: the island map (**q** leaves it)
- **@**: your character, **K**: skills
- **?**: help, **S**: save, **Q**: save and quit

## History

Whales wrote Cataclysm, a post-apocalyptic roguelike, from 2010 to 2013,
and its players carried it on as Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead, the big
community fork still going today. But Whales had run out of direction:
Cataclysm had no ending. So in 2013 Whales started over. Cataclysm II was
new code, set fifty years after the first game, and planned from the first
day as a game you could win. On a development blog of nearly three
hundred posts, Whales laid it all out: the wreck on the beach, the clocks
that press on you, the camps and the bandits, four factions fighting over
a Lab at the island's centre, and a "win of last resort" by boat.

In 2016 Whales stopped, with the island built and the ending still on
paper. Cataclysm II waited ten years. In 2026 the Asciiverse picked it up
and built the rest from Whales' own plan: first the saving and the dying,
the sleep and the boat, then the Lab, then the camps, and then the people
and the four factions and their race, with every choice Whales left open
written down beside the code.
