**SUNDAY, 9:05 PM. THE PAGER WILL NOT STOP.**

"Net extremely hoseled. Engine team being hideously masticated by this
outage. Demo rapidly approaching. Get to the cages. Reboot the servers.
Hasten. Do not rest. Please. All five of them."

That's Wilbur, your boss. You're the new system administrator for nWare.
You moved to nTopia a few days ago, and your apartment holds little more
than your nLap 500, a heap of clothes and the glowing stars on the ceiling.
It's the weekend. It doesn't matter. Out you go into the city, with the
clock in the corner running and the pager buzzing like a dental drill.

Nick Montfort's Book and Volume is a night-time wander through a gleaming
tech-boom city where everything has an n in front of it and everyone wants
something from you, right now.

- **A whole city to roam** by night, with the clock always running
- **Sharp, funny writing** about work, technology and a place that never
  quite lets you rest
- **An [auto mata] release**, with an introduction to interactive fiction
  built into its opening menu

## How to play

Type what you want to do, in plain words, and press Enter:

- **look**, **inventory** (or **i**), **examine** something (or **x**)
- **north**, **south**, **east**, **west**, **up**, **down** (or **n**, **s**, **e**, **w**, **u**, **d**)
- **take**, **drop**, **open**, **push** things; **help** or **about** in most games
- **save** and **restore**; **quit** to stop

Your saved games are kept in `~/.local/share/book-and-volume`.
