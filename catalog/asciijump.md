**THE IN-RUN. THE LIP. THE TELEMARK.**

A ski jumping game drawn in ASCII art. Pick a hill, push off and ride the
in-run. At the lip of the take-off you jump, then hang in the air
over the slope, leaning forward or back, while a rain of characters falls
behind you. Press **Enter** again to land in a telemark: too early or too
late and you crash. Five judges score the landing, and the longest jump on
each hill stands as its record.

- **Eight hills**, from England's K50 to the mighty Tarnica K180
- **World Cup**: you, your friends on the same keyboard and computer
  jumpers at three levels, over the hills you choose
- **Training**, to find your timing on any hill
- **Network games**: one player runs `aj-server`, invites the others, and
  everyone joins from the menu

## Controls

- **Enter**: start the run, jump at the lip, land with a telemark
- **Left** and **Right**: lean in the air
- **q**: leave the hill
- In menus: **Up**, **Down**, **Enter**, **Space** to select and **Tab**
  for the next field

Hill records are kept in `~/.asciijump/`.
