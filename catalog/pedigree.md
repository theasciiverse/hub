**FOUR GENERATIONS OF YOUR FAMILY FLEW THESE MINES. NOW THE MINES SHOOT BACK.**

Three days ago the mining robots of the Cinder Reach stopped answering the guild. This morning they started shooting. Your grandmother flew the first survey of the Reach, so nobody was surprised when the guild called you first.

Take your mining ship down through the tunnels, and remember that down is only a suggestion: you fly in six degrees of freedom, turning, rolling and sliding through shafts that go every way at once. Drones hunt you through dark rock, wasps come in swarms, brutes soak up everything short of a missile, and blasting charges fly straight at you and go off. Under it all waits the Foreman, the rig that ran the deep works and has stopped taking orders from anyone.

Five levels from the upper workings to the deep works, through a gold seam, moss caves and a smelter where the floor is molten rock. Find the reactor that powers each one, destroy it, pick up the miners still trapped below, and get out before the whole place goes up.

It is drawn in your terminal, two pixels to a character in full colour (or sextants, or Braille), with your shields, energy and weapons on a status line of real text, and menus and briefings you can read at a glance.

**Fly in first. It runs in the family.**

## Controls

- **W** and **S**: thrust forward and back; **A** and **D**: slide left and right; **C** and **X**: slide up and down
- **Arrow keys**: turn; **Q** and **E**: roll
- **Space**: fire the gun; **F** or **Enter**: fire a missile; **G**: drop a flare
- **1** to **5**: guns; **6** to **0**: missiles
- **Tab**: map; **Esc**: menu

A gamepad works too: the left stick thrusts and slides, the right stick turns, the triggers fire, and the bumpers roll. Everything can be rebound under Options, Controls.

Sit in an amber energy station to fill your energy and in a cyan repair bay to mend your shields.

## Options

The picture (half blocks, sextants, Braille or chafa) is chosen under Options, Graphics.
