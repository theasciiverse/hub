You are a robot, the **@**, in a world full of hostile robots, and the
only way out is down through twenty levels: open grass and woods, rough
ground, enemy buildings, caves, chasms, flooded ruins and a metal fortress
at the bottom. Every robot, yours included, is built from up to eight
systems: power cells, weapons, propulsion, sensors and armour. Shots damage
systems rather than hit points; a system at 50% or less stops working until
your power cell repairs it, and one at 0% is gone for good. If a power
cell is destroyed, it explodes and takes the robot with it.

Destroy an enemy's power source and it becomes a wreck (**%**). Drive into
the wreck to salvage what is left of it: a buggy's minigun, a tank's
caterpillar tracks for crossing rough ground, a sentry's X-ray scope for
seeing through walls. Your robot grows into whatever you take from the
robots you beat.

## Controls

- **hjkl yubn**, the cursor keys or the numeric keypad: move, the last
  four diagonally; drive into a wreck to salvage it
- **1** to **8**: use a system; for a weapon, the nearest target is picked,
  **+** and **-** cycle through targets, **x** cancels and any other key
  fires; a power cell chooses which system it repairs, a sensor looks
  around
- **d**: details of your systems, or of the robot being looked at
- **r**: reorder your systems, **s**: switch a system on or off
- **.**: wait, `>`: take the exit to the next level
- **@**: which level you are on, **?**: the controls
- **S**: save and quit, **R**: restore the saved game, **Q**: quit

## Saving

**S** saves the game to your data folder and quits. Start Scrap again
and press **R** to carry on; a saved game can be restored once, then it
is deleted, as in the original.

## History

Thijs van Ommen wrote Scrap in ten days, a Ten-Day Roguelike released
in March 2005. In October and November 2006 he released the Extended 10DRL
and its 1.1, which added saving and the other features the ten days had
left out while keeping the game itself as it was, and in 2017 he put it
on GitHub under the GPL. This edition keeps the savefile in your data
folder.
