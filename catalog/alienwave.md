**THEY COME IN WAVES. YOU HAVE ONE SHIP.**

The zorxians fly down in formation, peel off and dive at you. Slide left and right along the bottom of the screen, fire your lasers into the swarm, and when it gets too hot, open a shield to hide behind. Shields don't move with you, though: they stay where you put them, and they only last as long as their charge.

Survive long enough, past 5000 points, and Xzarna, the zorxian queen, comes for you herself. You have one life, and the score is printed on the way out.

## Controls

- **Left** and **Right**: move your ship; **Down**: stop
- **Space** or **Enter**: fire
- **S**, **Up** or **Backspace**: open a shield (when it has charge)
- **P**: pause; **Q** or **Esc**: quit

The charge indicator for the shields is in the top left corner.
