**KEEP YOUR TORCH HIGH. THE DARK DOWN THERE ISN'T EMPTY. IT'S HUNGRY.**

A king once went down into the earth alone, cut a single coal out of the place where the rock itself burns, and carried it up in his bare hands. The town he built around it is Emberhold, the last lit town before the dark. Every lantern in it is lit from Hadrik's forge, the forge from the Ember, and the dark stops at the gate. So everything below climbs toward it instead, and Captain Voss needs a blade that doesn't shake. You are the stranger they hire to go down and meet it.

Termablo is a Diablo-like for the terminal, and its light is real. Every torch, street lamp, campfire, brazier, glowing crystal, lava pool and spell throws coloured, flickering light across the map, and you see only what the light reaches. A camp of the Fallen is an orange glow across the Ashen Fields long before you see the Fallen around it; a monster standing in the dark is not there at all until light touches it. Throw a Firebolt down a crypt corridor and it lights the walls as it flies.

- **Emberhold**, to come home to: Hadrik's forge for steel and a gamble on unidentified gear, Mirela's Remedies for potions and patching up, a fountain, and townsfolk who remember you
- **Talk to anyone** by walking into them: highlighted words become topics you can ask the whole town about, and what people say changes as the bosses fall. Come back bleeding, broke, or wearing the Bone King's crown, and they notice
- **One story, six quests**: the Bone King on his throne of ribs, the Buried Captain still standing watch in his barrow, the Drowned Oracle in her pool under Blackmarsh, Hadrik's stolen coal and the Cinder Prior under the town's own graveyard, and at the bottom of the Burning Abyss the Last Wanderer, who puts out every light near him, your torch included
- **Loot that glows** in the dark: magic, rare and unique, from Kingsbane, Voss's father's axe, to the Last Lantern, which burns when all else is dark
- **Fighter or caster**: spend your points on Strength, Dexterity, Vitality or Energy, and swing a war hammer or burn and freeze everything with Firebolt and Frost Nova
- **Shareable worlds**: start with `-seed 42` and the world is the same every time. Share the seed, race a friend
- **Seven languages**: English, Deutsch, Français, Italiano, Русский, العربية and 简体中文, picked on the title screen

## Controls

- **Arrow keys**, **h j k l y u b n** or the numpad: move, and attack by walking into a monster
- **f**: Firebolt at your target; **r**: Frost Nova; **Tab** and **Shift+Tab**: next and previous target, or click one
- **q** and **w**: healing and mana potion; **t**: Scroll of Town Portal
- **g**: pick up; **o**: explore automatically; **.** or **5**: wait
- **i**: inventory; **c**: character sheet, **1** to **4** spend points; **m**: map; **J**: quest journal
- **Mouse**: hover over anything on the map to see what it is
- **?**: help; **Q**: quit

## Options

- `-seed 42`: the same world every time
- `-lang de`: the language (en, de, fr, it, ru, ar, zh); otherwise it follows your `LANG`
- `-bot fighter` or `-bot caster`: watch a scripted hero play; **Space** pauses, **+** and **-** change the speed

## History

Anton Medvedev, who made the terminal JSON viewer fx and Google's zx, released Termablo on 29 September 2026: a Diablo-like in which light is cast ray by ray, in colour, from every flame. Within a week it could be played over SSH from his own server, and two scripted heroes, a fighter and a caster, were playing it to the end over and over, so that the numbers could be balanced from their runs instead of by hand. Then came the Last Wanderer and the story of the Stolen Ember, seven languages, conversations in which every highlighted word is something to ask about, and on 10 October the Cinder Sanctum under the graveyard, with the Gravewardens' Barrow out in the south fields.
