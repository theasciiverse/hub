**THE BOARD HAS ONE EDGE. YOUR KING IS ON IT.**

Take a chessboard, give it a half twist, and join its ends. Now the h-file runs straight on into the a-file, upside down: a1 sits right beside h8, and nothing is ever quite where it should be.

Möbius Chess is ordinary chess on that board, drawn as a real twisted strip that turns in 3D beside a flat board showing exactly where everything stands. Every rule you know still holds. It is the board that has changed:

- **Rooks run round the loop**: along a rank, through the join and back along the enemy's rank, 21 squares from anywhere
- **Bishops turn at the join** and come out on the other colour
- **Pawns that take across the join** turn round and march home, to promote on their own first rank
- **No corners** to hide a king in, and **no castling**
- **A starting position found by search**: the usual one hands each side a rook on move one, so every arrangement was tried for one where neither side can take, check or threaten anything on the first move and every pawn is guarded. The rooks wait in the pawn line, and the corners, right on the twist, start empty

Play the computer at three strengths, play a friend at one keyboard, or sit back and watch the computer play itself.

## Controls

- **Mouse**: click a piece, then where it goes, on the strip or on the flat board; drag the strip to turn it
- **Arrows** and **Enter**: the same with the cursor
- **u**: take back a move; **Esc**: menu (play White or Black, two players, watch, strength)
- **s**: spin on or off; **+ -**: zoom; **m**: sound; **?**: help; **q**: quit

## Options

- `--black`, `--two`, `--watch`: start as Black, two players, or watching
- `--easy`, `--hard`: the computer's strength
- `--mute`: start with the sound off
