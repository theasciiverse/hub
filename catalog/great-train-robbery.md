**WELL NOW, LISTEN UP, BOYS.** Word is there's a train fixin' to roll
through our humble little town, and this one's belly is stuffed to the
brim with glitterin' gold.

You do not ride with the gang. You plan the robbery, and then you watch
it happen. In the saloon you hire your men, in the general store you buy
them horses, revolvers, rifles and dynamite, and in the hotel you give
each of them a place to start from (a roof is safe from fists, but there
is no getting down) and a list of orders: attack the locomotive, shoot the
wagons loose, deal with the guards, collect the gold, escape. They will do
the first thing on their list that can be done, and nothing else. They
have no ideas of their own.

At midnight the train comes. Then it all unravels on the town map, man by
man and shot by shot, and you see how good your plan was. Pause it to take
a look, or retry the same town with the same crew once you know better.

## Controls

- The keys are on screen: **m** the town map, **o** the saloon, **g** the
  general store, **t** the hotel, **k** kick it off, **?** help
- **1** to **0**: pick from a list; the marked letter of an action does it
- **Enter**: confirm; **Escape**: cancel
- **Space**: pause the heist; **A** aborts it while paused
- **Arrows**: scroll the town map when it does not fit the window
- **Ctrl+Q** or **Ctrl+C**: quit
