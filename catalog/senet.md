**PLAY YOUR WAY THROUGH THE NETHERWORLD.**

For three thousand years, Egypt played Senet. Pharaohs played it, scribes played it, and in time the dead played it too: the board became the soul's journey through the netherworld, and sets were buried with their owners, Tutankhamun among them.

Throw the four sticks, count the light sides, and race your pieces along the S-shaped track and off the board. Guard your pieces in pairs, block with three in a row, swap places with a lonely enemy. The House of Beauty must be landed on. The House of Water must not. And the last houses want just the right throw, and the gods are not in a hurry.

Nobody kept the rules, only thirty squares, a handful of sticks and a lot of tomb paintings of people having a great time. So choose your reconstruction:

- **Kendall, five pieces**: the rules on most sets sold today
- **Kendall, seven pieces**: the 1978 original, where falling into the House of Water stalls your whole side until you fish the piece out
- **R. C. Bell 1960**: ten men each, a long die, and capture by landing

Play the computer at three strengths, or a friend at the same keyboard.

## Controls

- **Space** or **Enter**: throw
- **Arrows** or **Tab**: choose a move; **Enter**: make it
- **R**: rescue a piece from the water (Kendall, seven pieces)
- **Mouse**: click to throw, then click a lit piece
- **F1** or **?**: the rules and what is known; **M**: menu; **Q**: quit
- **O**: the braille ornament on or off

## Options

- `--demo`: the computer plays both sides
- `--seed N`: the same throws every time
- `--plain`: start without the ornament, for fonts that draw braille thin
