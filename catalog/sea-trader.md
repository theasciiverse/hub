**THE YEAR IS 1850. YOU HAVE A SMALL JUNK, $1500 AND 200 DAYS.**

Hong Kong harbour, the first morning of the voyage. Shanghai grows its own tea and sells it cheap. Nagasaki, where the kilns are, sells porcelain below anyone else, and Manila its spices. Between them lie storms and typhoons, reefs, uncharted islands with buried chests, and pirates who want your hold. Buy where it is cheap, sell where it is dear, and when day 200 comes, retire a Captain, or an Admiral.

Sea Trader is a trading voyage played one passage at a time. Every port has its own market, every fourteen days the prices move, and every thirty the season turns: tea climbs in winter, porcelain in summer, spices fall when the cold comes. Your crew brings you merchant tips in each market, and the better your name on the waterfront, the better the tips.

- **Five ports**: Hong Kong and Singapore, the busy trading hubs; Shanghai, the big market; Nagasaki; and little Manila, where prices run high and wild
- **The passage**: every voyage between ports takes days, and the sea decides what happens on the way. Push through a winter storm and win your crew's respect, or shelter and pay for it; follow a veteran sailor's secret route; host a banquet for a foreign trade delegation and watch tea and porcelain soar
- **Pirates**: attack, flee or bribe them. Win, and there may be treasure in their hold; lose, and they take part of yours
- **The shipyard**: your junk sails at 8 knots with room for 120 picul. Buy speed up to 20 knots, a stouter hull, a hold for 650 picul, and guard ships that take the blows for you, for a daily upkeep
- **Greed has a price**: overload the hold by a fifth and you sail slower and escape pirates less often. Run short and the moneylenders charge 3% a day; fall too deep and they take everything
- **Classic or extended**: the classic voyage ends on day 200; the extended one sails on, a little of your score lost each day past it

## Controls

- **Number keys** pick an option, or **arrow keys** and **Enter**: press **C** on the start screen to switch
- **Space**: continue after a report from the crew
- **Esc**: back; on the main menu it asks whether to quit
- **PgUp** and **PgDn**, or the mouse wheel: scroll a long screen
- On the start screen: **H** how to play, **M** classic or extended voyage, **A** text animations on or off


## History

Yishai Zehavi started Sea Trader on 11 November 2024 as a game anyone could play with one command, `npx ctrader`, and released it as he built it: a first version a week later, 1.0 by the end of November, pirates at Christmas, then in January moneylenders, interactive events, seasons and merchant tips. By February 2025 it had passed thirty releases. And in October 2026 he came back for another voyage, with fairer money and the cargo counted in picul, the old porter's load, as the real traders of the South China Sea counted it.
