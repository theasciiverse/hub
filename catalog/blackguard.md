**THE DECK IS THE DUNGEON.**

Forty-four cards, twenty hit points, one way out: through every card.
Each room deals four. The black cards are monsters, and they hurt as much
as they are worth. Diamonds are weapons that soak up the damage, but a
blade grows dull: once it has killed a monster, it can only kill weaker
ones after. Hearts are potions, and only the first one in a room does you
any good.

Play three cards and the fourth waits for you in the next room. Run from a
room you cannot face and it goes to the bottom of the deck for later, just
never twice in a row. Do you spend your sharp sword on the jack now, or
save it for the king still to come? Fight the queen bare-handed and hope
the next room has hearts?

Clear the whole deck to win, scored by the health you have left. Every
dungeon comes from a seed, so you can replay one, share one, or take on
the daily dungeon that everyone plays on the same day.

**Choose your fights.**

## Controls

- **Left** and **Right** (or **h** and **l**): choose a card
- **Enter** or **Space**: use it: fight, equip or drink, whichever fits
- **f**: fight the chosen monster with your blade
- **b**: fight it bare-handed
- **a**: avoid the room
- **n**: a new dungeon; **r**: the same dungeon again
- **t**: switch the weapon rule and replay the same deck
- **?**: the rules; **q**, **Esc** or **Ctrl+C**: quit

## The weapon rule

Scoundrel's own rules leave open whether a dulled blade may kill a monster
of exactly the value it last killed. Blackguard starts with the strict
reading, only weaker monsters, and **t** switches to the equal reading
mid-game, on the same deck, so you can see what it changes.

## Options

From a terminal: `blackguard --seed 42` for a dungeon you can share,
`--daily` for today's dungeon, and `--rule equal` to start with the equal
reading.
