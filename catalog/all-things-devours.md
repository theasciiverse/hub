It is 4:17 in the morning. The plan is simple: get into your lab, plant the bomb and run, so the prototype is destroyed and nobody dies. The guard is out securing the grounds and you have six minutes. Then time itself starts to fold back on you, and every move you make has to fit with the ones you have already made.

## How to play

Type what you want to do, in plain words, and press Enter:

- **look**, **inventory** (or **i**), **examine** something (or **x**)
- **north**, **south**, **east**, **west**, **up**, **down** (or **n**, **s**, **e**, **w**, **u**, **d**)
- **take**, **drop**, **open**, **push** things; **help** or **about** in most games
- **save** and **restore**; **quit** to stop

Your saved games are kept in `~/.local/share/all-things-devours`.
