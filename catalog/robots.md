**THEY ARE EVIL. THEY ARE NOT VERY BRIGHT.**

The field is full of robots, and every one of them wants you dead
("which is why they are evil", as the original manual puts it). Each
time you move, every robot takes one step straight toward you. You have
no gun and no blade, nothing to fight with at all. What you do have is
their stupidity: when two robots run into each other they crash into a
junk heap, and any robot that blunders into a heap is scrap too.

So you dance. Step aside, hold still for a turn, and watch them pile
into each other. Clear the field and the next one starts with sixteen
more of them, up to 64 at once. Cornered? Hit the teleporter. It drops
you on a random empty square, which might be safety and might be right
next to a robot.

- **One point for every robot wrecked**, and a bonus for each field you
  clear
- **One way out of a tight spot:** the teleporter, as often as you dare
- **A table of the top 16** to fight your way onto

When they finally get you, the game is honest about it:
"Congratulations! You have been eaten by the evil robots."

**How long can you keep them crashing?**

## Controls

- **h**, **j**, **k**, **l**: move left, down, up, right
- **y**, **u**, **b**, **n**: move diagonally up left, up right, down
  left, down right
- **.** or **Space**: stay where you are for a turn
- **t**: teleport
- **q** or **F10**: quit

The keys are also shown beside the field while you play. Robots are
`+`, junk heaps `*` and you are `@`.

## History

Robots is one of the oldest BSD games, first written at the University
of California in 1980. Mike Sharov rewrote it from scratch in 2016 for
his bsd-games, the modern Linux port of the BSD games, keeping the play
and the look of the field from Christos Zoulas's 1999 NetBSD version.
Robots is part of BSD Games in the Asciiverse catalog, under the BSD
licence.
