**DOWN THERE IS THE AMULET OF YENDOR. YOUR GOD WANTS IT.**

Go down through the Mazes of Menace, past the Gnomish Mines, Sokoban's boulder puzzles and the Oracle, all the way into Gehennom, and bring the Amulet back for your god. Choose one of thirteen roles, from Archeologist and Valkyrie to Wizard and Tourist, and take your kitten, little dog or pony along.

Everything is a character on the map, and almost anything you think of trying works: dip, engrave, pray, polymorph, kick, bargain with shopkeepers. Write Elbereth in the dust and many monsters will not dare step on it. Death is final, and when it comes the game asks, politely: "Do you want your possessions identified?"

- **Almost forty years in the making**, and still going: 5.0.0 came out in May 2026.
- **New to it?** Say yes to the tutorial at the start.

**THE DUNGEON IS WAITING.**

## Controls

- **hjkl yubn**: move, the last four diagonally; with **Shift**, run
- **F** and a direction: fight; **t**: throw, **z**: zap a wand
- **i**: inventory, **,**: pick up, **d**: drop
- **e** eat, **q** quaff, **r** read, **w** wield, **W** wear, **T**
  take off, **P** put on, **R** remove, **a** apply
- **o** and **c**: open and close doors; **s**: search; **.**: rest
- `>` and `<`: stairs down and up
- **#**: extended commands, such as `#pray`
- **?**: help, **;**: what is that symbol, **Ctrl-P**: earlier messages
- **S**: save and quit

## Saving and options

**S** saves the game to your data folder (`~/.local/share/nethack`), and
starting NetHack again carries on from there. Your options go in
`~/.nethackrc`, or in `nethackrc` in that folder. At the end of each game
a full account of it is written to its `dumplog` folder.
`OPTIONS=windowtype:curses` switches to the curses interface.

## History

NetHack grew from Hack, which Jay Fenlason wrote with Kenny Woodland,
Mike Thome and Jon Payne after Rogue and Andries Brouwer reworked at the
Mathematisch Centrum in Amsterdam (Hack 1.0.3, 1985, is also in the
catalog). Mike Stephenson merged its descendants into NetHack 1.4 in 1987,
and the NetHack DevTeam took it on from there. NetHack 3.6 came out in
2015; the next release, long known as 3.7, became 5.0.0 on 2 May 2026,
with over 3100 fixes and changes and its levels written in Lua.

This edition is built for Linux and macOS, keeping everything it writes
in your own data folder.
