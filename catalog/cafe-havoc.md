**GOOD MORNING!** This is it, your grand day, the first day in a new job.
Working in this big café is going to be exciting.

Jenkins, the chief steward, gives you a badge and a simple assignment: do
your best for three days (and three nights), and your performance will be
assessed afterwards. Guests sit at tables of wood and stone all over the
floors of Café Havoc and want their cappuccino, their cheesecake, their
ice-cold Club Mate. Find what they ordered in the storage rooms and the
kitchens, carry it to the right table and hand it over. Talk to the guests,
look at them, give them what they want.

And if you hear people talking about some shady night-murder-death-blood
stuff, please be assured that it is only gossip. When night falls, the
guests are not quite what they were by day, and a waiter may need to defend
himself with whatever he has in his hands. Survive the three days and see
what the assessment says.

## Controls

- **Arrows** or **vim keys** (hjkl yubn), **Home**, **PgUp**, **End** and
  **PgDn** for the diagonals: walk; walk into someone to interact
- **.**: wait a turn
- **d**, **c**, **g**, **a**: what walking into someone does: describe,
  chat, give, attack (each appears once you need it)
- **1** to **8**: ready an item from your tray
- **R**: a new game, once the assessment is in
- **Ctrl+Q** or **Ctrl+C**: quit
