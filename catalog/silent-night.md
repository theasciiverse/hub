**THE WIND PICKS UP. THE SNOW STARTS TO MOVE.**

Night in a snowy forest, deep in winter. Snow drifts down at three depths, near flakes fast and large, far ones faint. Then a gust sweeps in from one side! It bends the spruces, shakes the snow off their branches in clumps and lifts the snow off the open field in a low, glittering blizzard, laying it down again behind the cabin and the trees. Watch long enough and the drifts grow and creep downwind, and the snow on the roof builds and builds until, with a soft thump, it slides off.

A log cabin glows at its windows, smoke drifting from the chimney, and the spruce beside it is lit for Christmas. Above the mountains the stars twinkle, the moon shines and a faint aurora moves. And every now and then, if you are patient, a sleigh crosses the sky.

Listen, too. The sound is made as it plays, nothing recorded: the wind in the trees rises and falls with the gusts you see and whistles in the strongest, snow thumps off the branches and the roof, an owl calls far off, and sleigh bells ring while the sleigh is up.

- **Real weather:** the wind moves real snow, so every drift is built flake by flake
- **A different forest every time,** or the same one again with `-s N`
- **Christmas in every corner:** the lit tree, the warm windows, the sleigh and its bells

**Turn the lights down, pour something warm, and let it snow.**

## Controls

- **m**: sound off and on
- **Any other key**: quit

## Options

From a terminal: `silent-night --mute` starts quiet, `--no-sound` has no
sound at all, and `-s N` builds the same forest every time.

## History

On Christmas Eve 1818, in the little church of St Nicholas at Oberndorf in Austria, Joseph Mohr and Franz Xaver Gruber sang *Stille Nacht* for the first time, to a guitar. Two hundred years and more later, in October 2026, Silent Night the demo was made for Christmas, after a search for a terminal snowfall turned up plenty of falling flakes but none with wind, drifting snow or depth. So this one has all three, and a forest to keep them in.
