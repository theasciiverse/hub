**THEY SAY YOU'VE LOST YOUR MARBLES.**

They're right. Twelve of them, rolled off into places that should not exist. Time to go and get them back, one at a time.

No jump button. No fire button. Just a marble, gravity and your nerve. Ease it down the slopes, coax it along the ledges and keep it rolling, because one hard landing and it shatters.

The first worlds are almost reasonable: grey checkered terraces and blue cliffs, and a little slime that doesn't chase you, it just waits for you to stop. Then come the Gulpers, who mean no harm; they simply can't tell a marble from a snack. A tide that keeps its own time. Iron bully marbles, heavy, proud and not very bright, who come straight at you without looking where they're going.

And then the madness sets in. Ice where nothing holds you back. Floors with opinions, rising and falling beneath you. Springs that throw you somewhere higher. A world where falling takes its time. Physics that shouldn't work, does!

- **Twelve hand-built levels in three acts:** Losing It, The Madness, and Recovery.
- **Race your own ghost:** your best run on every level rolls right beside you.
- **Grab a gamepad** and the stick feels like an arcade trackball. Keyboard and mouse work too.

Twelve marbles, every one of them out there somewhere. **GO AND GET THEM BACK!**

## Controls

- **Arrow keys**, **W A S D** or **H J K L**: roll
- **Mouse**: hold a button and push toward the pointer, like a trackball
- **Gamepad**: the stick rolls
- **Enter** or **Space**: go on, **R**: restart the level, **Esc**: back, **Q**: quit
- **N** and **P**: next or previous level, **+** and **-**: zoom
