**YOU HAVE 1 CANDY.**

That is all you get at first: a number, ticking up once a second, and a
button to eat them. Eat them, or throw ten on the ground and see what
happens. Before long you are requesting new features from the developer
with your hard-earned sweets, and every one of them opens the world a
little wider: a status bar, a health bar, a map.

Then the map turns out to be a whole country. A village with a candy
merchant who would do anything for candies, a forge, a lonely house, a
lighthouse with questions, a desert, a bridge, a cave, a castle full of
things that want to hit you. You go questing with whatever you can carry,
cast spells from a grimoire, brew potions in a cauldron by the book, farm
lollipops, boot up a computer that runs GNU/Candies, and find out what is
really inside your box. All of it drawn in ASCII art, all of it in your
terminal.

Candy Box 2 is aniwey's sequel to his 2013 browser hit, and it is full of
jokes, secrets and surprises that are much more fun found than read
about. So this page will stop here.

## Controls

- **Mouse**: click buttons and links, hover over items for their
  descriptions, wheel to scroll (Shift+wheel sideways)
- **Tab** / **Shift+Tab**: move between buttons, lists and fields;
  **Enter** presses the highlighted one, so the game plays without a mouse
  too
- **Typing**: click a field (or Tab to it) and type; Enter sends
- **Page Up** / **Page Down**: scroll a long page; **Shift+arrows**
  sideways; on the world map the **arrows** move the view
- In quests and minigames the game has its own keys; in
  terminals with the kitty keyboard protocol (kitty, Ghostty, foot,
  WezTerm) held keys work as in the browser
- **Ctrl+C** or **Ctrl+Q**: quit. Save first, on the game's **SAVE** tab
  (it can also save every ten minutes); `candybox2 --slot 1` starts with
  slot 1

The game's own **CFG** tab changes the language (seventeen translations besides English)
and has "Invert colors?" for a black page instead of the white one.
