**GO INTO THE SEWERS AND ELIMINATE THE SOURCE OF SLIME!**

In the not-too-distant future, THE YEAR 1999, fallout from THE WAR has caused RADIOACTIVE MUTANT SLIMES to appear in the sewers of THE CITY. You are a GENETICALLY-MODIFIED PRECOG SUPER-SOLDIER, whose free will was in part traded for the power to PREDICT THE OUTCOME OF COMBAT ENCOUNTERS. Six floors down, the Source of Slime is waiting.

There are no dice in slime99, because you can see the future. Down the side of the screen it lies face up: the stack of every attack you will make (Hit 5, Hit 7, Cleave 3, Skewer), every defence you will put up (Armour 2, Dodge, Revenge), and every tech you have ready. Each time you fight, the top card comes off. You know the next hit will land for 7. You can also see, three defences down, the one that simply says Die.

So the game is not about luck. It is about what you do with what you know. Your abilities let you bend fate: stash the top card for later, swap the top two, or throw one away for good, so that Die never comes up. Your techs do the rest. Blink across the room, pull the slimes towards you or push them back, make your next hit a critical, or make the next slime's attack never happen at all.

The slimes have their own ideas. Hit a divide slime and there are two of them. Hit a swap slime and you trade places. Hit a teleport slime and you both vanish. Goo slimes turn the floor to sludge, curse slimes slip misses into your future, and upgrade slimes, struck, deal you better cards. Every fight is a puzzle you can see to the bottom, if you look.

And on the way down, the commander has a word for you: "Good work soldier." Then you choose a new ability, and go deeper.

## Controls

- **Arrows**, **WASD** or **HJKL**: move, and walk into slimes to fight
- **t**: use the top tech (aim it with the movement keys); **Escape**: cancel
- **1** to **8**: your abilities; **Space**: wait; **x**: examine
- **Escape**: the menu

## Options

- `--rgb`, `--ansi`, `--greyscale`: for terminals without true colour

## History

Stephen Sherratt made slime99 in seven days for the 7DRL Challenge of March 2020, on chargrid, his library for games drawn in characters, and released it properly that summer. It takes the randomness out of the moment and lays it out in front of you instead: the dice are all still there, already rolled, waiting in a stack you can read.
