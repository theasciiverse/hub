**THREE ROUNDS. FIND THE WEAK SPOT.**

An amateur bout of three rounds against one of five opponents. Before the bell, pick your best punch and your weak spot. Your opponent has both too, hidden: you find them out as your punches land, and they find out yours. Each exchange, throw a jab, a cross, a hook or an uppercut, or cover up to get your wind back.

- **Three judges** score every round; a knockdown costs a point and brings a count.
- **What you have learned** about your opponent, on screen as you learn it.
- **A sporting finish:** the boxers touch gloves, whoever wins.
- **Your record:** wins, early wins, losses and draws.

## Controls

- **1** to **4**: your best punch, then your weak spot
- **1** to **5**, **↑ ↓** and **Enter**, or a click: each exchange
- **Enter**: the next round; **N**: a new bout; **Tab**: the rules; **M**: sound on or off; **Esc**: quit
