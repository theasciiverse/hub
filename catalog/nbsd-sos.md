**THREE LETTERS. TWO PLAYERS. ONE CRY FOR HELP.**

It looks like noughts and crosses, but here nobody owns a letter. Each turn you write an S or an O in an empty square of the 5x6 board. Complete S-O-S in a straight line, across, down or diagonally, and the point is yours, and the letters take your colour. One well-placed letter can finish more than one line at once. But every letter you write is one your opponent can use too...

When the board is full, the higher score wins.

- **Play the computer, a friend, or watch**: you play blue and move first, the computer plays yellow; two people can share one keyboard, and the computer can play itself.
- **A computer that learns from you**: it starts out playing at random, plays better after you win ("I'd play better next time.") and worse after it wins. Keep beating it and it asks: "Are you human?"
- **Mouse too**: left click writes S, right click writes O.

**SPELL IT OUT.**

## Controls

- **Arrow keys** or **hjkl**: move
- **S** or **O**: write that letter
- **Mouse**: left click writes S, right click writes O
- **F1** or **?**: help on the controls, **F2** or **!**: how to play
- **q** or **Esc**: quit

## Options

The command is `sos`.

- `-1 h` or `-1 c`: a human or the computer plays blue
- `-2 h` or `-2 c`: the same for yellow; either one skips the questions
- `-p 3`: a fixed strength for the computer, from 1 to 127
- `-n`: quit after one game instead of offering another

## History

SOS is one of the games abakh wrote for New BSD Games, a collection of
small curses games started in 2018 and named after the classic BSD games.
The collection is packaged by Debian, Ubuntu, openSUSE, Alpine, Nix,
Homebrew, FreeBSD and pkgsrc, and is in the AUR.
