**FOUR DIMENSIONS. ONE WAY THROUGH. DO YOU DARE STEP SIDEWAYS?**

Up, down, left, right: you know those. Tessera hands you two more, and a maze that uses every one of them!

Every box on the screen is one flat slice of the maze. The boxes beside it are its neighbours in the third dimension, the boxes above and below are its neighbours in the fourth. Walk the corridors of one slice, and where a cell is marked, step clean through into the next slice and land in the very same spot. Cyan `◂ ▸ ↔` for the slices left and right, amber `▴ ▾ ↕` for the slices above and below. You start in the top left corner of the first slice. The pink `◆` waits in the far corner of the last. Between you and it: a tangle of passages no three-dimensional creature was ever meant to walk.

Lost? Then turn the whole maze. Any two of its four directions can be the slices, so every maze can be seen six ways, and a path that snakes through slice after slice in one view may run dead straight in another. That is the moment Tessera clicks, and it is a wonderful moment.

- **The campaign:** twelve mazes, the same for everyone, from *Side by side* and *Upstairs* through *Nine rooms* and *The long way* to *Twelve rooms*, and last of all the *Dark tesseract*
- **Lights out!** In fog you see only what you have already walked past. Two campaign mazes are fogged, and any endless one can be
- **The daily maze:** one new maze a day, the same for every player in the world. Solve it day after day and Tessera counts your streak
- **Endless mazes** in four sizes, fresh every time
- **Race your own ghost:** beat a campaign or daily maze, and next time your best run walks beside you as a grey `@`, step for step on your clock
- **Hints** when you are truly stuck, at ten seconds a time
- **Watch it solve itself,** the maze turning as the way through is walked

Your mind will bend. Your times will drop. **STEP THROUGH!**

## Controls

- **Arrows** or **WASD**: walk inside a slice
- **J** and **L**: step to the slice on the left or right
- **I** and **K**: step to the slice above or below
  (**Shift** with the arrows works too)
- **V** or **Tab**: turn the maze to its next view
- **H**: hint (+10 s); **P**: pause; **R**: start the maze over;
  **M**: sound off and on; **G**: show or hide your best run;
  **Esc**: back
- **Gamepad**: left stick or d-pad walk, right stick or the face buttons
  step between slices, the triggers turn the maze, LB hints, Start pauses

## Options

- `--daily`: straight into today's maze
- `--level N`: campaign maze N
- `--endless small`, `medium`, `large` or `huge`, with `--fog` for fog
- `--watch`: watch the way through being walked, the maze turning as it goes
- `--mute`: no sound

## History

In 1888 Charles Howard Hinton, a British mathematician obsessed with the fourth dimension, published *A New Era of Thought*, a book of model cubes meant to train the mind to see in four dimensions, and gave the world the word for a four-dimensional cube: the tesseract. More than a century later bleuje built a 4D maze for the web browser that drew the impossible as a grid of flat mazes, one beside the other. Tessera took that idea into the terminal on 2 November 2026, written from scratch, and gave it a campaign, a maze for every day, a ghost to race and six ways to turn the whole thing in your hands.
