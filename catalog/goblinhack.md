**GOBLINS HOARD IN THEIR FOUL DARK HOLE.**

Pick a hero, pick a dungeon and go down. Goblin Hack is a real-time dungeon romp: you walk, the monsters walk, and everything happens at once. Most levels are made fresh, from twisting dungeons and caves to fractal forests, with shops to spend your zorkmids in, keys and doors, traps and treasure.

Everything in the game is drawn in characters: in the original window each orc, spider and dragon is a little picture with its own face. Here the dungeon is one character a square, so you see the whole level around you, and **Tab** switches to the pictures.

- **Twenty-one heroes**, from Wizard, Valkyrie and Barbarian to Battle Orc, Necromancer, Future Soldier and Goblin, each with their own strength, skill and kit
- **Dozens of kinds of monster**: centipedes, slime molds, dread spiders, hydras, ghosts, vampires, dragons and, of course, goblins
- **Spells, potions and wands**, swords, axes, warhammers, bows and guns, armour and amulets
- **Shops**, with a shopkeeper who expects to be paid
- **Fate points** that pull you back from death, until they run out
- **Save and continue**, a high score table, and the bones of fallen heroes left for the next one to find

## Controls

- **Arrow keys** or the keypad: walk; **Space**: attack and fire
- **a** and **z**: choose an item; **u** or **Enter**: use it; **d**: drop it
- **o**: open doors; **p**: pause, or pay in a shop
- **s**: save; **h**: help; **q**: quit; **Tab**: the pictures view
- **Ctrl-C** leaves at once

Holding a key to keep walking works best in a terminal with the kitty keyboard protocol, such as Ghostty, kitty, WezTerm or foot; elsewhere each tap walks about a square.
