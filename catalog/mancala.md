**SEEDS, PITS, AND A LOT OF COUNTING.**

Two rows of pits, a pile of seeds, and one move: lift every seed from a pit and sow them one at a time into the pits that follow. Where the last one lands decides everything.

That's mancala, and it isn't one game but hundreds, played from West Africa to the Philippines. They all sow; after that they argue about nearly everything. Here are nine of them:

- **Oware (Abapa)**: the Ghanaian tournament game; capture twos and threes on the other side, but never take every last seed
- **Wari**: the same family, where taking everything is simply not allowed
- **Mankal'ah, the game of the ignorant** and **the game of the wise**: the coffee-house game of 1830s Cairo, played with 72 pebbles set out by hand. Don't like how they were set out? Turn the board round, and give up the first move
- **Kalah**: the American game most people call Mancala
- **Congkak**: the Malay game on a boat-shaped board, where the sowing runs on and on from every loaded hole
- **Pallanguli**: the Tamil game, where four seeds in a hole make a cow for its owner
- **Toguz Kumalak**: nine holes of nine stones from the Kazakh steppe, where three stones in an enemy hole make it yours for good
- **Bohnenspiel**: the bean game Fritz Jahn found on a Baltic baron's estate in 1908

Some games run over several boards: Kalah to 40 points, Lane's Cairo games to 60, Pallanguli round after round until someone is cleaned out.

Play the computer at three strengths, from either side, or a friend at the same keyboard.

## Controls

- **Left, Right**: choose a pit; **Enter**: sow it
- **Mouse**: click a pit
- **Any key or click** while seeds are moving: finish the move
- **Lane's games**: **Up** and **Down** drop a pebble in or take one out, **Tab** jumps to the other row, **R** scatters the rest, **Enter** when your hand is empty; then **T** turns the board round, **Enter** keeps it
- **F1** or **?**: the rules and where they come from; **M**: menu; **Q**: quit

## Options

- `--demo`: the computer plays both sides, going through the games in turn
- `--seed N`: the same game every time
