**THE SILMARILS ARE IN MORGOTH'S CROWN. BELERIAND IS WAITING FOR YOU.**

This is the First Age, the days of the War of the Jewels, and the free peoples are losing. You begin where your people live: a Grey-Elf in Menegroth, the thousand caves of Doriath; a Dwarf in the halls of Belegost; a Hobbit by the Gladden Fields; an Easterling in little Eriador Town; even an Ent. Then you walk out of the gate, and the whole of Beleriand is open to you.

Between the towns lies wilderness: plains, forests, mountains, deserts, swamps and valleys, with day and night passing over them and a choice of roads at every edge. At the ends of those roads wait four dungeons, each with a guardian at the bottom. Mîm, Betrayer of Turin, in Amon Rudh. Glaurung, Father of the Dragons, in Nargothrond. Sauron the Sorcerer on Tol-in-Gaurhoth. And a hundred levels down in Angband, Morgoth, Lord of Darkness. Ungoliant, the Unlight, broods in the valley of Nan Dungortheb.

Everything from later ages is gone: no Saruman, no Witch-King, no Elven Rings. In their place are the Silmarillion's own heroes, monsters and treasures, Ringil and Anguirel and Túrin's black sword Gurthang, and sets of artifacts that grow stronger worn together.

- **Thirteen races and nine classes**: Edain and Easterlings, four kinds of Elves and three of Dwarves, Hobbits, Drúedain, Beornings who become a great bear, and Ents
- **Specialties**: abilities you choose as you grow, and slaying a guardian earns more
- **Four ways to play the world**: the standard wilderness, an extended one, a hybrid with portals between the dungeons, or a classic Angband dungeon under a single town
- **Monster traps**, recall points you set yourself, and a house you can move to any town you have walked to

## Controls

- **Arrow keys** or **1** to **9**: move; walk into a monster to attack it
- **<** and **>**: take a path or staircase; at the edge of a wilderness map they lead on to the next
- **g**: pick up, **i**: inventory, **e**: equipment, **w**: wear or wield
- **q** quaff, **r** read, **E** eat, **a** aim a wand, **u** use a staff, **z** zap a rod, **f** fire, **v** throw
- **m**: cast a spell; **S**: specialties; **!**: change shape (Beornings)
- **+**: set a monster trap; **$**: move house; **#**: the time of day
- **C**: character sheet, **l**: look around, **M**: map, **~**: knowledge
- **=**: options (a roguelike keyset is there too); **?**: help
- **Ctrl-S**: save, **Ctrl-X**: save and quit

## History

Nick McConnell released First Age Angband 0.1.0 in November 2005, built on Leon Marrick and Bahman Rabii's Oangband 0.7.0, with one aim: to put the player in Tolkien's Silmarillion, late in the First Age. Si Griffin made version 0.3.6, and Nick carried it on to 1.4.4 at the end of 2013. Then it slept. In 2020 he started again from the ground up, rebuilding the best of the old FAangband on the latest Angband, which he was by then maintaining himself; FAangband 2.0 came out in August 2021, and it has followed Angband's fixes ever since.
