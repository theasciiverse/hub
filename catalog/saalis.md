**HIGH ABOVE THE BELT, NOTHING IS FREE.**

The Northern Reach: the cold, thin fringe of the asteroid belt, where the rocks ride high above the ecliptic and the patrols run out. You have a light courier-fighter, two thousand credits and an empty hold. Everything else, you take.

Haul ice from the mines to the farms and ore to the refinery. Buy where it's cheap, sell where it's scarce, and watch the prices move with every load. Take a cargo run from the Free Traders' Guild, a bounty from the Reach Watch, or a fixer's job in a bar that pays double for not asking questions. Run stims past the customs scanners in a compartment they mostly can't see.

And keep your guns warm. Claim-jumpers work the lanes, and their fleets are taking regions one by one. Every raider you burn down weakens their grip.

- **Real-time dogfights** with four-way shields, armour that only a dock can patch, seeking missiles and ECM.
- **Nine belt regions** joined by drive lanes, an autopilot that drops out when trouble appears, and a war that keeps moving while you fly.
- **Trade, smuggle, take contracts**: ten goods and two kinds of contraband, guild boards, fixers, deadlines.
- **Four hulls to own** and a shipyard full of plating, plasma guns, turrets and hidden compartments.
- **Five factions** that remember what you did: patrols that escort friends and shoot enemies, pirates who can be paid off.
- **On foot**: walk every base in classic ASCII, from the hangar to the bar. Knock a ship out, clamp on, and fight its crew room by room for what's in the hold.
- **Quests** written in Lua, starting with a prospector whose claim was jumped. Write your own: the game loads them from its save folder.

The catch is out there. **WILL YOU TAKE IT, OR BE IT?**

## Controls

- **Arrows** or **W A S D**: turn and throttle; **Home** full speed, **End** stop, **M** match the target's speed
- **Space** (hold): guns; **F**: a missile (it seeks when LOCK shows); **Tab** (hold): afterburner
- **T**: next target; **R**: the nearest hostile; **C**: hail
- **N**: the nav map (Tab in it: the whole Reach); **[ ]**: nav points; **G**: autopilot; **J**: jump through a lane; **L**: land
- Docked, on foot: **arrows**, **W A S D** or **h j k l** walk; walk into a counter or a person to use them; **Enter** talks
- Aboard a boarded ship: **F** fires, **M** uses a medkit
- **P**: pause; **F1**: keys; **F8**: sound; **Esc**: quit
- **Gamepad**: stick to fly, A fires, B burns, the bumpers pick targets

Terminals with the kitty keyboard protocol (kitty, foot, WezTerm, Ghostty) give exact key presses and releases.

## Options

- `--play`: straight into a new game
- `--board`: a new game, clamped onto a disabled raider
- `--demo`: the game plays itself; any key quits
- `--seed N`: the same Reach every time
- `--mute`: no sound
