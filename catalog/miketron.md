You drive a strange vehicle, the **+**, that never stops and leaves a
trail behind it. It can cross the edges of the board to come out on the
other side, and it survives explosions, but it cannot cross its own
trail: run into it and the game is over. Keep it running as long as you
can; the score counts up as it goes.

Pick-ups lie around the board:

- **?**: flight for a few moves, over the trail
- **&**: no trail for a while
- **%**: immunity for a while; trail you run over is wiped away
- **8**: a bomb that clears the board around it and sets off any bombs nearby
- **B**: five more pick-ups
- **S**: a stop; the vehicle waits until you press a key

Every hundred points, five more pick-ups and twenty pieces of loose trail
appear. The vehicle moves at half speed up and down. The board fits the
terminal, from 40 by 10 up to 80 by 24. When the game ends, your score
goes on a top-ten list.

## Controls

- **Arrow keys**, **hjkl** or **wasd**: change direction
- **e**: turn off the colors (for the flashing effects)
- **F1** or **?**: help on the controls, **F2** or **!**: how to play
- **q** or **Esc**: quit

## History

Miketron is one of the games abakh wrote for New BSD Games, a collection
of small curses games started in 2018 and named after the classic BSD
games. Its manual page says it was copied from MikeOS. The collection is
packaged by Debian, Ubuntu, openSUSE, Alpine, Nix, Homebrew, FreeBSD and
pkgsrc, and is in the AUR.
