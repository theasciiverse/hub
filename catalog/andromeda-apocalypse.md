**IT'S JUST A PERFECT DAY. THEN THE STORM COMES IN.**

"Down on the horizon, thunderstorms cloud the sky. But the sea is fresh, and
the air has taken on that orange hue. It's just a perfect day."

Korhos is setting over the shore of Monarch. Loud waves splash on the sand,
and Uncle Oren, once your mentor at the University Radiation Technology
Division, is staring at the horizon "like a bird in search of a migratory
route". "It looks like Paradise," he says. A white electric arc paints the
southern sky.

What follows takes you, Ektor Mastiff, a very long way from that beach:
"Floating in space on a strange vessel, sole survivor of a world... and
maybe of the entire human race, Ektor Mastiff must find a way through the
cosmos, on a voyage that can change the history of mankind forever."

- **Big, cinematic science fiction** from Marco Innocenti: the second part
  of his Andromeda series. You can start here, though the author recommends
  Andromeda Awakening first for the full story
- **The Extended Edition**, with achievements and a walkthrough in its own
  menus for when you are stuck
- **Plain English**: type what you want to do, and ABOUT or HELP for the
  game's own guide

## How to play

Type what you want to do, in plain words, and press Enter:

- **look**, **inventory** (or **i**), **examine** something (or **x**)
- **north**, **south**, **east**, **west**, **up**, **down** (or **n**, **s**, **e**, **w**, **u**, **d**)
- **take**, **drop**, **open**, **push** things; **help** or **about** in most games
- **save** and **restore**; **quit** to stop

Your saved games are kept in `~/.local/share/andromeda-apocalypse`.
