Warp is a space war in real time: the enemies keep moving and shooting
whether you act or not. You command an Enterprise and a Base at the same
time, in a universe that wraps around at every edge, against Klingons,
Romulans, Gorns, Tholians, space amoebas, planet crushers and homing
torpedoes. Things that blow up damage what is near them, and a dense
universe can go up in a chain reaction.

Each wave ends when your Enterprise and Base are both gone or every
enemy is destroyed, and the waves get harder. Besides phasers and photon
torpedoes you have tractor beams and a cloaking device, and the shape of
each universe's star field changes how you have to fight.

## Controls

- **hjkl yubn**: steer the current ship (diagonals on yubn)
- **Shift** with a direction: fire a photon torpedo that way
- **Ctrl** with a direction: fire phasers that way
- **%** or **Delete**: torpedoes in every direction
- **i** or **w**: Enterprise in impulse or warp mode, **p**: switch to
  the Base, **o**: the other vessel
- **c** and **v**: cloak and uncloak
- **s**: stop all torpedoes, **S** or **0**: stop the Enterprise
- **?**: command summary, **^R**: redraw, **q**: quit (it asks), **Q**:
  quit at once

## History

Larry Wall wrote Warp from 1983, years before he wrote Perl, and
version 7.0 came out in 1986. NetBSD added it to its games in November
2020, under the NetBSD Foundation's BSD licence.

The Asciiverse edition builds it for Linux and macOS, fixes three
overflows that crashed a second game in one run, and keeps its saved
games and scoreboards in the player's own data folder. Warp is part of
BSD Games in the Asciiverse catalog.
