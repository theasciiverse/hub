That tourist map of Paris must be up here somewhere in all this clutter. Your search through the attics of Meldrew Hall, an old family house full of junk, curiosities and ancestors, turns into something much larger. A long, witty and famously hard puzzle game.

## How to play

Type what you want to do, in plain words, and press Enter:

- **look**, **inventory** (or **i**), **examine** something (or **x**)
- **north**, **south**, **east**, **west**, **up**, **down** (or **n**, **s**, **e**, **w**, **u**, **d**)
- **take**, **drop**, **open**, **push** things; **help** or **about** in most games
- **save** and **restore**; **quit** to stop

Your saved games are kept in `~/.local/share/curses`.
