**RACE DOWNHILL. MIND THE TREES.**

The slope rushes up the screen at you: pine trees, rocks and snowy hills, scattered as thick as a forest. You lean left, you lean right, you straighten out again, and you look for the gap. Hit a trunk or a rock and the run is over. All that counts is how far you got.

GNUSki is a little game in the spirit of SkiFree, the skiing game many of us played on Windows in the 1990s, drawn in plain characters for the terminal.

- **Three speeds**: take it slow through the trees, or put on speed in open snow
- **An endless slope**: new obstacles keep coming from below
- **Your distance and time** at the top of the screen

## Controls

- **Left** / **h**: lean left; from a right lean, back to straight down
- **Right** / **l**: lean right; from a left lean, back to straight down
- **Down** / **j**: faster
- **Up** / **k**: slower
- **q**: stop the run
