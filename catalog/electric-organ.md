**A CORRUPTION IS TAKING OVER RETRO-FUTURE CITY, AND ONLY YOU CAN STOP IT.**

You step out of the evac zone with a pistol, one heart, two lungs, a stomach, a liver and an appendix that does nothing at all. Four levels down, under the streets, the CORRUPTOR is waiting. Getting there will cost you, quite literally, an arm and a leg.

Because in Retro-Future City, your body is your character sheet. Every heart you carry raises your health, every lung brings your breath back faster, every stomach squeezes more out of your food. So you take them from the dead. Cut out a heart, carry it in a container, pay the clinic to install it, and hope it isn't radioactive, or vampiric, or one of those prolific ones that turn out to be a tumour copying itself through your chest.

Then there are the Cronenberg guns: a pistol or a shotgun grown right into your flesh, firing on your own blood. Or swap a hand for a claw and hit like a truck, as long as you never want to hold a gun again. Every upgrade is a bargain with your own body, and every bargain has a catch.

The city fights back. Zombies get up again unless you destroy the corpse. Snatchers run off with your things, boomers explode, dividers split in two when you hit them, glowers pour out radiation and venters fill the streets with smoke. Soak up enough radiation and you mutate, whether you wanted to or not.

All of it plays to a soundtrack that doesn't exist until you hear it: every note of music and every sound effect is synthesised live, in code, as you play.

Reach the fourth level, destroy the CORRUPTOR, and make it back to the evac zone alive. Or take its heart for yourself.

## Controls

- **Arrows**: walk, and walk into enemies to fight
- **f**: fire your weapon; **c**: fire every Cronenberg weapon (costs health); **r**: reload
- **g**: get; **a**: apply; **d**: drop; **u**: unequip
- **o**: your organs; **m**: the message log; **?**: help
- **Escape**: the menu, with save and quit

## Options

- `--mute`: no sound
- `--rgb`, `--ansi`, `--greyscale`: for terminals without true colour

## History

Stephen Sherratt made Electric Organ in seven days for the 7DRL Challenge of March 2024. It draws with chargrid, the Rust library for games in characters he had written years before, and it sings through caw, a synthesiser library he wrote as well, so the game makes its own music. He didn't let it go when the week was over: in 2025 and 2026 he came back to it and brought it up to date.
