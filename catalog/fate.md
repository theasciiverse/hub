**YOUR SON IS NOT YET BORN. YOU HAVE ALREADY SEEN HOW HE DIES.**

You are nine months pregnant, and the contractions have already begun.
You are Queen in name but not in influence, trapped in a castle with more
enemies than friends, and at last your spells have worked: the crystal
ball in your den shows what waits for your child. Writhing lines of black
and purple, and then an image. It is not good.

The crown prince, Harold, wants no rival. His knight Sir Charles has
moved onto the couch in your parlour. Old Mebeira keeps her tower room
full of skulls and powders, and real power. And in a golden cage hangs
Amalia, your pixie, whose wings are worth a great deal to a sorcerer. You
can WAIT in your den for fate to arrive. Or you can act, with speed and
determination, and do what is necessary before the waters break.

- **Every move changes the vision**: look into the ball again and the
  future has shifted, toward banishment, ruin, glory or a quiet life
  far from the power games of kings
- **How far will you go?** The game asks it of you, and keeps the score
  in what you are willing to do, not in points
- **Victor Gijsbers' dark little fable**, first released in 2007, short
  enough for one evening and built to be replayed

*"The fault, dear Brutus, is not in our stars, but in ourselves."*

**The waters will break soon. What will you do?**

## How to play

Type what you want to do, in plain words, and press Enter:

- **look**, **inventory** (or **i**), **examine** something (or **x**)
- **north**, **south**, **east**, **west**, **up**, **down** (or **n**, **s**, **e**, **w**, **u**, **d**)
- **take**, **drop**, **open**, **push** things; **help** or **about** in most games
- **save** and **restore**; **quit** to stop

Your saved games are kept in `~/.local/share/fate`.
