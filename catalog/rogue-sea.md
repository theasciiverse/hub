**AHOY AN' YE BE WELCOME TO THE ROGUE SEA!**

This is yer ship. Be not she nice? She might be the Bloody Betty, or Old
Pearl, or Boaty McBoatyface, but she is small, she is yours, and she sails
a small sea dotted with islands with names like Kraken's Grave, Cursed
Lagoon and Cove of Rum. Collect four coconuts from them, and a bigger
sea and a bigger ship are waiting.

Sailing takes some learning. Yer ship is several tiles long, turns in
eighth parts of a circle, and cannot sail backwards, so take jolly care not
to get stuck (if ye get stuck, ye be cursed). Her cannons are on her sides,
so to hit another ship ye have to bring her broadside round. Some islands
have a weaponsmith for cannonballs, some fix yer deck, and some have the
coconut ye be after, always shown as yer target.

Ye are not alone out there. Merchants ply between the islands, and
corsairs come lookin' for a fight. Sink one and ye salvage its gold from
the wreck. Collect yer coconuts, earn the middle-sized ship with more
firepower, and then the biggest boat that ever sailed these seas, and loot
as much gold as ye can before the fourth coconut ends yer career. Or hear
the words no pirate wants to hear: "Scurvy! Yer ship been sent to Davy Jones' locker!"

- **Three seas and three ships**, each bigger and better armed than the last
- **Broadsides**: every cannon is numbered, and ye fire them one by one
- **Islands with names** worthy of a pirate chart, from Lonely Shores to
  Sandy Hideout
- **Corsairs and merchants** with minds of their own, and gold in their
  holds
- **A tutorial** for landlubbers, and a nontrivial amount of piratey talk

## Controls

- **Arrows**, **A W D** or **H K L**: turn the ship and sail
- **Space**, **Enter** or **.**: wait a turn
- The other keys are shown in the game as you need them
- **+** and **-**: zoom the sea in and out
- **Ctrl+Q** or **Ctrl+C**: quit

## History

Ondřej Žára, the author of rot.js, made Rogue Sea in seven days for the
Seven Day Roguelike Challenge in March 2021, "created in a hurry during a
pandemic lockdown", as the title page says, a few experimental ideas stitched
together with late-night coding and gruesome amounts of coffee: square
bitmap tiles, a zoomable sea, and ships several tiles long. It now
sails in the terminal, the game's own script exactly as he wrote it.
