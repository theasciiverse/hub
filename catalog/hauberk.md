There's a little town at the top of a very long staircase. It has a home
for your loot, six shops with names like Dirk's Death Emporium and
Skullduggery and Bamboozelry, and not much else. Everything interesting is
downstairs.

A hundred levels down, to be exact, and none of them hold still: every
time you take the stairs, the dungeon builds itself anew. Rooms and halls,
dark water and narrow bridges, pools of candlelight and long stretches of
nothing at all. Bring a light. Seriously, bring a light.

Down there: sewer rats, giant cockroaches, scurrilous imps, frosty slimes,
a murderous eye or two and, if you make it that far, The Rat King. Also
the odd hapless adventurer, who got here before you and is not doing well.

Make yourself a dwarf, an elf, a gnome, a human or one of the fae, then an
adventurer, a barbarian or a sorceror. Then decide how much death should
hurt. Gentle: you lose what you found since the last stairs or shop.
Honest: you lose the hero, for good.

Fight, loot, haul it home, sell it, buy something sharper, go deeper.
Repeat until legendary, or dead.

## Controls

- **Arrow keys**, the **number keys**, or **i o p k ; , . /**: move;
  walk into a monster to attack it
- **Shift** with a direction: run
- **L**: rest, **g**: pick up, **e**: equip, **u**: use, **d**: drop,
  **t**: throw, **x**: swap weapons, **c**: operate doors and chests
- **a**: use an ability, **Shift+E**: spend experience, **Shift+A**: about
  your hero
- In item lists, a **capital letter** shows the item's details
- **h**: help, with every key
- **q** or **Esc** on the main menu, or **Ctrl+C**: quit

Hauberk needs a terminal of at least 80 columns by 40 rows.

## Saving

Heroes are saved in your data folder (`~/.local/share/hauberk`, or
`%APPDATA%\hauberk` on Windows) whenever you take the stairs or leave a
shop.

## Options

- `--hero NAME`: start in the town with your hero NAME
- `--demo`: watch the computer play a new hero
