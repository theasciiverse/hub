The last part of the trilogy, at the bottom of the endless stair. Here the treasures matter less than the trials: the Dungeon Master is watching, and only an adventurer who proves worthy will take his place.

## How to play

Type what you want to do, in plain words, and press Enter:

- **look**, **inventory** (or **i**), **examine** something
- **north**, **south**, **east**, **west**, **up**, **down** (or **n**, **s**, **e**, **w**, **u**, **d**)
- **take lamp**, **open mailbox**, **put sword in case**
- **save** and **restore**; **quit** to stop

Your saved games are kept in `~/.local/share/zork3`.
