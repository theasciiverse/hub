**YOU ARE CHARMING, WELL DRESSED AND COMPLETELY BROKE.**

"The beautiful life is always damned, they say." Fifteen years of
champagne, carriage rides in the Tuileries and getting elegant ladies
out of elegant fixes, and now your creditors want paying. You came to
the Count's chateau to ask for an advance. But the Count has not been
home in months, Marie has vanished, and they have even turned off the
cook. So you slipped in through a gap in the kitchen garden wall, with
a dashing hat, a swordstick and a snuffbox. You will just have to find
what you can.

Emily Short calls it "an interactive search for loot", and you have a
rare talent for it. **LINK** two similar things, the
snuffbox to a small yellow box, say, and whatever you do to one happens
to the other. **REMEMBER** brings back your memories of what you find.
Put the two together, and the empty house starts to give up its
secrets.

- **Four XYZZY Awards** in 2002: Best Game, Best Story, Best Puzzles
  and Best Player Character
- **An old-fashioned puzzle game**, in the author's own words, set in
  an alternate France of the early 1780s
- **Cruel, and proud of it:** you can lock yourself out of winning, so
  save often

**Mind your manners. Help yourself to everything.**

## How to play

Type what you want to do, in plain words, and press Enter:

- **look**, **inventory** (or **i**), **examine** something (or **x**)
- **north**, **south**, **east**, **west**, **up**, **down** (or **n**, **s**, **e**, **w**, **u**, **d**)
- **take**, **drop**, **open**, **push** things; **help** or **about** in most games
- **save** and **restore**; **quit** to stop

Your saved games are kept in `~/.local/share/savoir-faire`.
