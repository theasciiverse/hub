**TWENTY-THREE DOLLARS FOR THE BEST DREAM OF YOUR LIFE.**

It's ten o'clock, there's a party downstairs at the house of people you've
never met, and you're up in the attic with the guy. You know the guy: "he's
just the guy you always meet at parties thrown by people you've never met."
He's holding up a repurposed cough syrup bottle with something green
inside. "Green like candy almost never is."

All around you is junk: old sewing machines, an Easy-Bake oven, a set of
folding chairs painted carnival blue. ("The blue chairs are bad news. Best
just to leave them alone.") Downstairs, that awful trance-bop keeps playing.

You could go back down. The game will even tell you how the rest of that
night goes, and the weekend after, and the weekend after that. Or you could
say yes.

- **A strange, sad and funny night** that goes a long way from that attic
- **A voice all its own**, from Chris Klimas, written for IFComp 2004
- **Help for the jaded**: the game's menu has hints, the author's notes and
  "The Official Bootleg Blue Chairs Soundtrack"

## How to play

Type what you want to do, in plain words, and press Enter:

- **look**, **inventory** (or **i**), **examine** something (or **x**)
- **north**, **south**, **east**, **west**, **up**, **down** (or **n**, **s**, **e**, **w**, **u**, **d**)
- **take**, **drop**, **open**, **push** things; **help** or **about** in most games
- **save** and **restore**; **quit** to stop

Your saved games are kept in `~/.local/share/blue-chairs`.
