**OPEN IT LATE. NOT TOO LATE.**

You're at the door, thousands of feet up, on a world picked at random: Mercury, Mars, the Moon, Jupiter, the Sun. You're told the height, the pull of gravity and your terminal speed, each up to five per cent off. Set the timer that opens your chute, and jump.

Open it too high and it's a dull jump. Open it too low and, well, splat.

- **Watch the fall** down a column of sky, with eight reports on the way.
- **Every good jump is ranked** against your earlier ones, by how close to the ground you opened.
- **Ten worlds** with their real gravity.

## Controls

- **0** to **9** and **.**: the free-fall timer in seconds; **↑ ↓**: ±0.5
- **Enter**: jump; **Tab**: a different jump
- **M**: sound on or off; **Esc**: quit
