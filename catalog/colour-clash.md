**RED ON RED. SKIP. REVERSE. DRAW TWO.**

Seven cards each from a deck of four colours. Match the colour, the number or the symbol on the pile. Skip the next player, turn the table around, make them draw two, or throw down a wild and name the colour yourself. The Wild Draw Four waits for when you have nothing of the colour in play.

First to an empty hand wins, and everyone else is ranked by the cards they're left holding.

- **Up to nine computer players,** who keep their skips and draw twos for whoever is about to go out.
- **The full deck:** numbers, skips, reverses, draw twos, wilds and wild draw fours.
- **Two to ten at the table,** four to start.

Colour Clash is one of the five tables of Pieczasz's tty.cards, here on its own.

**WHO'S DOWN TO ONE?**

## Controls

- **Left** / **Right**: choose a card
- **Enter**: play it (after a wild, pick the colour and Enter again)
- **D**: draw
- **Esc**: leave (asks first in a game)
- **Left** / **Right** on the setup screen: how many players; **Enter**: deal; **Q**: quit
