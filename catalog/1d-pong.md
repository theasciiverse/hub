**ONE LINE. ONE BUTTON. NOWHERE TO HIDE.**

Forget up and down. In 1D-Pong the whole court is a single glowing line, your paddle is the stretch of it at your end, and the ball can only come straight at you. All you decide is *when*.

Press while the ball is in your zone and it flies back. Hit it early and it goes back slow and safe. Wait, let it get deep, hit it at the very last moment and it screams back as a **SMASH!** Wait one moment too long and it's gone. Press before it arrives and your paddle goes dim, and for a heartbeat you can't hit at all.

Every rally gets faster. The ball heats from green through amber and red to white hot, the line throws sparks, and the whole game holds its breath for a split second on every hit. And whoever is ahead plays with a shorter paddle, so no lead is ever safe.

- **It unfolds as you play.** You start with nothing but the line. Sound, the score, the trail, the sparks, the menu: each one arrives the first time you earn it.
- **Four computer players,** from Easy to the King of Lineland. Beat the King and see what lies beyond the line.
- **Two players at one keyboard,** or with two gamepads: one button each, nothing else.
- **Watch** two Kings rally on their own.
- **The pure view:** press V for just the line and the score, nothing else at all.

First to seven. **ONE MORE MATCH...**

## Controls

- **Space**: hit (also A, Z, ← or Enter); you are on the left
- **Two players**: left A, right L, or one gamepad each
- **Gamepad**: any face, shoulder or d-pad button hits; Start pauses
- **P**: pause, **M**: sound on or off, **V**: the pure view
- **Esc**: back to the menu, **Q**: quit from the menu

## Options

- `--cpu easy|normal|hard|king`: straight into a match against that computer
- `--two`: straight into a two-player match
- `--watch`: the computer plays itself
- `--to N`: points to win a match (default 7)
- `--pure`: start in the pure view
- `--mute`: start with the sound off
- `--unfold all`: skip the unfolding; `--unfold 0` starts it over
