**FIFTY-TWO CARDS. NOTHING HIDDEN. NO MORE THAN THIRTEEN DEALS.**

Every card is face up from the start: four rows of thirteen, shuffled
anyhow. The aces slide to the left end of their rows, and the four gaps
they leave are where the game begins. A gap takes exactly one card, the
next of the same suit as the card on its left. Fill it, and the card you
moved leaves a gap of its own.

When no gap can be filled, every card not yet in sequence is gathered up
and dealt again, and you go on. Put all four suits in order, ace to
king, in as few deals as you can.

- **One key a move**: each card that can move is marked with a letter;
  press it. When only one move is left, the game plays it for you.
- **Scored by deals**: 1 to 3 is good, 4 to 7 average, 8 or more poor.
- **Kings close a row**: nothing goes to a king's right, so the order you
  fill the gaps in decides the deal.
- **Real suits**: hearts and diamonds in red, spades and clubs in black,
  on white cards.

Tim Lister wrote Blue Moon in GFA BASIC for the Atari ST, and was
surprised to find it, years later, ported to C and loose on the
Internet. Eric S. Raymond gave it its curses screen in 1994, and still
maintains it.

**Find the gap. Fill it. Beat the deal.**

## Controls

- **a, b, c, d**: move the card with that letter
- **Any key**: deal again when a deal is over
- **n**: a new game, at the end
- **r**: redraw; **q**: quit
