**YOU ARE TRAPPED IN THE CAVEZ OF PHEAR. DEATH AWAITS YOU.**

Eleven caves stand between you and freedom, and the only way out of each
one is to find every diamond hidden in it. Dig through the earth, and the
stones above you start to move: they fall into the tunnels you leave
behind, roll off each other's backs, and crush whatever is underneath,
including you.

Pick up the bombs you find, plant them where you need a door, and set
them all off at once. The bombs fall and roll like stones too, so plan
where they will land. Monsters roam the deeper caves: let them catch you
and you die, or drop a stone on them, or blow them up.

Diamonds are worth 10 points and money 100, and every thousand points
earns an extra life. Got yourself walled in? Give up the life and try the
cave again.

**Dig carefully.**

## Controls

- **Arrow keys** (or **8**, **2**, **4**, **6**): dig and move
- **b**: plant a bomb; **t**: set off every bomb you have planted
- **k**: give up this life, when you are stuck
- **s**: sound on and off; **w**: show where you are
- **q**: quit (it asks first); **Ctrl+C** quits at once

## The editor

From a terminal, `phear -e mycave` opens the cave editor: the number keys
place things, **l** locks drawing to the arrow keys, **s** saves and **q**
quits. Play your cave with `phear mycave`.
