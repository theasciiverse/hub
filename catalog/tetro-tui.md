**FOUR SQUARES. SEVEN SHAPES. AS MANY SETTINGS AS YOU LIKE.**

Pieces fall, you turn them and slot them in, full lines clear. The idea
is old; this is the modern way to play it, in your terminal, with the
handling a serious stacker expects: hold, previews, hard drops, and every
timing (DAS, ARR, soft drop, line clear and spawn delays) yours to set.

- **Seven modes and two to unlock**: Regular (160 lines as gravity
  climbs), Swift (40 lines against the clock), Puzzle (24 hand-made
  boards), Cheese, Survival, Combo and Custom; finish Regular for Classic
  and Master.
- **Your rules**: four rotation systems, piece randomizers from pure
  chance to bags, preview count, keys of your own.
- **Your look**: Unicode, ASCII, Braille or Elektronika 60 tiles, ten tile
  palettes, and line clears that burn, pop, sparkle or rain confetti.
- **Scores, replays and all-time stats**, kept in one save file.

**STACK CLEAN.**

## Controls

- **Left** / **Right**: move; **A** / **D**: rotate left / right
- **Down**: soft drop; **Up**: hard drop; **Space**: hold
- **Esc**: pause; **?**: every key, at any time
- Menus: arrows and **Enter**, or Vim keys

Keys, handling and graphics are all in Settings.

## Options

- `--seed N`: the piece sequence for a custom game
- `--board "..."`: a starting board for a custom game
