A prince has been turned into a toad. Only a kiss can turn him back, so he
sets off, crossing road after road to reach the other side and kiss
whatever is waiting there. A mailbox. A garden gnome. A cow, who licks him.
A cactus (ow). He kisses a great many strange things before he finds a real
princess, and she turns out to be a toad too. That is the happy ending.

Hop through seven worlds: village lanes full of carts and geese, the
highway, a river of logs and diving turtles, a winter road where the ice
slides you along, a night road lit only by headlights, a castle with a moat
and a snoring dragon, and at last a quiet pond, where the lily pads sink
under you and the princess keeps hopping away. Every crossing ends in a
short, silly scene.

When the story is done, the endless road keeps going, harder with every
crossing, and two players can race each other at one keyboard.

## Controls

- **W A S D** or the **arrows**: hop
- **Q E Z C** or **numpad 7 9 1 3**: hop diagonally, in the pond only
- **Esc** or **P**: pause, **H**: help, **M**: sound on or off
- Two players: player one on **W A S D**, player two on the **arrows**
- Gamepad: the d-pad or left stick hops, two d-pad directions at once hop
  diagonally, **A** goes on, **Start** pauses

## Options

- `--endless`: straight onto the endless road
- `--two`: a two-player race
- `--crossing N`: start the story at crossing N (19 is the pond)
- `--mute`: no sound
