Your daughter has caught a strange disease and none of your remedies
help. The only hope is the potion of cure dianthroritis, somewhere in
the caverns of Larn, the old haunt of the magician Polinneaus, or in the
volcano beneath them. Larn is a roguelike with a deadline: time is
counted in mobuls and runs out, and the deeper you go, the more gold, magic and
danger you find.

It starts in a town with a bank, a school, a trading post and the
dungeon's entrance, so you can buy gear, learn, and come back up to
spend what you found. You cast spells, quaff potions and read scrolls,
and the map stays as you left it when you return to a level.

## Controls

- **hjkl yubn**: move (diagonals on yubn); with **Shift**, run
- **.**: stay, **i**: inventory, **d**: drop, **g**: pack weight
- **c**: cast a spell, **r**: read, **q**: quaff, **e**: eat
- **w**: wield, **W**: wear, **T**: take off armour
- **^**: identify a trap, **D**: list everything found so far
- **?**: help, **S**: save, **Q**: quit, **^L**: redraw

## History

Noah Morgan wrote Larn in 1986 and posted it to the net "for the
enjoyment of all"; it was soon part of the BSD games. With Larn 12.0 he
let anyone use, modify and port the sources as long as no profit is
made from them.

The Asciiverse edition ports it to Linux and macOS and keeps its
scoreboard and saved games in the player's own data folder.
Larn is part of BSD Games in the Asciiverse catalog.
