**THE HOUSE ALWAYS WINS. PROVE IT WRONG.**

You walk in with a hundred chips. The dealer has a shoe, a hole card and all night. Put down 10, 20, up to 50, and look at what you've been dealt. Eleven against a dealer's five? Double down! Twelve against a six? Stand, and let the dealer bust. A hopeless sixteen against a ten? Surrender, take half your bet back, and live for the next hand. Then the hole card flips, the dealer draws to the house rule, and the chips slide one way or the other.

"Natural Blackjack! Player Wins!" That's the line you're playing for.

And it's your casino. Before the first card you write the house rules: blackjack paid at a generous 3:2, or the stingy 7:5 and 6:5 of the tougher tables; a dealer who hits or stands on soft 17; one deck, two, or a six-deck shoe; and how deep into the shoe the dealer goes before the reshuffle. Card counters, take note: the count of cards dealt is always in the corner.

- **Your house, your rules:** payout, soft 17, decks and shuffle point, all set before the first deal
- **Double down and late surrender,** the two moves that turn blackjack into a game of nerve
- **The hole card stays down** until you've made your call, just like at the real table
- **Save and quit** on a hot streak: your bankroll and your table wait for you, ready for the comeback

**HIT OR STAND?**

## Controls

- **Up** / **Down**: move between choices
- **Enter**: choose
- **Backspace**: go back while setting up the table
- **Ctrl+C**: quit (Save and Quit keeps your game)

## History

MarcusBilo wrote Blackjack in Go with Charm's Bubble Tea and dealt the first hand in September 2025: one player, one dealer, and the house rules handed over to the player for once. Nearly every word on the screen lives in one strings file, so the whole table can be retold in another language without touching a line of the game.
