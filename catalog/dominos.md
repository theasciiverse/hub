**IVORY TILES. GREEN BAIZE. CLACK!**

Pull up a chair. Three games, two to four players, and nobody minds if you count on your fingers.

- **The Block game**: the purest one. Match an open end or pass; the first to go out, or the lowest hand when everyone is stuck, takes everyone else's pips.
- **The Draw game**: the same, with a boneyard to draw from whenever you like, even when you could play, until only two tiles are left there. More tiles, more choices, more pips to be caught holding.
- **All Fives**: the first double becomes the spinner and the line grows four arms. Every time the open ends add up to a multiple of five, you score on the spot. First to 200 wins, the moment it's reached.

Play the computer, or open a table on the local network: your friends find it by themselves, take the open seats, and the computer fills the rest. At four, play partners, sitting opposite and scoring as a side. Everyone only ever sees their own hand, so no peeking.

The computer comes in three strengths, and in All Fives the hard one is sly: it remembers which numbers you couldn't play, and leaves you ends you can't match. **SHUFFLE UP.**

## Controls

- **← →** or **h l**: choose a tile from your hand, or click it
- **Enter** or **Space**: play it; where it fits two ways, **← →** choose the end
- **D**: draw from the boneyard
- **?** or **F1**: the rules of the game you are playing
- **Q** or **Esc**: back to the menu

## Options

- `--rules block|draw|fives`: the game the menu starts on
- `--demo`: the computer plays every seat
- `--seed N`: the same deal again
