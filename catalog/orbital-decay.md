**DON'T OPEN! DEAD INSIDE!**

Those are the words smeared in blood across the airlock door, and you are going through it anyway. The space station is out of fuel and sinking towards Earth, and you are its special delivery: one hydrogen fuel cell in a pack on your back, five flights of stairs down to the fuel bay, and no word from the crew. Back home your buddies are betting on whether you make it out. Last you heard, the odds were 5 to 1 against. Better make that 6 to 1.

Orbital Decay is a turn-based tactical roguelike about shooting your way through a station full of the undead former crew, where every gun is also a hole you might put in the hull.

- **Mind the hull.** Every weapon has its damage and its penetration, and every zombie its health and armour. A round that punches through one enemy flies on into the next, and one that reaches the wall may breach it
- **Breaches are a storm.** When the hull goes, a red light comes on and the air roars out towards space, dragging you, your loot and the dead along with it. Then the light turns blue, the vacuum sets in, and your oxygen starts to run down
- **Seven ways to make a mess:** the trusty rifle, a shotgun, a railgun that shoots through nearly anything, a chainsaw, a gaus cannon that cooks the crew and spares the hull, an oxidiser that turns the dead into air, and a life stealer that turns them into health
- **Credits** lying about the station to spend on the upgrades you find: a strong back for a third gun, a hardy body with twice the health, a careful aim that halves your chance of breaching
- **A map** of each floor, if you can find it, and an ending with a twist and a great deal of paperwork

## Controls

- **Arrows**, **WASD** or **HJKL**: walk, and aim
- **1** to **3**: fire the gun in that slot; **Escape** cancels the aim
- **Space**: wait; **G**: pick up a weapon; **X**: examine
- **Escape**: the menu, with save and quit, options and help
- A gamepad works too

## Options

- `--rgb`, `--ansi`, `--greyscale`: for terminals without true colour
- `--new-game`: skip the menu; `-r <seed>`: the first run's seed

## History

Stephen Sherratt, who makes games as gridbugs, wrote Orbital Decay in seven days for the 7DRL Challenge of March 2021, on chargrid, his own Rust library for games drawn in characters. The same game ran in a window, in a browser as literal text, and in a terminal, and he kept polishing it for two more years. It is part of a line of his seven-day roguelikes, one most years since 2018, each a small complete world.
