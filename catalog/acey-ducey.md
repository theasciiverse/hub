**WILL IT FALL IN BETWEEN?**

Two cards are dealt face up, aces high. Bet any amount you hold, or pass, that the next card falls strictly between them; a card matching either one loses. You start with $100 and play until you go broke or decide to walk away while you are ahead.

- **A ruler of ranks** lights the cards that would win.
- **The odds,** as the share of cards left in the deck that would win, before you bet.
- **Your record:** games, times broke, the best walk-away and the most you ever held.

## Controls

- Type a bet, or step it with **↑ ↓** or the mouse wheel; **Enter** or the Bet button: bet
- **P**: pass; **H**: bet half; **A**: all in; **W**: walk away
- **N**: start over; **Tab**: the rules; **M**: sound on or off; **Esc**: quit
