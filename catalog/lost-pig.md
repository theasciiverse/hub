Pig lost! Boss say it Grunk fault. Grunk go find pig, bring it back, or not bring back Grunk either. Grunk the orc follows the pig into the woods and under the ground, where a gnome and a lot of old things are waiting. Told entirely in Grunk's own words, and very funny.

## How to play

Type what you want to do, in plain words, and press Enter:

- **look**, **inventory** (or **i**), **examine** something (or **x**)
- **north**, **south**, **east**, **west**, **up**, **down** (or **n**, **s**, **e**, **w**, **u**, **d**)
- **take**, **drop**, **open**, **push** things; **help** or **about** in most games
- **save** and **restore**; **quit** to stop

Your saved games are kept in `~/.local/share/lost-pig`.
