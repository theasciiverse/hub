**PUSH. JUMP. FALL. THINK.**

A maze seen from the side, where gravity is always on. Reach the exit
`}` and eat the sweets `.` on the way, by pushing the blocks `@` into
places that make a floor, a step or a bridge. Push a whole row at once;
knock one loose and watch everything above it come down.

You can jump, and pass through one-way doors `< v ^ >` that the blocks
cannot. Some floors crumble under your feet. And the maze wraps around:
walk off one edge and you come back on the other. Six levels, from
GiveItAShoot to Dors, and the game remembers where you got to.

With **bed**, the level editor, you can build mazes of your own and play
them by name.

## Controls

- **h j k l** (or the keypad **1 2 5 3**, or the arrow keys): left,
  down, up, right
- **H** and **L** (or **z** and **x**, or keypad **4** and **6**): jump
  left and right
- **R** or **Space**: redraw; **w**: high scores; **?**: help; **q**: quit

## Options

From a terminal: `block -s` asks for every sweet before the exit counts,
`block -m` sets a limit on moves, and `block mylevel` plays a level of
your own. `bed mylevel` opens the editor (`?` there lists the pieces,
`w` saves).
