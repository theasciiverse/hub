**TOO LATE TO SLEEP. TOO EARLY TO LEAVE.**

"Odd, how the light just makes your apartment gloomier." It is the dark
before dawn and you are sprawled on the futon with gritty eyes. Your
luggage is packed for a week in the deep desert at the Death Valley Om,
your ride arrives in a few hours, and there is nothing left to do but
wait. Water the hyacinth. Check the to-do list. Get a drink; your mouth
is dry wool.

One desk, one futon, one mirror trying to make the place look twice its
size. You know every inch of this room. So why does it keep looking
less familiar?

- **One room**, and everything in it matters
- **Short and best played cold:** the less you know going in, the better
  it works
- **By Andrew Plotkin** (credited on screen as "Ampe R. Sand"), coded in
  a race for the 2000 Interactive Fiction Competition, where it placed
  tenth

**Turn on the light. See what it shows you.**

## How to play

Type what you want to do, in plain words, and press Enter:

- **look**, **inventory** (or **i**), **examine** something (or **x**)
- **north**, **south**, **east**, **west**, **up**, **down** (or **n**, **s**, **e**, **w**, **u**, **d**)
- **take**, **drop**, **open**, **push** things; **help** or **about** in most games
- **save** and **restore**; **quit** to stop

Your saved games are kept in `~/.local/share/shade`.
