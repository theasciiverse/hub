**THE AMULET OF YENDOR HAS BEEN STOLEN. ARMIES WERE SUGGESTED. THEY SENT YOU.**

The Wizard of Yendor took the Amulet and went to ground in the Dungeons of Doom. Hiring a superhero or two was discussed, but in the end it was cheaper to offer a reward and let some poor adventurer with dreams of glory go and fetch it. SLASH'EM, Super Lotsa Added Stuff Hack, Extended Magic, is NetHack with more of everything on the way down.

Eighteen roles: a Flame Mage or an Ice Mage, a Necromancer who raises the dead, an Undead Slayer, a Yeoman with his pony, as well as the Valkyrie and the Wizard. Ten races, from drow and hobbits to doppelgangers who change shape at will, lycanthropes and vampires. Every role learns techniques of its own as it grows, and there are lightsabers in the dungeon, and pistols and shotguns.

Past the Gnomish Mines and Sokoban the dungeon branches again and again: Grund's Stronghold, the Wyrm Caves, the Lost Tomb, the Spider Caves, the Sunless Sea, the Temple of Moloch, the Giant Caverns. One-eyed Sam runs a black market behind a portal, a guild of disgruntled adventurers keeps a level of its own, and down in Gehennom, beside Vlad's Tower, stands Frankenstein's Lab. Beside your role's quest, three more portals lead to the aligned key quests, where the Nightmare, the Beholder and Vecna wait.

- **Eighteen roles and ten races**, each role with its techniques
- **New branches and special levels** all the way down
- **Lightsabers, firearms** and hundreds of new monsters and items
- **The full NetHack game** underneath: Elbereth, prayer, polymorph, shops and all

## Controls

- **hjkl yubn**: move, the last four diagonally; with **Shift**, run
- **F** and a direction: fight; **t**: throw, **f**: fire, **z**: zap a wand
- **i**: inventory, **,**: pick up, **d**: drop
- **e** eat, **q** quaff, **r** read, **w** wield, **x** swap weapons, **W** wear, **T** take off, **P** put on, **R** remove, **a** apply
- **Alt-t** or `#technique`: use a technique
- **o** and **c**: open and close doors; **s**: search; **.**: rest
- `>` and `<`: stairs down and up
- **#**: extended commands, such as `#pray`
- **?**: help and the Guidebook; **;**: what is that symbol; **Ctrl-P**: earlier messages
- **S**: save and quit

**S** saves the game to your state folder (`~/.local/state/slashem`), and starting SLASH'EM again carries on from there. Your options go in `slashemrc` in that folder, as `OPTIONS=` lines; `OPTIONS=name:Freya,role:Valkyrie` skips the questions at the start.

## History

In February 1996 Tom Proudfoot released SLASH, built from his own NetHack-- and Stephen White's NetHack Plus; Enrico Horn later carried it onto NetHack 3.2. In 1997 Warren Cheung, a high school student in his graduating year, wanted both SLASH and the Wizard Patch, and his name on Darren Hebden's Roguelike News. The two would not patch together, so one bored night he did it by hand, never having written C before. "I had to fix a couple typos. It compiled. It ran. SLASH'EM was born." That was November 1997. Bug reports and thank-you notes kept arriving, so he kept going, and a team grew around the game: J. Ali Harlow, Pekka Rousu, Clive Crous, Kevin Hugo and Paul Hurtley among many. Its last releases came on 30 December 2006, and SLASH'EM has been played on public servers ever since, and forked into variants of its own.
