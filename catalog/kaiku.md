**THE WAR IS RAGING. AND YOU'RE ON THE WRONG SIDE OF IT.**

An intergalactic war burns across the stars, and you're right in the thick of it. Then, out of nowhere, a savage blast rips through your ship and hurls you off course, far beyond the front line, deep into enemy territory where no human has ever been!

No charts. No backup. No way back. Just the Amber and Jade fleets closing in from every side, and a receiver crackling in the dark...

*...the eagle has landed... houston, we've had a problem... godspeed, john glenn...*

Earth's old transmissions, bouncing through space for decades! Broken, garbled, half lost in the static, and they're your only map home. The clearer the echo, the nearer home!

Interceptors flash out of nowhere! Lancers charge down their deadly lines! Jammers scramble the very voices you need to hear! Every sector is a gauntlet, and at the end of every one wait three gates. Only one leads closer to Earth.

- **Fight with your guns, decode with your fingers!** Type each echo clear, letter by letter, with enemy fire all around you.
- **Choose your gate!** Pick wrong and you're thrown off the path. Pick right and Earth gets closer.
- **Snatch power-ups by typing their words:** spread shot, rapid fire, shields, smart bombs, an escort drone, even slow time!
- **The real words of Apollo, Mercury, Gemini and President Kennedy**, waiting to be pulled out of the static.
- **Two players, one ship!** Plug in a gamepad: one flies, one decodes.

You're alone, outgunned and light-years from home. **CAN YOU MAKE IT BACK?!**

## Controls

- **Arrows** or **Ctrl+h/j/k/l**: fly
- **Space** (hold): fire
- **a-z**: lock onto an echo or a power-up's word, and decode it
- **Backspace**: drop the lock (progress is kept)
- **Tab**: dash, a burst of speed you can't be hit in
- **Enter**: smart bomb; on the map, fly on
- **Esc**: pause; **F1**: the controls; **F8**: sound and music on or off
- **Gamepad**: stick or d-pad to fly, bottom face button to fire, right face button to bomb, left face button to dash

Terminals with the kitty keyboard protocol (kitty, foot, WezTerm, Ghostty) give exact key presses and releases.

## Options

- `--skip-intro`: straight into sector 1
- `--sector N`: start in a later sector
- `--demo`: the game plays itself; any key quits
- `--credits`: the credits on their own
- `--mute`, `--no-music`: no sound, or effects only
