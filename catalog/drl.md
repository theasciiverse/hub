**THE ONLY WAY OUT IS DOWN.**

Something has gone very wrong on the Phobos base. The comm is full of screams, then gunshots, then silence. You are the marine they send in, with a pistol and a bad feeling, and the only way out is down: through the moon bases, through the gates, and into hell itself.

DRL is a fast, tactical roguelike. Every level is new, every death is final, and every fight is a question of where you stand: behind a barrel, next to a door, or far out of reach of whatever is coming around the corner. Pick up shotguns, chainguns, plasma rifles and rocket launchers, and modify them. Grow your marine with traits as you gain levels, and work towards a master trait that changes how you play.

- **Three classes**: Marine, Scout and Technician
- **Five difficulty levels**, from *I'm Too Young To Die!* to *Nightmare!*
- **Challenge games** that change the rules, and seeded games
- **Assemblies**: combine mods into unique weapons and armour

## Controls

- **Arrow keys** or the number pad: move (Home, End, PgUp, PgDn diagonally)
- **Esc**: menu, help and Save & Quit
- In menus: **Up**, **Down** and **Enter**

Your settings, saves and scores are kept in `~/.local/share/drl`.
