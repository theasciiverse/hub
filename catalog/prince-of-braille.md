**SIXTY MINUTES. FOURTEEN LEVELS. ONE PRINCESS.**

The Sultan is away at war, and the Grand Vizier Jaffar has seized the
palace. He has given the princess an hour: marry him, or die when the last
sand runs through the glass. You are in his dungeon, without a sword.

Run, leap and climb your way up through the dungeons and the palace. Test
each step for loose floor, time your run past the slicers, jump the spikes,
find the plate that opens the gate. Take a sword from the dungeon floor and
fight your way past the guards, each with a style of his own, and past the
skeleton that will not stay down. Drink the potions you find, if you dare,
and beware the stranger who steps out of the mirror.

The clock never stops. Every few minutes the time left goes up on the
screen, and in her room the princess waits as the sand falls.

## The original, in the terminal

Every routine is ported from Mechner's own 6502 source: the same moves,
traps, guards and levels, the same 12 frames a second. The prince's
rotoscoped animation is drawn in Braille dots, so every frame of his run,
his leap and his careful step survives; the stone around him is drawn in
block glyphs, in four colour themes from dusk blue to the Apple's own.

- All fourteen levels, the shadow, the skeleton, the vizier and the mouse
- The princess's room between levels, and both endings
- The speaker sound effects as the Apple played them, and the DOS music
- `--demo` plays the original's attract demo

## Controls

- Arrow keys, WASD or hjkl to run, climb, crouch and jump; Home and PgUp
  (or `q` and `e`) jump up and to the side
- Shift or Space: grab a ledge, step carefully, drink, strike with the
  sword; Up blocks
- `r` restarts the level, `p` pauses, `m` turns the sound off, `c` changes
  the colours
- Esc twice quits

Held keys are read exactly in terminals with the kitty keyboard protocol;
elsewhere from the keyboard's repeat.
