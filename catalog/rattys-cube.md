**TWENTY-SIX CUBIES. FORTY-THREE QUINTILLION WAYS TO MIX THEM UP. ONE WAY HOME.**

A real 3D cube hangs in your terminal, shaded and turning in smooth animation. Grab any sticker with the mouse and drag it: the whole layer swings round with it. Drag the empty space around the cube to look at it from any side.

Then press Space. You get a scramble the way competitions make them, from a truly random position, and fifteen seconds of inspection while the judge calls eight and twelve. Your first turn starts the clock, the last one stops it, and every solve goes into your best time and your averages of five and twelve.

- **Turn it your way**: drag stickers with the mouse, or type the notation on the keyboard, faces, slices and whole-cube turns
- **A speedcubing timer** with inspection, +2 and DNF just like the real thing, best, ao5 and ao12
- **Stuck? Press h** for the moves that solve it from wherever you are
- **Or press Enter** and watch it solve itself, slowly enough to follow along
- **Its own solver**, Herbert Kociemba's two-phase algorithm, usually finds a way home in about twenty-one moves
- **A flat net** beside the cube shows every face at once
- **Clicks and calls**: every layer clicks into place, the judge calls the inspection, and a fanfare greets a new best

## Controls

- **Mouse**: drag a sticker to turn its layer; drag the background to look around; the wheel zooms
- **R L U D F B**: turn a face clockwise; with **Shift**, the other way
- **M E S**: a middle slice; **x y z**: the whole cube
- **Space**: scramble and start a timed solve; **Esc**: stop the solve
- **Backspace**: undo; **n**: a solved cube to practise on
- **h**: a hint; **Enter**: show me
- **Arrows**: look around; **0**: look home; **+ -**: zoom
- **Tab**: the net; **F8**: sound on or off; **?**: help; **q**: quit

## Options

- `--mute`: start with the sound off
