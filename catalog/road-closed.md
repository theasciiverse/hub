**YOU SLIP PAST THE CORDON IN THE EARLY HOURS OF THE MORNING.**

Somewhere deep within the zone there is rumoured to be a room that will grant any wish. It is two thousand kilometres down the road, the road is closed, and you are going anyway, in an old car with a full tank, a little food and nothing in your hands.

Road Closed is a traditional roguelike about a road trip through an exclusion zone. Most of it you drive, watching the hours and the kilometres tick by. Then you stop, because you have to: the fuel is low, the food is gone, night is coming. You get out of the car at an abandoned fire trail through a pine plantation gone wild, a swamp where the road bridges flooded woodland past ruined cabins, a mountain pass between the cliffs, and you scavenge what you can before the things that live there find you.

- **Keep the car going.** Fuel cans to refill it, a boot to load with what you find, and a long road ahead
- **Keep yourself going.** Health, energy and food all run down. Coffee and fruit help; the effort of swinging an axe tires you out, and heavy armour makes you hungry
- **Fear the dark.** Light a fire with your firewood to sleep through the night: night stalkers come out after sunset, can climb, and are afraid of only one thing
- **Things in the zone:** zombies that don't stay dead, slimes that split when you hit them, climbers that come over anything, and drainers that sap your strength
- **Bare hands, a knife or an axe**, and overalls, light armour or heavy armour
- **Make a wish.** Reach the end of the road and write it down, "I wish..."; the game keeps every wish you have ever made

## Controls

- **Arrows**: walk, and walk into enemies to fight
- Walk into the side of the car to drive; into its back for the boot
- **g**: get; **i**: inventory; **a**: apply; **d**: drop
- **u**: unequip the weapon; **r**: remove armour
- **m**: message log; **?**: help; **Escape**: the menu, with save and quit

## Options

- `--rgb`, `--ansi`, `--greyscale`: for terminals without true colour

## History

Stephen Sherratt, who makes games as gridbugs, wrote Road Closed in seven days for the 7DRL Challenge of March 2026, inspired by the Strugatsky brothers' Roadside Picnic and the driving game Pacific Drive, on chargrid, his own Rust library for games drawn in characters. It is the newest of a line of his seven-day roguelikes, one most years since 2018.
