**KILL THE EGGS BEFORE THEY HATCH!**

You are sixty metres below the ground, in a facility overrun by insects from somewhere else, and there is only one way out: up. Every level gives you an order, flashing at the top of the screen. Kill the eggs before their hatching meters fill. Activate the emergency beacon. Kill the boss. Do it, find the stairs, and climb a little closer to the surface.

Meters Below the Ground is a short tactical dungeon crawler in which everything is a progress meter. Your health is a meter, your armour and your stamina are meters, your weapons are meters you spend and refill, the eggs are meters counting down to hatching, and down at the bottom of the screen, the biggest meter of all counts the metres still between you and daylight.

- **A brood that grows up:** eggs hatch into larvae, larvae wrap themselves into chrysalises, chrysalises break open as aracnoids and beetoids, super eggs hatch into queens, and on one level the queen herself is the target
- **Meters for everything:** a quadgun that shoots in four directions at once, a railgun that shoots every enemy in a line, a push that throws them back, a blink that teleports you a short way, a medkit that recharges, a metabol that slows nearby enemies' transformations, a compass to the nearest goal, and stamina to spend on punches
- **Ammo on the floor**, meds and armour shards, to fill the meters up again
- **A choice on every stair:** as you climb, you find a new meter or an upgrade to take with you

## Controls

- **Arrows**: move, and walk into enemies to punch
- **0** to **9**: use the meter in that slot, then a direction to aim it
- **Space**: wait; **Escape**: the menu, with save and quit

## History

Stephen Sherratt, who makes games as gridbugs, wrote Meters Below the Ground in seven days for the 7DRL Challenge of March 2018, on prototty, the Rust library for games drawn in characters that later grew into his chargrid. It was the first of the line of his seven-day roguelikes that followed most years after: slime99, Orbital Decay, Rain Forest, Boat Journey, Electric Organ and Road Closed.
