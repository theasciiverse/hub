You wake in a luxurious stateroom aboard the last battlestar, while
explosions shake the ship. Get off it, find your way down to a tropical
island, and explore its beaches, palm groves, caves and a village, until
you win the hand of a goddess.

It is an adventure in the classic style, typed in plain English, but
less of a puzzle box than most: the fun is in exploring its 275 rooms.
Along the way there is a fight in space, played in real time.

## Controls

Type a command and press Enter. The parser understands simple English,
several objects at once and the word you used last:

- **take**, **drop**, **wear**, **draw**, **put on**, **take off**,
  **throw**, **eat**, **open**, **use** and many more: `take knife and
  sword, drop all`
- **ahead**, **back**, **left** and **right** (or **a**, **b**, **l**,
  **r**), **up** and **down**: move. Directions are relative to where
  you face; find a compass to know where north is
- **look**, **inven**, **score**
- **save**: save the game; it is restored the next time you start
- **quit**: leave

## History

David Riggle wrote Battlestar in 1979 "to experiment with the niceties
of the C Language", and it became one of BSD's games.
This edition is Mike Sharov's bsd-games. Between 2016 and 2020 he made
the space fight winnable within its time limit, took out the original's
sexual violence (marrying the goddess now wins the game), renamed the
parts borrowed from Battlestar Galactica, and added saving and a score
table.
