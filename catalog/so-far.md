Hot, foul and dark: an indoor theatre in high summer, a play you love, and Aessa, who was supposed to meet you, nowhere to be seen. From there the way leads out of the theatre and into worlds joined by doors and choices, each with its own logic and its own price. A long, demanding puzzle game about distance and resolution.

## How to play

Type what you want to do, in plain words, and press Enter:

- **look**, **inventory** (or **i**), **examine** something (or **x**)
- **north**, **south**, **east**, **west**, **up**, **down** (or **n**, **s**, **e**, **w**, **u**, **d**)
- **take**, **drop**, **open**, **push** things; **help** or **about** in most games
- **save** and **restore**; **quit** to stop

Your saved games are kept in `~/.local/share/so-far`.
