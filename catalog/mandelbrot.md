The Mandelbrot set, drawn in characters and colour bands. It opens on the
whole set, fitted to your terminal; steer anywhere along its edge and zoom
in, and the spirals, seahorses and tiny copies of the set keep coming, a
hundred levels deep. Every point is worked out live as you move, from Ken
Perlin's famous one-line Mandelbrot.

## Controls

- **Arrow keys**: move the view
- **+** or **=**: zoom in
- **-**: zoom out
- **q** or **Ctrl+C**: quit

The most detail lives along the boundary of the black shape: try the
valley between the two big bulbs, or the tips of the small buds around them.
