Korhos is setting over the shore, a storm is coming in from the south, and Uncle Oren is waiting by the water. What follows takes Ektor Mastiff, the sole survivor of a world, across the cosmos on a strange vessel. The second part of the Andromeda series; the first is not needed to play.

## How to play

Type what you want to do, in plain words, and press Enter:

- **look**, **inventory** (or **i**), **examine** something (or **x**)
- **north**, **south**, **east**, **west**, **up**, **down** (or **n**, **s**, **e**, **w**, **u**, **d**)
- **take**, **drop**, **open**, **push** things; **help** or **about** in most games
- **save** and **restore**; **quit** to stop

Your saved games are kept in `~/.local/share/andromeda-apocalypse`.
