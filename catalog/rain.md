**IT HAS BEEN RAINING SINCE 1980.**

Drops of rain fall on a still pond. Each one lands as a dot, swells to a small o and then a big O, and spreads out in two widening rings before it fades and another drop lands somewhere else. Five ripples are on the water at any time, all in plain characters, and it never repeats the same pattern twice.

## Controls

- **Any key**: quit

## Options

From a terminal, `rain -d 50` sets the pause between drops in
milliseconds (the default is 120).

## History

Eric P. Scott wrote rain at Caltech High Energy Physics in November
1980, modelled on the VAX/VMS program of the same name, and it has
shipped with BSD Unix ever since. This edition quits on any key and
makes a new pattern every run. It is part of BSD Games in the Asciiverse
catalog.
