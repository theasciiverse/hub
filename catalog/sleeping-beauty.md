**A TRULY BEAUTIFUL DAY FOR A HEROIC ACTION!**

Into a profound slumber she sank, surrounded only by dense brambles, thorns
and roses. Many adventurers tried to find and rescue her, but none came
back.

Now it is your turn. The tower stands among plains and trees, and there
might be a princess sleeping on its last floor. The only way to her is up,
floor after floor, through doors and dark rooms and brambles that scratch
you as you push through. Pick up daggers, swords, axes, maces and, if you
are lucky, an epic greatsword; shields, helmets and tempered armour; health
and mana potions; golden coins. Meet the other heroes who had the same
idea, dwarven knights and halfling explorers, one of whom sighs: "I used
to be an adventurer like you. But then I got hurt on a thorn..."

Not everyone in the tower is friendly. Rats, bats and goblins, orcs and an
orcish witch, skeletons, zombies, spiders, poisonous snakes, ogres, animated
trees and minotaur warriors, and every fight with them is played as the
**Game of Thorns**: a square board of coloured thorns where you clear runs
of one colour. Green runs are your sword, blue ones your spells; red and
yellow belong to your enemy, and they strike when you clear them too. A
sword of power or a shield of magical domination tips the board your
way, so dress for the fight you want.

And on the last floor, where she sleeps, the heroes who got there first
keep watch and have only one rule: **no kissing!**

- **A fairy tale tower**, made fresh every game, floor by floor
- **The Game of Thorns**: every battle a quick match-the-colours puzzle,
  where what you carry decides which thorns grow
- **Gear with names**: sharp or blunt, small or tower, leather, iron or
  tempered, some with a power of their own
- **A kiss for everyone**: you may try to kiss any creature gently to wake
  it up. None of them is amused
- **And a lutefisk.** Eat it and you feel weird

## Controls

- **Arrows**, **numpad**, **1 to 9** or **vim keys** (hjkl yubn): move
- **Shift** (or **Ctrl** or **Alt**) with a direction: open doors, talk,
  attack
- **Space**: wait a turn
- **Enter**: pick up what lies here, take the stairs; in a fight, clear
  the run under the cursor
- **1** to **9**, **0** or **Escape**: answer when the game asks
- **R**: a new game, after dying
- **Ctrl+Q** or **Ctrl+C**: quit

## History

Ondřej Žára, the author of rot.js, made Sleeping Beauty in seven days for
the Seven Day Roguelike Challenge in March 2017, from the fairy tale
Charles Perrault wrote down, as its title page likes to remind you along
with a fresh fun fact every time ("This game can be won!", "This game can
be lost!"). His twist was to make every fight a puzzle on a board of
thorns. It now climbs its tower in the terminal, the game's own scripts
exactly as he wrote them.
