**TEN YEARS. ONE CITY. NOT ENOUGH GRAIN.**

You hold the seal of a city of a hundred souls, a thousand acres and a store of grain. Every year you decide three things: how much land to buy or sell, how much grain to hand out, and how many acres to sow. Every person eats twenty bushels a year and can work ten acres; every two acres take a bushel of seed.

Then the year happens. The harvest might be fat or thin. Rats get into the stores. Newcomers arrive when times are good, and plague can take half the city in a night. Let too many starve in one year and the people will drag you from the palace.

- **A report every year,** in plain words, of what the year brought.
- **The city by year:** people, grain and land charted across your reign.
- **Live arithmetic:** your orders show what they leave in store, and warn before anyone goes hungry.
- **The people's judgement** after ten years, from golden age to exile.

## Controls

- **Tab**, **← →** or a click: move between the orders
- **↑ ↓**, **PgUp PgDn** or the wheel: change a number; typing replaces it
- **F**: feed everyone; **P**: plant all you can
- **Enter**: end the year
- **M**: sound on or off; **Esc**: quit
