**FIVE IN A ROW. TWO AT A TIME.**

Five in a row, with one twist that changes everything: captures. Put a
stone at each end of two of your opponent's, and both come off the
board. Five in a line wins, and so does taking five pairs, so every
attack leaves a weakness and every pair you build can be taken.

Play the computer at any of six strengths, or a friend at the same
keyboard. The opening rule: the first stone goes in the centre, and the
first player's second stone at least three points away from it.

## Controls

- **I J K M**, or **P B F N**: move the cursor up, left, right and down
- **Space** or **Enter**: place a stone
- **U**: undo; **R**: restart; **L**: redraw; **Q**: quit
- At the start, for each side: **0** for a person, **1** to **6** for
  the computer
