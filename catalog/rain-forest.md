**YOU'VE BOOKED FIVE DAYS AT A CABIN IN THE FOREST.**

You arrive close to midnight, exhausted, looking forward to falling asleep to the sound of rain. Through the trees to the south you can see the lights of the cabin. It is raining, lightly for now. There is a forecast for the next five days, if you can find it.

Rain Forest is a chill turn-based game with no enemies and no combat. Your one meter is motivation, and the weather is against it: every step out in the rain, through flood water or in the dark wears it down, while sheltering under a tree, hopping from stone to stone across the water and making the most of your stay build it back up. Let it run out and you pack up and go home early. Keep it going for five days and the break is just what you needed.

- **Things to find in the forest:** an umbrella, a pair of gumboots for the flood water, a portable lantern, a crowbar to push rocks with, a shovel to dig ditches that drain the water away, a topographic map, and a weather report with the next days' rain
- **Small pleasures, once a day:** a cozy chair to sit in and enjoy the rain, a cup of tea if you can find tea leaves for the teapot, a flower for the long-abandoned altar in the ruins, a lake vast enough to put your life into perspective
- **A bed** at the cabin for eight hours of sleep, with the rain still falling outside
- **Weather that changes**, light to medium to heavy rain, hour by hour across five days

## Controls

- **Arrows**, **WASD** or **HJKL**: walk; **Shift** with WASD or HJKL: walk quickly
- **Space**: wait two minutes; **.**: wait an hour
- **g**: pick up or put down; **x**: examine
- **m**: map; **r**: weather report; **f**: lantern; **p**: push rocks; **e**: dig
- **Escape**: the menu, with save and quit

## Options

- `--rgb`, `--ansi`, `--greyscale`: for terminals without true colour

## History

Stephen Sherratt, who makes games as gridbugs, wrote Rain Forest in seven days for the 7DRL Challenge of March 2022: a seven-day roguelike with nothing to kill, made on chargrid, his own Rust library for games drawn in characters. Spend a few days in the forest, in the rain, says its page, and above all sit inside and enjoy the rain. It is part of a line of his seven-day roguelikes, one most years since 2018.
