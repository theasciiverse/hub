**SHUFFLE UP AND DEAL.**

Two cards face down, five to come in the middle, and a thousand chips in front of everyone. Blinds are 25 and 50. Check, call, raise to any amount the table can match, or push it all in, and the side pots sort themselves out at the showdown.

A match is ten hands. Whoever holds the most chips at the end takes it, so a bad beat in hand three is not the end of the world.

- **Up to eight computer players,** who weigh their cards against the price of staying in, and bluff just often enough to keep you guessing.
- **No-limit, with real side pots,** raises to any amount, and the result of every hand on the table.
- **Two to nine seats,** six to start.

Hold'em is one of the five tables of Pieczasz's tty.cards, here on its own.

**ALL IN?**

## Controls

- **C**: check or call
- **R**: raise (**Left** / **Right** to choose the amount, **[** / **]** to fine-tune, **Enter** to bet)
- **A**: all in
- **F**: fold
- **Enter**: deal the next hand, when it's yours to deal
- **Esc**: leave (forfeits your chips)
- **Left** / **Right** on the setup screen: how many players; **Enter**: deal; **Q**: quit
