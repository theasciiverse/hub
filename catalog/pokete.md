**YOUR ADVENTURE STARTS AT HOME.**

You wake up in Nice Town with one Pokete, a little stone creature called Steini, fifteen Poketeballs and a healing potion. Out there are tall grass, dark caves, deep forests and a whole region of towns, each with trainers waiting to battle you. Walk into the grass and something will jump out.

Battle wild Poketes, weaken them and throw a Poketeball to make them yours. Pick the six who fight for you, level them up and watch them **evolve**. Every Pokete has its own type, nature and attacks, and picking the right one for the fight is half the game.

- **Nearly sixty Poketes to catch,** each with its own art, stats and moves, and your Pokedex to fill.
- **A whole world to explore:** towns with a Pokete Center to heal at and a shop to stock up in, routes, caves, and trainers who won't let you pass without a fight.
- **Achievements, evolutions, natures and shiny Poketes** for those who want to catch them all.
- **Music and sound** from Eric Skiff's *Resistor Anthems* and xDeviruchi's 8-bit fantasy pack.
- **Saves as you go,** and your Poketes are waiting for you next time.

Can you become the best trainer in the land?

## Controls

- **W A S D**: walk
- **Enter**: talk, confirm; **Esc**: back
- **1**: your deck, **2**: quit, **3**: the map, **4**: inventory, **5**: Pokedex, **6**: clock
- **In battle**: **1** attack, **2** run, **3** inventory, **4** switch Pokete
- **?**: help, with every key; the keys can be changed in the menu

## Options

- `--no_audio`: no music or sound
- `--log`: write a log beside your save
- `--no_mods`: start without mods
- `--save_dir DIR`: keep saves somewhere else
