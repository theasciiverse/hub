**INTO A PROFOUND SLUMBER SHE SANK,** surrounded only by dense brambles,
thorns and roses. Many adventurers tried to find and rescue her, but none
came back.

It is a truly beautiful day for a heroic action. The tower stands among
plains and trees, and there might be a princess sleeping on its last
floor; the only way to her is up through every floor. Open the doors,
pick up potions, swords and shields, talk to the other heroes who had the
same idea, and climb.

The tower's inhabitants are not all friendly, and every fight is played as
the **Game of Thorns**: a square board of coloured thorns, where you match
runs of the same colour. Green runs are your attacks, blue ones your
spells; red and yellow are your enemy's, and they strike when you clear
them too. What you carry changes how often each colour comes up. Pick your
runs well, keep an eye on your health and mana, and reach the last floor.

## Controls

- **Arrows**, **numpad**, **1 to 9** or **vim keys** (hjkl yubn): move
- **Shift** (or **Ctrl** or **Alt**) with a direction: open doors, talk,
  attack
- **Space**: wait a turn
- **Enter**: pick up what lies here, take the stairs; in a fight, clear
  the run under the cursor
- **1** to **9**, **0** or **Escape**: answer when the game asks
- **R**: a new game, after dying
- **Ctrl+Q** or **Ctrl+C**: quit
