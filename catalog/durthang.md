A terminal MUD client: keep your servers and characters in one list, pick
one, and you are in. Durthang keeps
passwords in your system's keyring (never in its config file), and has
aliases, triggers, an automap panel and notes per character.

## Controls

- **Arrow keys**: move through servers and characters
- **Space**: expand or collapse a server
- **N**: add a server; **n**: add a character
- **Enter**: connect with the selected character
- **Ctrl+Q**: disconnect, back to the list
- **q**: quit
