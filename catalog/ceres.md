**3000 METRES. ONE GIRL. ONE DASH.**

At the foot of Ceres Mountain stands a stone. *This memorial to those perished on the climb*, it says. Ceres reads it, ties her boots a little tighter, and starts up anyway.

Thirty-one screens of ice, spikes and howling wind stand between her and the summit, a hundred metres at a time. Jump! Dash through the air in any of eight directions! Cling to the rock and kick off again! When her hair turns red, the dash is spent: land, catch your breath, and go again.

Bounce off springs. Snatch a balloon for a fresh dash in midair. Race across snow that crumbles under your feet, and ride the moving platforms over the spikes. Hunt down the cherries hidden off the beaten path, though some of them flap away the moment you dash. Find the key, open the chest. And somewhere up there, past the Old Site, a golden sun-seed is waiting to give you a second dash...

You will fall. A lot. Every fall puts you straight back at the start of the screen to try again, and when you plant the flag at the summit, the mountain tells you exactly how many times.

- **Super-tight platforming, true to the frame:** 31 levels from 100 m to the summit, and every jump, dash and wall-kick plays exactly as in the original PICO-8 classic, checked frame by frame against it.
- **All-new art and an all-new soundtrack.**
- **Keyboard or gamepad,** on a terminal that reports key releases (kitty, foot, WezTerm, Ghostty, Alacritty, iTerm2 and others).

The summit is waiting. **HOW MANY TIMES WILL YOU FALL?**

## The original

In 2015, Maddy Thorson and Noel Berry made a hardcore little platformer for the PICO-8 fantasy console in just four days. They filled the cart almost to the last byte: 8186 of 8192 code tokens, the entire sprite sheet, the entire map and 63 of 64 sounds. That game was Celeste Classic, and it grew into the acclaimed Celeste (2018). Ceres restores it for the terminal, their code and their levels, with new art and new music.

## Controls

- **Arrow keys** or **W A S D**: move
- **Z**, **C**, **N** or **Space**: jump
- **X**, **V** or **M**: dash, in any of eight directions
- **Esc** or **P**: menu, **R**: restart, **Q**: quit
- **K**: screen shake on or off, **A**: square pixels or sharp ones, **F8**: sound on or off
- **Gamepad**: stick or d-pad moves, bottom or top button jumps, the other face buttons or the triggers dash, Start opens the menu

## Options

- `--level N`: start at level N (1 to 31; 31 is the summit)
- `--no-shake`: no screen shake
- `--sharp`: exact 1:1 pixels
- `--mute`: no sound
