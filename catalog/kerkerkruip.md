**YOU SAID YOU COULD TAKE HIM. NOW PROVE IT.**

When you claimed you were tough enough to take on Malygris
single-handedly, everyone knew you had been drinking much more than was
good for you. And the prince is not one to let an opportunity pass.
Before you could so much as protest, his court mage opened a portal and
you were shoved through. Predictably, the portal immediately
disappeared.

So here you are in the dungeon of Kerkerkruip, with a weapon or two,
three packages of ment (a powerful and highly addictive drug) and a
boast to make good on: kill Malygris, the Wizard of Kerkerkruip!

The dungeon is built new every game ("Placing epic treasures (well, you
hope)..."). Kill a monster with a level and you absorb its soul: it
heals you, makes you stronger and grants you its power. But a new soul
drives out every soul of the same level or lower, so the order you kill
in matters. Fights are tactical, with attacks, parries, dodges, blocks
and rolls, and a tension that rises the longer a fight drags on.

- **Permanent death**: no undo ("it would take all tension out of the
  game"); the game saves itself every turn.
- **A coffee-break roguelike**: short games, in Victor Gijsbers' own
  words.
- **Temples and gods**: sacrifice your powers for a god's favour.

**Absorb their souls. Find the wizard. Keep your boast.**

## How to play

Type what you want to do, in plain words, and press Enter:

- **look**, **inventory** (or **i**), **examine** something (or **x**)
- **north**, **south**, **east**, **west**, **up**, **down** (or **n**, **s**, **e**, **w**, **u**, **d**)
- **take**, **drop**, **open**, **push** things; **help** or **about** in most games
- the game saves itself every turn and picks up where you left off; **quit** to stop

Your game in progress is kept in `~/.local/share/kerkerkruip`.
