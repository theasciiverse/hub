**2048. THEY TOLD US AN AMERICAN WAS DEAD. SHE WAS. THEY LIED ABOUT WHO KILLED HER.**

Dr. Ruth Calloway dug beneath Golgotha, the place of the skull, and found
one: human in shape, three times too large. Then she was murdered, Iraq
was blamed, and America went in. Commander John Fisher leads the
incursion. At every site on his orders his column finds her trenches, and
what she found in them.

You drive the supertank yourself: shell towers from across the water,
lock the turret on what moves, rearm at your HQ, and earn upgrades for
your next tank with every kill. Behind you the garage builds the army
and sends it down the roads to the enemy: peon tanks, rocket tanks,
electric cars, trikes, engineers that take banks and towers, bridgers
that lay themselves across a gap. Junctions decide where they go, and you
switch them. Banks fill crates of money, and planes fly them home.

**Act I, The Incursion**, seven missions through Iraq:

- **Al Basrah:** the beachhead, the supertank alone against the road north
- **Shatt al-Arab:** marsh channels, every bridge blown; bridgers first
- **Ur:** hold Calloway's first dig, under the ziggurat
- **Nakhayb:** her abandoned camp between the mesas; whoever's engineer
  gets there first takes it
- **Babylon:** the bones in the open, ringed by electric towers
- **The Euphrates line:** six trucks, two roads, a night of changing orders
- **Nineveh:** Fisher refuses to raze the site, and both armies turn on him

**Act II, The Desertion**, seven more, through the Levant and Egypt:

- **Palmyra:** five minutes of rearguard among the columns
- **The King's Highway:** trucks south past Kerak, the canyon of Wadi Mujib
- **Sinai:** four thousand in the chest; banks are taken, not owned
- **The Suez Canal:** bridgers under fire, trucks waiting at the gaps
- **Cairo:** street war in the walled town, and the first helicopters
- **Giza:** under the pyramids a third army shows itself, at war with all
- **Alexandria:** board the ships for Greece while the jets come in

Golgotha's own levels are on the title menu too: a battle on the Greek
coast, a skirmish in a Swiss valley, a walled town in Egypt. Play from
above, from the strategy map with its road network, or from behind the
supertank in the follow view, with the original's music and radio voices.

## Controls

- **W**/**S** gears, **A**/**D** turn, **Q**/**E** turret, **T** turret
  lock
- **Space** main gun, **F** machine gun, **Enter** rockets; or the mouse:
  the turret follows the pointer, the left button fires
- **1** to **8** build at the garage, **V** a bridger, **9** **0** **B**
  aircraft
- **J** switches the nearest junction (or click it); click or drag to
  select units, right-click to send them; **G** rally, **X** hold, **R**
  back to the road
- **Tab** map, **M** follow view, **+**/**-** zoom, **F1** all the keys
- A gamepad plays it too: left stick drives, right stick aims, triggers
  fire

## Options

- `calvary --no-intro`: straight to the title
- `calvary --edit maps/x.cmap`: the map editor
- `calvary --mute`: no sound

## History

Crack dot Com, the studio of Abuse, was making Golgotha, an arcade tank
game fused with real-time strategy, when it closed in 1998. It released
the source, graphics, music and levels so the work would not disappear,
and Patrick Grawehr's Golgotha Forever kept the code alive. Calvary
builds the game in the terminal from that source, its balance table, its
levels, music and sounds, and finishes the story its team had begun.
