**FERTILIZE THAT EGG, SOLDIER!**

You are one cell of a whole army of them, a little `@` with a tail, and
somewhere at the end of a long, winding run of tunnels through living
tissue waits the egg. Your comrades fall along the way; you pass their
bodies as you go.

Every move costs energy, and the tunnels are not empty. Platelets drift
in the way and can be pushed aside. Red blood cells give your strength
back. Bacteria close in on your tail and bite: the deeper you get, the
nastier they are. Candida grows out of the walls in sudden lines and
blocks the passage. You can bite back, and as your energy drains
your tail grows shorter. Run out of energy and the mission is over.

The map is made of hexagons: six directions, not eight, and the tunnels
twist in all of them.

## Controls

- **y u h l b n** (or **k** and **j** for up-left and down-right): move in
  the six hex directions; the arrows and the numpad (7 9 4 6 1 3, 8 and 2)
  work too, as do the digits 1 to 9
- Move into a bacterium to bite it, into a platelet to push it
- **.** or **5**: wait a turn
- **R**: a new game, once this one is over
- **Ctrl+Q** or **Ctrl+C**: quit
