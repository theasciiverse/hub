**4:17 AM. SIX MINUTES. ONE BOMB.**

"You're in. The plan now is simple: go to your lab, plant the bomb, and
run. The prototype will be destroyed. The military will have no way to
continue the experiment. No-one will die."

The guard is out securing the grounds. The building is empty, a darkened
foyer with a security desk, four CCTV screens and four buttons: 'basement',
'first', 'second' and 'alarm'. You have six minutes. Then time itself starts
to fold back on you, and every move you make has to fit with the moves you
have already made.

Be warned, in the author's own words: "it is more cruel than one might hope
for a modern piece of interactive fiction. In particular, any move you make
may put things into an unwinnable state." Save often. You will probably
start over more than once to find the best ending, and that is the fun of
it.

- **A tight clockwork puzzle**: one building, one night, six minutes
- **A familiar science-fiction idea**, explored "in what I hope is a
  rather refreshing and satisfying manner"
- **An IFComp 2004 game**, given to the public domain by its author

## How to play

Type what you want to do, in plain words, and press Enter:

- **look**, **inventory** (or **i**), **examine** something (or **x**)
- **north**, **south**, **east**, **west**, **up**, **down** (or **n**, **s**, **e**, **w**, **u**, **d**)
- **take**, **drop**, **open**, **push** things; **help** or **about** in most games
- **save** and **restore**; **quit** to stop

Your saved games are kept in `~/.local/share/all-things-devours`.
