**PILOT. MERCENARY. HERO, IF YOU LIVE THAT LONG.**

You start with a pilot, a little money and a home in the space colonies, where spinning habitats each have their own laws, factions and feuds. Talk to people, take jobs at the clubhouse, buy a mecha and go out to fight in it. Every character gets a story of their own, built by the game from your choices and the people you meet, so no two campaigns run the same way.

GearHead 2 is the sequel to GearHead: Arena, and the more ambitious game: more mecha, more factions, a world map to cross between cities, romance, lancemates who fight beside you, and quests that grow into a campaign-long plot.

- **Mecha by the dozen**: buy, repair and customise giant robots, part by part
- **On foot or in the cockpit**: fight hand to hand in town or in mecha out in the field
- **A story every time**: plots and quests generated for your character
- **Deep characters**: stats, skills, talents, relationships and reputations

## Controls

- **Arrow keys** or **1 to 9** on the number keys: move, diagonals with **Home**, **PgUp**, **End**, **PgDn** or **7 9 1 3**
- **t**: talk; **a**: attack; **A**: the attack menu; **l**: look
- **i**: inventory; **e**: equipment; **,**: pick up
- **u**: use something nearby; **s**: use a skill; **S**: search
- **>** or **<**: go through an entrance
- **M**: choose your mecha; **H**: field HQ; **C**: character info
- **m**: memos, email and news; **V**: past messages; **Z**: rest an hour
- **h**: help; **X**: save; **Q**: save and return to the title menu

## Options

- `SCREENWIDTH` and `SCREENHEIGHT` in `~/.config/gearhead2/gearhead2.cfg`: a larger screen

## History

Joseph Hewitt released the first GearHead 2 in 2005, a few years after GearHead: Arena, and kept building it into the 2010s; 0.701 came out in 2017. It runs in a graphical window or as ASCII in a terminal. This edition is the ASCII version, with one fix so a first start always finds a place for its settings and saves.
