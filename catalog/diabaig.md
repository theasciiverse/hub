**A DRAGON SLEEPS THIRTY FLOORS DOWN. BRING BACK ONE OF ITS TEETH.**

Back when tigers used to smoke, the Great Halls of Diabaig were home to a thriving people, until a dragon moved in and the fires took nearly everything. Now the halls are a maze of rooms and dark passages, crowded with whatever crawled in after. You go down to the bottom, pull a tooth from the jaws of the beast, and then you have to climb every floor back to daylight with it.

Every run is a new dungeon and every death is final. You are never more than a few mistakes away from the gravestone: bats flutter out of the dark, goblins swing clubs in doorways, and deeper down wait vampires, trolls, harpies, hydras and things far older. Potions and scrolls come unlabelled, so you drink, read, throw and learn. Coat a dagger in an unknown potion and strike three foes with it. Set a room on fire and watch flame ignite miasma, mist smother flame and smoke choke them all.

- **Five ways to start**: Rogue, Warrior, Monk, Wizard, or Stark Raving Mad
- **Spells that grow** stronger the more you cast them: fire bolts, blizzards, grappling vines, hypnosis, arcane barriers
- **Strangers in the deep**: a hooded trader with a bag of trinkets, and a soul guide who offers a second life at a price
- **Auto explore and click to walk**, so the journey moves fast until something moves back
- **Save slots** to stop and pick up where you left off

## Controls

- **Arrow keys**, **numpad** or **h j k l** (diagonals **y u b n**): move and attack
- **Shift** or **Space** with a direction: run
- **o**: explore automatically; **z**: rest until something happens
- **e** eat, **d** drink, **r** read, **t** throw, **f** fire your bow, **a** apply a potion to your weapon
- **w** wield or wear, **W** swap weapons, **D** drop, **,** pick up, **s** search
- **1**, **2**, **3**: cast the spell in that slot
- **i** inventory, **>** and **<** stairs, **?** the full help
- **S** save, **Q** quit without saving

## Options

- `-s <seed>`: play a dungeon from a chosen seed
- `-v`: write a log of the session beside the saves

## History

conornally built Diabaig in C with ncurses, the way the old roguelikes were made, and shared it on itch.io as it grew, release by release. In September 2025 the dragon's halls reached version 1.0.
