Four lifeboats from a crippled interstellar liner are adrift in a
starfield of 20 by 9 squares. To find them, drop probes: a probe looks in
all eight compass directions and tells you in how many of them it sees a
lifeboat (only the nearest boat in each direction counts). Drop a probe
right on a lifeboat and it is found. Find all four in as few tries as
you can, before the passengers run out of oxygen.

The rays from the cursor are highlighted, so you can see what a probe
would look along. Mark the squares you have ruled out; the game marks
the obvious ones for you as the boats turn up.

## Controls

- **Arrow keys**, **h j k l y u b n** or the **number pad**: move (the
  field wraps round)
- **p**: drop a probe, or ask an old probe again
- **Space**: mark a square as empty, or unmark it
- **o**: turn the ray highlighting off or on
- **Mouse**: click to mark, double-click to drop a probe
- **?**: the rules; **q**: give up and see where the boats were
