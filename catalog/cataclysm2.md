**SHIPWRECKED, FIFTY YEARS AFTER THE END OF THE WORLD.**

You were born after the cataclysm, in a coastal farming and fishing town
that kept its walls up and its people mostly safe. Then the walls broke.
You grabbed what you could, ran for the docks and put out to sea, the last
safe way out. Days adrift, then a storm. Now you wake on the beach of an
island, beside the wreck of your boat, with some of your supplies
scattered around you.

Find water, food and somewhere to sleep before night. Inland lie fields,
forests, swamps and salt marsh, and towns: the towns hold what you need,
and the zombies. Out in the wild there are mutant rabbits, demented dogs
and giant crabs, and worse in the places the island keeps to itself.

The radio from the wreck still works. On it, a relay on the mainland calls
boats home, and a recorded voice from a lab at the island's heart asks for
the power to come back on. There are two ways to end this:

- **Sail home.** Patch the wreck's hull, rig a mast and sail, find a GPS
  unit to follow the relay, stock the boat and go.
- **Close the seam.** Bring the island's power plant back to life, pass the
  power through to the Lab, find a passcard, and shut down the core that
  everything on the island came through.

- **Whales' own sequel** to Cataclysm, built around a game you can win
- **A whole island**, with towns, roads, z-levels you can see between, and
  monsters that live where their kind lives
- **Hunger, thirst and sleep** that press on you, slow healing, and death
  that is permanent
- **Make a survivor** from stats, traits and a profession (Survivalist,
  Medic, Farmer, Mechanic, Hunter...)
- **Saved as you go**, and carried on with **L** from the first screen

## Controls

- **hjkl yubn** or the numpad: move, the last four diagonally; walk into a
  monster to attack it, into a door to open it
- **,**: pick up, **d**: drop, **i**: inventory
- **w**: wield, **W**: wear, **T**: take off, **e**: eat, **R**: read,
  **a**: apply (the radio, the GPS unit, a flashlight)
- **t**: throw, **f**: fire, **r**: reload
- **o** and **C**: open and close, **s**: smash, **x**: examine (the wreck,
  panels, doors), **;**: look
- `<` and `>`: stairs up and down
- **$**: sleep
- **:** or **M**: the island map (**q** leaves it)
- **@**: your character, **K**: skills
- **?**: help, **S**: save, **Q**: save and quit

## History

Whales wrote Cataclysm, a post-apocalyptic roguelike, from 2010 to 2013;
its players carried it on as Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead, the big
community fork that is still developed today. Cataclysm II is a different
project: Whales' own start over, with new code, its own interface layer
and data files, set fifty years after the first game, and planned from the
start as a game with an ending: survive the island, deal with the factions
fighting over the Lab at its centre, or leave it by sea. Whales stopped in
2016 with the island built and the ending still on paper. The Asciiverse
carries it on from those notes.
