**YOU SLIP PAST THE CORDON IN THE EARLY HOURS OF THE MORNING.**

Somewhere deep in the zone, they say, there is a room that will grant any wish. It is two thousand kilometres down the road. The road is closed. You are going anyway, in an old car with a full tank, a little food, and nothing in your hands.

Most of the trip you drive, watching the hours and the kilometres roll by. Then you have to stop, because the fuel is low, or the food is gone, or night is coming. So you pull over and get out: at an old fire trail through a pine plantation gone wild, at a swamp where the road bridges flooded woodland past ruined cabins, at a mountain pass between the cliffs. You have until the things that live there notice you.

Out of the car, everything runs down. Your health, your energy, your food. Swinging an axe tires you, heavy armour makes you hungry, and coffee and fruit keep you on your feet. You fill the boot with whatever will help: fuel cans, firewood, a knife or an axe, overalls or armour. And you get back in before it's too late, because the zone has its residents. Zombies that won't stay dead. Slimes that split when you hit them. Climbers that come over anything. Drainers that sap your strength.

And after sunset, the night stalkers. They can climb, they move in the dark, and they are afraid of exactly one thing. So when the light goes, you build a fire, and you sleep beside it, and you hope there is enough wood to last until morning.

Make it to the end of the road, and you write down your wish: "I wish..." The game keeps every wish you have ever made.

## Controls

- **Arrows**: walk, and walk into enemies to fight
- Walk into the side of the car to drive; into its back for the boot
- **g**: get; **i**: inventory; **a**: apply; **d**: drop
- **u**: unequip the weapon; **r**: remove armour
- **m**: message log; **?**: help; **Escape**: the menu, with save and quit

## Options

- `--rgb`, `--ansi`, `--greyscale`: for terminals without true colour

## History

Road Closed is the newest of Stephen Sherratt's seven-day games, made for the 7DRL Challenge of March 2026. It grew out of two inspirations: the Strugatsky brothers' novel Roadside Picnic, with its forbidden Zone and its wish-granting heart, and Pacific Drive, the game about keeping a car running through an exclusion zone. Put them together in characters, give them seven days, and this is the road you get.
