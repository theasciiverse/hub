**WARNING: You have violated Klingon space; prepare to die.**

Four empires share one universe, and none of them is sharing nicely. Fly for the Federation out of Earth, the Romulans out of Romulus, the Klingons out of Klingus or the Orions out of Orion, and everything between your sun and theirs is up for grabs: Altair, Janus, Telos, Sherman, Jinx, and a dead world called Hell. Conquest is played in real time, everybody at once. Lead your targets with photon torpedoes, burn them with phasers at close range, and watch your shields, your fuel and your weapons temperature, because an overheated bank leaves you a sitting duck.

Kills are only half the war. Orbit an enemy planet, bombard it until its armies are gone, then beam your own troops down and it is yours. Take every core planet and your team conquers the universe: your name goes up in lights, your last words go out to every captain in the game, and a new universe begins.

You are never alone out there. The robots fly for every team nobody is playing: M-5 for the Federation, Colossus for the Romulans, Guardian for the Klingons and HAL 9000 for the Orions, and they talk back if you message them. Somewhere a doomsday machine is eating planets.

- **Play here**: a universe of your own, with the robots for company
- **Host**: open it to your local network; friends see your address and join
- **Join**: type a friend's address and fly in their universe
- **Twenty ships, three hulls**: Scout, Destroyer and Cruiser, and you can refit once you have a kill

## Controls

- **k**: set a course (a planet name, an angle, or q w e a d z x c); **arrow keys** turn too
- **0** to **9**, **=**: warp factor
- **p**: torpedo, **P**: a burst of three, **f**: phasers, **F**: phasers again
- **+** and **-**: shields, **R**: repair, **o**: orbit
- **B**: bombard the planet you orbit, **b**: beam armies up or down
- **i**: information, **M**: long range map, **?**: planet list, **/**: ship list
- **m**: send a message, **W**: war and peace
- **Q**: self-destruct, the honourable way out; **h**: every other command

## History

Jef Poskanzer and Craig Leres wrote Conquest in Ratfor for VAX/VMS in 1983, and it ran wild through university terminal labs; years later Michael Erskine put it this way: "We were playing xtrek before there was X." Jon Trulson was one of those students. In the early to mid 1990s, with a multi-user Unixware system at home, he ported it to C, adding colour, keypad steering and macros while keeping the original's mechanics and balance. He split it into a server and clients that meet over the network, and he still works on it today. When version 9 went to OpenGL, the terminal client was retired. This edition brings it back, for today's servers.
