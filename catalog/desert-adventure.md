**HOT. DRY. AND SOMEWHERE OUT HERE, TREASURE.**

"I'm in a hot and dry desert." There is an oasis to the north, with palm
trees, a pool of cool water, a flowering plant, and a sign that says
*leave treasures here and SCORE*. Further out are caves, and things that
want finding.

Desert Adventure is a small game in the style of the first home-computer
adventures, the ones Scott Adams wrote for computers with a few kilobytes
of memory: the room is drawn at the top of the screen, and you tell the
game what to do in two words. Find the treasures, bring them back to the
oasis, and see your score.

## How to play

Type two words and press Enter:

- **GO NORTH** (or **N**, **S**, **E**, **W**, **U**, **D**), **LOOK**, **INVENTORY**
- **GET** and **DROP** things, and try other verbs on what you find;
  **VERBS** lists the ones the game knows
- **SCORE** to see how you are doing; **COPY** for the copyright
- **SAVE GAME** to save; **QUIT GAME** to stop

When you start the game, it lists your saved games and asks which one to
pick up again; press Enter for a new game. They are kept in
`~/.local/share/desert-adventure`.
