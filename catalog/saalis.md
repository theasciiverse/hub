**HIGH ABOVE THE BELT, NOTHING IS FREE.**

The Northern Reach: the cold, thin fringe of the asteroid belt, where the
rocks ride high above the ecliptic and the patrols run out. You fly out of
Routa Station in a light courier-fighter, with two thousand credits and an
empty hold. Everything else, you take.

Haul ice from the mines to the farms and ore to Halla Refinery. Buy where
it's cheap, sell where it's scarce, and watch the prices move with every
load. Take a cargo run from the Free Traders' Guild, a bounty from the
Reach Watch, or a fixer's job in a bar that pays double for not asking
questions. Run stims past the customs scanners in a compartment they mostly
can't see.

And keep your guns warm. Claim-jumpers work the lanes, worst in Revontuli
Gap where no station watches, and their fleets are taking the Reach region
by region. Burn them down in real-time dogfights, or knock one out, clamp
on, and fight its crew room by room for what's in the hold.

Then, in Routa Station's bar, a prospector called Ilta Rask is looking for
a pilot. Jumpers have cut the beacon off her claim in Kide Drift, and they
went straight for her survey log. What she finds forty metres down in the
ice has been waiting two hundred years for someone to carry it. The
Cooperative wants it. The Crown will pay a fortune for it. Customs wants it
asleep. And it has a wish of its own. Who gets it is up to you, and the
Reach will never be the same.

- **Real-time dogfights** with four-way shields, armour that only a dock
  can patch, seeking missiles and ECM
- **Twelve belt regions** joined by drive lanes, from the greenhouses of
  Lumi Field to the wreck field of Kalmisto, and a war that keeps moving
  while you fly
- **Trade, smuggle, take contracts**: ten goods and two kinds of
  contraband, guild boards, fixers, deadlines
- **Four hulls to own** and a shipyard full of plating, plasma guns,
  turrets and hidden compartments
- **Five factions** that remember what you did: patrols that escort friends
  and shoot enemies, pirates who can be paid off
- **On foot**: walk every base in classic ASCII, from the hangar to the bar
- **A story with four endings**, and side stories across the Reach: a
  failing air plant, a lost wingman's flight recorder, a smuggler's
  manifest, a run of medicine past three factions' scanners
- **Quests written in Lua**: write your own and the game loads them from
  its save folder

The catch is out there. **WILL YOU TAKE IT, OR BE IT?**

## Controls

- **Arrows** or **W A S D**: turn and throttle; **Home** full speed, **End**
  stop, **M** match the target's speed
- **Space** (hold): guns; **F**: a missile (it seeks when LOCK shows);
  **Tab** (hold): afterburner
- **T**: next target; **R**: the nearest hostile; **C**: hail
- **N**: the nav map (Tab in it: the whole Reach); **[ ]**: nav points;
  **G**: autopilot; **J**: jump through a lane; **L**: land
- Docked, on foot: **arrows**, **W A S D** or **h j k l** walk; walk into a
  counter or a person to use them; **Enter** talks
- Aboard a boarded ship: **F** fires, **M** uses a medkit
- **P**: pause; **F1**: keys; **F8**: sound; **Esc**: quit
- **Gamepad**: stick to fly, A fires, B burns, the bumpers pick targets

Terminals with the kitty keyboard protocol (kitty, foot, WezTerm, Ghostty)
give exact key presses and releases.

## Options

- `--play`: straight into a new game
- `--board`: a new game, clamped onto a disabled raider
- `--demo`: the game plays itself; any key quits
- `--seed N`: the same Reach every time
- `--mute`: no sound

## History

Saalis is Finnish for the catch: the prey, the loot, the plunder, one word
for both halves of the game, the trader's haul and the raider's take. It
was made in 2026 out of love for Ascii Sector and the space trading games
before it, the kind of game where a cockpit, a cargo hold and a bar full of
shady offers are a whole life. Rather than borrow anyone's galaxy, it built
its own: a belt where the drive lanes were grown two centuries ago from
seeds with minds of their own, until one of them grew a lane nobody had
ordered, and a short war ended with every seed planted or burned. Every
seed but one. In a single week it went from its first flight to the whole
story told to its end.
