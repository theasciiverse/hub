**REAL ROCKETS. REAL ORBITS. ONE TERMINAL.**

Kerbal Space Program, in your terminal. Our own solar system at full
scale: low Earth orbit takes about 9.4 km/s, the Moon's orbit is tilted,
and the rockets on the pad are the real ones (Saturn V, SLS, Falcon 9)
with their real thrust and fuel.

Fly a launch off the pad by hand in the chase cam, drop your spent stages,
and watch the projected high point climb until ORBIT READY. Then plant a
burn and go: target the Moon, press **H**, and a transfer appears with
the orbit it will leave you in drawn on the map.

- **Five star systems**: Sol at full scale, Lumen at a tenth (the home
  planet Kern with its moons Cursor and Glyph, and the red planet Rust),
  Alpha Centauri, Kepler-452 and TRAPPIST-1.
- **Plan like a flight director**: burn nodes, one-key Hohmann transfers,
  Lambert and porkchop solvers for the planets.
- **Rendezvous and dock** port to port, with a true-scale Proximity view.
- **Build your own** in the Vehicle Assembly Building, with live
  delta-v, TWR and mass.
- **Flight School** walks you from the camera to orbit to your first
  docking; a Challenge ladder waits after it.
- **Play together**: host a session from your own game and friends on
  your local network join from their terminals, each with their own space
  program and their own clock.

## Controls

- **F1**: every key, in the game
- **t** / **T**: set / clear the target; **H**: plan a transfer to it;
  **G**: warp to the next burn
- **z** / **x**: full throttle / cut; **b**: light the engine;
  **Space**: drop a stage
- **W**: point along your ground speed (the gravity turn);
  **→** / **←**: tip the nose toward / back from your heading
- **.** / **,**: warp faster / slower; **+** / **-**: zoom; **V**: launch
  view; **F2**: hide the instruments
- **M**: missions; **n**: new vessel; **Tab**: next star system
- **F5** / **F9**: quicksave / quickload; **Esc**: menu;
  **Ctrl+C**: quit

## Options

- `--launch-site KSC --loadout Saturn-V`: start on the pad
- `--orbit moon --altitude 100km`: start around the Moon
- `--system Lumen`: start in the Lumen system
- `--serve`: host a session for your local network
