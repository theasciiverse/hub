**TOY TANKS. REAL RICOCHETS.**

Twenty missions. One little tank. And a whole toy box of enemy tanks that want you scorched off the map!

Every shell you fire bounces off the walls, and so does every shell they fire. Bank a shot round a corner! Shoot their shells clean out of the air! Drop a mine behind a crate and back away very, very slowly. You get five shells and two mines at a time, and one hit is all it takes. For them, and for you.

Meet the opposition:

- **Tinpot** can't move. It just sweeps its turret, waiting for the bounce.
- **Plodder** wanders about. Thump. Thump. Thump.
- **Hornet** hangs back and fires rockets twice as fast as your shells.
- **Skitter** dashes about in a nervous panic, sowing mines as it goes.
- **Rascal** comes straight for you, three shells at a time.
- **Sniper** never moves, and never misses the angle you forgot.
- **Viper** banks its shots, dodges yours and lays the odd mine.
- **Ghost** is invisible. Its tread marks are not. And it hums.
- And **Onyx**? The big black one. It will not stop coming.

Listen closely! Every enemy has its own engine voice, and the battle march picks up a new instrument for every kind of enemy on the field: a woodblock for the Tinpot, a tuba for the Plodder, a kazoo for the Hornet, timpani for the Onyx. Hear a theremin but see nobody? That's a Ghost. Lose a tank and you get a sad trombone.

- **Twenty missions,** from First Shot to Last Stand, with a spare tank every five.
- **Two-player co-op** on the local network, one machine each. The spare tanks are shared, and friendly fire is very much on.
- **Keyboard and mouse, or a twin-stick gamepad.**

**GOOD LUCK, COMMANDER.**

## Controls

- **W A S D** or the **arrow keys**: drive
- **Mouse**: aim (or **I J K L** for eight ways, **Q** and **E** to turn slowly)
- **Left click** or **Space**: fire (five shells at most)
- **Right click** or **F**: drop a mine (two at most)
- **P** or **Esc**: pause, **H**: help
- **M**: music on or off, **F8**: all sound on or off
- **Gamepad**: left stick drives, right stick aims, right trigger fires, left trigger or A drops a mine

Panzer needs a truecolour terminal. 160x50 is playable, 200x60 or more is comfortable: the arena scales to fit.

## Options

- `--mission N`: straight into mission N
- `--host`, `--join`: co-op on the local network
- `--mute`: no sound
