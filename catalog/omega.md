**A WHOLE WORLD, NOT JUST A DUNGEON.**

Most roguelikes give you a staircase and a long way down. Omega gives
you a world. You begin at the gates of Rampart, a city with a Duke,
guilds, temples, a college of magic, banks, shops and a few places you
would do well to stay out of. Beyond its walls lies a countryside of
villages, wilderness, special sites and dungeons, each with a master at
the bottom.

There are many ways to win. Rise to the head of the Legion, the
Thieves' Guild, the Collegium Magii, the Order of Paladins, the Circle of
Sorcerors or a priesthood of one of the gods; grow rich; or go after the
total winner's crown, with the Oracle to point the way. The hall of fame
remembers who holds every title.

## Controls

- **h j k l y u b n**: walk (or fight), the keypad too
- **H J K L Y U B N**: run until something is worth a stop
- **i**: inventory; **g**: pick up; **t**: talk; **x**: examine
- **m**: cast a spell; **M**: go straight to a place you know in the city
- **<** and **>**: up and down; **.**: rest a turn
- **?**: help, with every command; **S**: save and quit; **Q**: quit
