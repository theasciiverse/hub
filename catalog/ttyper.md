**HOW FAST DO YOU REALLY TYPE?**

Fifty common words, one box to type them in. Each word lights up green or red as you go, and the moment you finish ttyper shows your words per minute, your accuracy, a chart of your speed through the test, and the keys you miss most.

Then press **r** for a fresh test, or **p** to practise the words you got wrong.

- **Many languages**: English (100, 200 or 1000 most common words, or advanced, or pirate), French, Galician, German, Korean, Norwegian, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Thai, Ukrainian
- **Or code**: C, C++, C#, Go, HTML, Java, JavaScript, PHP, Python, Ruby, Rust, SQL and Qt
- **Your own words**: any text file, line by line
- **Hard modes**: sudden death restarts on your first mistake; turn off backspace or going back

## Controls

- Type the words; **Space** moves to the next one, **Backspace** fixes the current one
- **Esc**: end the test
- On the results screen: **r** for a new test, **p** to practise your mistakes, **q** or **Esc** to quit

## Options

- `-w N`: number of words (default 50)
- `-l LANGUAGE`: word list, for example `-l english1000` or `-l rust`
- `--list-languages`: every list built in
- `--sudden-death`, `--no-backspace`, `--no-backtrack`: harder tests
- `ttyper FILE`: type the lines of a text file
