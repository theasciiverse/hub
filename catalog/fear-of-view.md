**WHAT YOU CANNOT SEE IS NOT THERE. IT IS BEING MADE AGAIN.**

Fear of View is played on a tiny board, five cells by five, walled in by box lines. Everything in your line of sight holds still. Everything behind you, behind a wall, round a corner, is thrown away and built afresh the moment you stop looking at it: new walls, new treasure, new monsters. Turn your back on a dead end and it may be a corridor when you look again. Turn your back on an empty room and something may be standing in it.

That is the whole fight. You have no sword. You win by managing what you see: keep a threat in view to know where it stands, look away to let the board forget it, and turn a blocked room into an open one by glancing elsewhere at the right moment. A wall you see is a wall you can trust; a wall you do not is a guess.

Each level hides a way out on its border, held shut by a seal. Find the exit and break the seal, and everything after gets harder, while the walls close in the longer a round goes on. Four kinds of monster hunt you, the calm one, the ghost, the clever one that keeps out of your sight, and the fast one, each with a stronger form. Gather treasure for score, gems that grant powers, and tools that bend vision your way:

- **A camera** you set down to keep one corner watched while you walk away
- **An orb** that rolls off and sees for you, an **umbrella** and a **tent** that put up walls of your own, a **flash** that lights every cell at once, a **balloon**, a **cloak**, **spray paint**
- **Eight powers** from gems: Heal, Dazzle, Smoke, Haste, Teleport, Ghost, Foresight, and Undo, which works even after death
- **Junk** to salvage into equipment: a bag, a charm, a hook, a key, a siphon

A run is a handful of tense minutes. Seven lives, rounds of lettered levels, a highscore table with the average of your last ten games beside it, and short hints that teach you as you go.

**Keep your eyes on what matters. Look away from what does not.**

## Controls

- **Arrow keys**, **WASD** or **HJKL**: move
- **1** to **8**, then a direction: use the item in that slot
- **0**: use your power
- **Space**: continue; **T**: turn the hints off
- **-**: plain ASCII instead of box lines
- **R**: recent scores in the highscore table
- **Q** or **Esc**: quit (the game is kept and resumes next time)

## Options

- `--dvorak`: Dvorak roguelike keys (htns)
- `--username NAME`: your name in the highscore table
- `--ascii`: start in plain ASCII

## History

Martin Bays, the maker of Intricacy, released Fear of View in August 2025 and reworked it a year later with four kinds of monster, each with a stronger form, a new model of vision and a fairer climb in difficulty. Players also reach it by ssh on his sshgames server.
