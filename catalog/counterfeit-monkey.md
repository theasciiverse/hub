**YOUR COVER IS BLOWN. GOOD LUCK GETTING OFF THE ISLAND.**

"Can you hear me?"

You wake in a back alley at noon, and you are two people. Alex and Andra have just been synthesised into one body, Alexandra, the most inconvenient disguise either of you has ever worn. You smuggle and spy for a living, the Bureau of Orthography has your faces on record, and a national holiday has the police spread thin for about eight more hours.

Welcome to Anglophone Atlantis, an independent nation since the April day in 1822 when "a well-aimed shot from their depluralizing cannon reduced the British colonizing fleet to one ship." Here, words are tools. Change a thing's name and you change the thing, and in your pocket is the tool to do it: a full-alphabet letter remover. Point it at something, pick a letter, take it out, and see what you're left holding.

- **A world built on wordplay**: every puzzle turns on letters and the words they make, across a whole town of streets, shops and museums, from Sigil Street to Ampersand Bend.
- **Two minds, one body**: your other half talks you through it, and your memories of how you got here come back as you go.
- **Gentle on newcomers**: a tutorial walks you through the first steps; type TUTORIAL OFF if you know your way.
- **Big and generous**: a full-length game by Emily Short, one of the great names in interactive fiction.

**Find a way off the island. Spell it out.**

## How to play

Type what you want to do, in plain words, and press Enter:

- **look**, **inventory** (or **i**), **examine** something (or **x**)
- **north**, **south**, **east**, **west**, **up**, **down** (or **n**, **s**, **e**, **w**, **u**, **d**)
- **take**, **drop**, **open**, **push** things; **help** or **about** in most games
- **save** and **restore**; **quit** to stop

Your saved games are kept in `~/.local/share/counterfeit-monkey`.
