**PROBE THE WORMHOLE. MIND THE STARS.**

Your ship, the **X**, sits in a field of stars. Somewhere out there is a wormhole, the **@**. Fly onto it, or line up and fire a probe into it, and you go up a level. But every wormhole you probe scatters a new batch of stars across the sky, and every star you fly into costs you power.

Nothing moves until you do, so take your time: find a clear line and let the probe launcher do the work. Probes fly straight through stars, along rows, columns and diagonals. Run out of power and you lose a life; lose three and it's GAME OVER. Reach level 30 and you are a true stardork.

- **Turn-based**: the stars wait for you
- **Eight-way flying and shooting**: rows, columns and diagonals
- **Harder every level**: each wormhole adds another batch of stars
- **A move counter**: win in as few moves as you can

## Controls

- **Arrow keys**, **Home**, **PgUp**, **End**, **PgDn**, or **1** to **9** on the number keys: move, diagonals included
- **a** / **f**: fire a probe left or right
- **s** / **d**: fire up or down
- **z** / **x**: fire up-left or up-right
- **c** / **v**: fire down-left or down-right
- Any key: carry on after hitting a star
- **q**, **Esc** or **Ctrl+C**: quit

## History

Ryan Kulla wrote StarDork as a small C and ncurses game and put version 0.7 on SourceForge in 2011. This edition ports it to Rust so it runs on Linux, macOS and Windows without ncurses, makes the number keys move as its README promised, and makes sure every wormhole can be reached. The original's beeps on the Linux console are gone.
