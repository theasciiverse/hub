**EIGHT BY EIGHT GLITTERING GEMS, AND ONE MORE MOVE IS NEVER ENOUGH.**

Seven kinds of gem glow on a dark board: red squares, blue and purple triangles, yellow diamonds, green pentagons, orange hexagons and white pearls. Swap two neighbours to line up three of a colour and they burst, the gems above tumble down, fresh ones rain in from the top, and if the fall lines up another row the cascade keeps rolling and the points keep climbing.

Line up four and you forge a **flame gem** that blows up everything around it when it goes. Five in a row makes a **hypercube**: swap it with any gem and every gem of that colour vanishes, and two hypercubes swapped together wipe the whole board. Catch an L or a T and you get a **star gem** that fires down its row and its column at once. Fill the bar along the bottom and the gems swirl away through a tunnel of rings and the next level begins.

- **Classic**: play until there are no moves left, then watch the board explode
- **Zen**: the board always finds you another move, so you can play as long as you like
- **A chiptune soundtrack** of about twenty minutes, with its own songs for the menu, Classic and Zen
- **Saves as you go**: leave in the middle of a game and pick up where you left off

## Controls

- **Arrow keys** or **h j k l**: move the cursor
- **Space** or **Enter**: SWAP mode, then an arrow swaps that way
- **Mouse**: click a gem and drag it onto a neighbour; click the buttons
- **Hint**: puts the cursor on a gem that can move
- **:** opens the command line: `:h` for a hint, `:p zen` or `:p classic` for a new game, `:m` for the menu, `:q` to save and quit, `:q!` to quit without saving

## Options

- `--mute`: play without the music

## History

pastthepixels began cmdjewel in Python at the end of 2023, then rebuilt it from the ground up in Rust in 2024. Its chiptune soundtrack is a tracker module that plays right inside the game.
