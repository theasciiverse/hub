**A DERELICT LINER, LOST FOR YEARS, HAS COME BACK. ITS VAULTS ARE STILL FULL. SO ARE ITS DECKS.**

A grand passenger cruiser, abandoned after an accident at the Solar System's outer frontier, has drifted back out of the dark near Neptune. Nobody ever emptied its deposit safes, its jewellery stores, its gilded robot holds, its arboretum or its whole deck of natural and enhanced animals. Your small crew of salvagers lands on the bridge to help themselves. Then the dead ship fires up its fusion engines, swings off Triton orbit and starts to climb out of Neptune's gravity well, with you on board.

Allure of the Stars is a turn-based science fiction roguelike played as a squad. You lead several explorers at once, switching between them with Tab, and every one of them counts. Send a single scout to slip past what the decks have become; bring the whole party together for an ambush at a doorway; hold one room while the first wave breaks against you. The ship is not empty. Self-repairing robots overrun with rogue code clone and rebuild themselves. Animals bred for the habitat deck have gone feral. Rival crews from the lawless frontier want the same loot and are not shy with nano, cyber and bio weapons. Everything hunts by sight, sound and smell, so you hide in shadow, light dark corners, distract and set up ambushes.

- **The long crawl**: deck after deck, some sealed at first, with food to gather and cook, gear to craft and walls to cut through
- **Short adventures** that teach you the squad one skill at a time, from a first tutorial to a frantic race to the exit and huge melee battles, and that tell how the crew came to be aboard
- **Difficulty levels and challenges**: cold fish, ready goods, lone wolf, finder keeper
- **Not a single image**: sixteen colours of text, and your imagination does the rest, like a good book

**Outsmart. Outlast. Get off the ship alive, and rich.**

## Controls

- **Arrow keys**, the **number pad** or **hjklyubn**: move, attack by moving into a foe
- **Tab**: switch to the next squad member
- **Space**: next message, or the full history
- **?**: help with every command; **Esc**: main menu, new game, save and exit

The game starts by playing itself until you press a key.

## History

Mikolaj Konarski began Allure of the Stars in 2011 as the science fiction game on his LambdaHack engine, a library for turn-based roguelikes written in Haskell. It has grown ever since into a squad tactics game with a long main crawl and a set of short adventures that flash back to how the crew came aboard. It also plays in a web browser at his site.
