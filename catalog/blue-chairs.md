A party at a house you have never been to, an attic stacked with junk and folding chairs painted carnival blue, and a guy with a cough syrup bottle full of something green: twenty-three dollars for the best dream of your life. What follows is a strange, sad and funny night that goes a long way from that attic.

## How to play

Type what you want to do, in plain words, and press Enter:

- **look**, **inventory** (or **i**), **examine** something (or **x**)
- **north**, **south**, **east**, **west**, **up**, **down** (or **n**, **s**, **e**, **w**, **u**, **d**)
- **take**, **drop**, **open**, **push** things; **help** or **about** in most games
- **save** and **restore**; **quit** to stop

Your saved games are kept in `~/.local/share/blue-chairs`.
