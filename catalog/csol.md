**ELEVEN WAYS TO LAY OUT A DECK.**

A whole shelf of patience games in one small program. Deal a Klondike,
then a FreeCell, then lose an evening to Spider: every game is played the
same way, with the arrow keys or the mouse, on proper drawn cards with
their suits in colour.

- **Klondike**, the one everyone knows, and **Klondike FreeCell**
- **FreeCell** and **Eight Off**, every card in view from the start and
  free cells to juggle with
- **Spider** with one, two or four suits
- **Yukon**, **Yukon FreeCell** and **Russian**, where whole stacks move at
  once
- **Golf**, up or down by one to clear the table

Undo and redo as far back as you like, a smart cursor that always lands on
a card, scores and times for every game, and a victory dance when the last
card goes home. Eight themes, from big framed cards to compact ones for a
small terminal, or plain ASCII.

**PICK A GAME. DEAL.**

## Controls

- **Arrows** or **HJKL**: move the cursor; with **Shift**, to the far end
  or the top or bottom of a pile
- **Space**: pick up the card (and those on it); again to send it home or
  to a free cell
- **Enter** or **M**: put it down on the pile under the cursor
- **Mouse**: left click picks up, right click puts down
- **A**: send a card to a foundation; **S** and **W**: deal from the stock,
  play from the waste; **1** to **9**: play from a free cell
- **U** or **Ctrl+Z**: undo; **Shift+U** or **Ctrl+Y**: redo
- **R**: deal again; **Q**: quit; **F10** or **Alt** with a letter: the
  menu bar, for games, themes and settings; **?**: how to play

## Options

- `csol freecell`, `csol spider2`, ...: start a game; `-l` lists them
- `-t compact`, `-t ascii`, ...: a theme; `-T` lists them
- `-S`: your stats for every game; `-S klondike`: every score in one
- `-s 12345`: deal from a fixed seed; `-m`: no colours
