**THE WORLD ENDED. THIS TIME YOU CAN END THE CATACLYSM.**

You made it to an evacuation shelter, and the shelter is all there is. Outside the door: the dead walking the streets, giant insects, killer robots, the fungus and the Mi-go, "things far stranger and deadlier", and the others like yourself, who want what you have. Scavenge the towns for food, water and gear. Learn by doing, craft almost anything, board up a house and wire it to an electric grid, fix up a car or a helicopter and get out.

Cataclysm: Bright Nights is the branch of the Cataclysm family with a way to win. Its developers took Dark Days Ahead in their own direction, reworking combat, bionics, mutations, explosives and much more, and put an ending in the world. Somewhere is the Central Lab, well guarded at the front, open to a careful survivor through an access shaft or a subway station, and at its bottom lies the way to stop it all.

And the world is worth the trip. Small New England towns give way to real cities with suburbs and a business district; airports keep full-size aircraft on the tarmac; alien outposts fight the dead in the streets. Up close, the fighting is yours to shape: choose your martial art technique, grab a smaller enemy and throw it, crash through a window to get away. And when you start a world you can switch on whole new layers of it, magic with Magical Nights, science fiction with Aftershock, dinosaurs with DinoMod and dozens more, or write your own in Lua.

## Controls

- `h` `j` `k` `l` `y` `u` `b` `n`, the arrow keys or the number pad: move (walk into a door to open it, into a monster to attack it)
- `.` or `5`: wait a turn; `<` and `>`: go up and down
- `g`: pick up; `e`: examine; `o` and `c`: open and close; `s`: smash
- `i`: inventory; `w`: wield; `W`: wear; `E`: eat or drink; `a`: use an item
- `f`: fire; `t`: throw; `G`: grab
- `&`: craft; `*`: construct; `$`: sleep
- `m`: the map; `x` or `;`: look around
- `?`: every key binding, which can be changed; `0`: help
- `S`: save and quit; `Esc`: game menu

## History

Whales began Cataclysm in 2010, and in 2013 its players carried it on as Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead, which grew under CleverRaven into one of the biggest open-source games there is. In 2020 Coolthulhu, long one of Dark Days Ahead's developers, restarted it as a fork of his own with a different idea of what the game should be, and that September it took the name Bright Nights. The victory condition came with its first stable release in October 2022, and the team that carries it now, scarf005, RobbieNeko, chaosvolt and Oren Audeles with hundreds of contributors, ships a new one every few months: redesigned cities, a rebuilt Central Lab and airports among the latest. Whales' original Cataclysm and Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead are in the catalog too.
