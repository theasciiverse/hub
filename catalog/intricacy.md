**EVERY LOCK IN THE CITY HAS A FLAW. FIND IT. THEN HIDE YOURS.**

By ruthlessly guarded secret arrangement, the council's agents can pick any
lock in the city. A secret guild makes the locks: apparently secure, each
with a fatal hidden flaw. You are a candidate for the guild.

A lock is a little machine on a hex grid: blocks held by springs, pivots
with arms, loose balls, and a bolt in the way. You work two tools at once,
a hook that turns and a wrench that keeps moving once it starts, and every
turn the springs push and pull back. Clear the bolt from its hole and the
lock opens.

- **Eleven tutorial locks** that teach the tools one idea at a time
- **An initiation** of eighteen locks laid out on a map, each one opening
  the way to its neighbours
- **A lock editor** to build your own, with springs, pivots and balls
- **The guild itself, online:** take a three-letter codename, put up to
  three locks of your own forward, pick other players' locks, and secure
  your notes on their solutions behind your own. Esteem is counted player
  against player; there is no ladder at the top, only rivals

**The bolt is in the way. Get it out.**

## Controls

- **h**, **l**, **y**, **u**, **b**, **n**: move the hook along the six hex
  directions; **j** and **k** turn it
- **H**, **L**, **Y**, **U**, **B**, **N**: move the wrench
- **x** switches tools, and **a**, **e**, **q**, **w**, **s**, **d** (or
  the number pad) move the one selected; **Space**: wait a turn
- **O**: open the lock once the bolt is clear
- **Backspace** or **X**: undo; **M**: mark a position, **'**: jump back
  to it
- The mouse drags tools too
- **?**: every key in the current screen; **Esc** or **Q**: back and quit

## Online play

The guild meets on Martin Bays' server, which the game connects to by
itself; register a codename there from the guild screen with **R**. The
tutorial, the initiation and the lock editor play offline.
