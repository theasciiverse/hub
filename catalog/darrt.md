Darts with the keyboard. The twenty-six letters of the alphabet fly
around the screen, bouncing off its edges, over a round target. Press a
letter's key and that letter keeps going for a moment, then stops where
it is and stays there. You have ten shots to land letters as near the
centre of the target as you can.

The nearer a letter lands, the more it scores, doubling with every step
toward the middle; a letter outside the target costs points, and one
exactly in the middle scores a million. Since the letters keep
moving for a while after you press, the skill is in judging where each
one is going. When the ten shots are used up, your score goes on a list
of the best eleven.

The game needs an 80 by 24 terminal.

## Controls

- **A** to **Z**: stop that letter
- **F1** or **?**: how to play
- **Esc**: end the round; at the end of a round, **n** quits and **y**
  plays again (pressing **Q** a second time in a round also ends it)
- **Ctrl-C**: quit at once

## History

Darrt is one of the games abakh wrote for New BSD Games, a collection of
small curses games started in 2018 and named after the classic BSD games.
The collection is packaged by Debian, Ubuntu, openSUSE, Alpine, Nix,
Homebrew, FreeBSD and pkgsrc, and is in the AUR.
