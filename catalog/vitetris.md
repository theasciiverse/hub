**SEVEN SHAPES. TEN COLUMNS. NO MERCY.**

The blocks fall, you turn them and slot them in, full rows vanish, and the speed creeps up. vitetris plays the way the early Nintendo versions did, with the same rotation, scoring, levels and speed, plus a short lock delay so the high levels stay playable.

Play a marathon for points, or a B-type game where you race to clear 25 lines, starting from a field already half full of junk if you like. Bring a friend: two players can share one keyboard, sit at two terminals on the same machine, or play over the network, each choosing their own level and height.

- **Two modes**: A-type marathon and B-type 25-line race
- **Head to head**: two players on one keyboard, on two terminals, or across the internet
- **Make it yours**: starting level and garbage height, rotation direction, soft-drop speed, block styles and colours, every key remappable
- **High scores** kept for you

## Controls

- **Left** and **Right**: move
- **Up**: rotate
- **Down**: soft drop; **Space**: hard drop
- **P** or **Enter**: pause
- In the menus: arrow keys and **Enter**, **Esc** or **Backspace** to go back, **Q** to quit at once

## Options

- `-level N`, `-height N`: start level (0–9) and garbage height (0–5)
- `-mode 1b`: B-type game; `-nomenu`: skip the menus
- `-listen PORT` / `-connect HOST:PORT`: network play
- `-hiscores`: print the high-score list
- `-help`: every option

Settings and high scores are saved in `~/.vitetris`.
