**FOURTEEN BATTLES. WILL HISTORY REPEAT?**

From First Bull Run in 1861 to Atlanta in 1864, fourteen battles of the American Civil War, each with the men history brought to it. You supply and command the Confederate army; the computer, or a second player at the same keyboard, the Union.

Before each battle, share out the treasury between food, pay and ammunition, against inflation that climbs as the war goes on. Then choose how to fight: an assault, a flanking march, digging in, falling back. Your losses and the outcome are set beside the real battle's.

- **A briefing for every battle,** with what happened there.
- **Morale** from how well the men are fed and paid; desertion when it's poor.
- **The whole war in a table:** history's result beside yours, battle by battle.
- **Two generals:** start with `--two` and the second player commands the North.

## Controls

- **Enter**: go on
- **Tab** and **↑ ↓**: move between food, pay and ammunition and change them; typing replaces a number
- **1** to **4**, **↑ ↓** or a click: choose a plan
- **M**: sound on or off; **Esc**: quit

## Options

- `--two`: two generals at one keyboard
