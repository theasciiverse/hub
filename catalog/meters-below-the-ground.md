**KILL THE EGGS BEFORE THEY HATCH!**

You are sixty metres below the ground, in a facility overrun by insects from somewhere else, and there is one way out: up. Every level flashes an order at the top of the screen. Kill the eggs before they hatch. Switch on the emergency beacon. Kill the boss. Do it, find the stairs, and climb a little closer to daylight.

Down here, everything is a meter, filling or draining. Your health is a meter. So is your armour, and your stamina for punching. Your weapons are meters you empty and refill. And every egg in the room has a meter of its own, ticking quietly towards the moment it hatches. Across the bottom of the screen runs the biggest meter of all: the metres still between you and the surface.

So each level is a race you can watch. Do you push through to the eggs on the far side before their bars fill, or stop and deal with the larva crawling towards you now? Leave them and the brood grows up: eggs hatch into larvae, larvae wrap themselves into chrysalises, chrysalises split open into aracnoids and beetoids, and the super eggs hatch queens.

Your answers are meters too. A quadgun that fires four ways at once. A railgun that hits every enemy in a line. A push that throws them back, a blink that teleports you clear, and a metabol that slows the brood's changes around you. Ammo, meds and armour shards lie on the floor to fill the bars again, and on every stair up you choose one more meter, or an upgrade to one you have, to take with you.

## Controls

- **Arrows**: move, and walk into enemies to punch
- **0** to **9**: use the meter in that slot, then a direction to aim it
- **Space**: wait; **Escape**: the menu, with save and quit

## History

This is where it all started. In March 2018 Stephen Sherratt took on the 7DRL Challenge, a whole roguelike in seven days, and made Meters Below the Ground on prototty, the Rust library for drawing games in characters that later grew into his chargrid. It was the first of the seven-day games he went on to make almost every year after: slime99, Orbital Decay, Rain Forest, Boat Journey, Electric Organ and Road Closed. All seven are here.
