**YOUR NAME IS PREY. HE IS STARVING.**

You wake in the delivery room of a sealed facility, half drowned, and a
voice is already talking. It belongs to Skashek, the Predator: pale as
death, two rows of sharp teeth, long arms ending in clawed hands. He is
kind enough to explain the rules. "I'm starving, Prey. So run. Avoid
becoming my next meal. I've scattered pieces of an environment suit
around the place. If you collect all seven pieces, you will be able to
survive the poison gas, and escape."

So run. Climb, leap and crawl through the facility, sneak, hide, and set
tricks for him to fall for, while you gather the helmet, the torso and
the rest before he gathers you. Then out through the emergency airlock.
"I may not be the first, but I will be the most elusive."

- **A hunter who learns**: each kind of trick only fools him so many
  times.
- **Six difficulties**: from Cat Mode, where you play the Predator's pet
  cat and he won't chase you, to Nightmare Mode: "What the heck?!
  Something has really gotten into the Predator today! His cruelty is
  insatiable!"
- **Parkour**: climb, jump and slide your way around, and learn the
  routes.
- **Different every time**: built for replaying, and UNDO won't change
  what the dice decided.

The game warns of violence, frequent crude language and rare mentions of
suicide.

**Run, Prey. Run.**

## How to play

Type what you want to do, in plain words, and press Enter:

- **look**, **inventory** (or **i**), **examine** something (or **x**)
- **north**, **south**, **east**, **west**, **up**, **down** (or **n**, **s**, **e**, **w**, **u**, **d**)
- **take**, **drop**, **open**, **push** things; **help** or **about** in most games
- **save** and **restore**; **quit** to stop

Your saved games are kept in `~/.local/share/i-am-prey`.
