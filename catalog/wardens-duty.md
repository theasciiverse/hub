**WARDEN, WELCOME TO THE PRISON.** As you might have already noticed, all
the cells are full. You really need to fix that.

Each level of the prison is a block of cells, and each cell holds a
prisoner: a goblin, a rat, a bat, an ogre, a carnivorous gelatine, a dragon,
a pangolin. Step into a cell and the prisoner stands before you
as one big glyph, with your choices beneath: hit it, cast a magic missile,
shoot a bow, breathe fire, water or poison once you have learned how. Before you
confirm, your gauges show what each choice will cost and what it will
bring: health, mana, arrows, gold, experience, resistance to the elements.

There is no map to wander. The whole game is in those choices. Clear every
cell and you go down a level, where the cells are more and the prisoners
tougher; shopkeepers have set up business in some of them, chests may be
trapped, some cells hold nothing but a trap to set off, and magic pools wait to be
drunk from. When nothing you can do
will clear a cell any more, your duty is over. You cannot really win; you
can only try to get deeper than before.

## Controls

- **Arrows**, **WASD** or **HJKL**: move between the cells of a level
- **1** to **9**: pick what to do with the prisoner
- **Enter**: do it; also continues after a message, and starts a new game
  after the last one
- **Ctrl+Q** or **Ctrl+C**: quit
