**GOOD MORNING! IT IS YOUR FIRST DAY. TRY TO SURVIVE THE NIGHT SHIFT.**

This is it, your grand day, the first day in a new job. You stand in front
of a large door labelled **Café Havoc**, and working in this big café is
going to be exciting. Jenkins, the chief steward, pins a badge on you and
explains: Café Havoc is a big business and a big brand, and its guests
expect top-level service. Do your best for three days (and three nights),
and your performance will be assessed afterwards.

"...and, uhm, if you hear people talking about some shady
night-murder-death-blood stuff, please be assured that it is only gossip
going on."

By day it is a lovely job. Ms. Vandelay, a retired librarian, would like a
Sachertorte. Mr. Zoidberg, a weird-looking archaeologist, has been waiting
for his Masala chai. Find what they ordered in the storage rooms and the
kitchens, carry it up and down the café's three floors, through the
billiard room and the smoking room, to the right table, and hand it over
with a smile. The cook will even patch you up with some magical cooking
power, once. Just never make the cook angry.

Then the clock ticks round. **Night falls!** And every guest still holding
a cheesecake or an ice-cold Club Mate comes for you with it. You can only parry a cheesecake with a cheesecake, so ready the
right item from your tray, switch to attack, and remember: a
self-respecting waiter does not attack his customers. Well, at least not
during the day.

- **A menu to memorise**: espresso, cappuccino, Pu-erh tea, kiwi juice and
  Red Bull, apple cake, tiramisu, gingerbread and, if you are unlucky, a
  lutefisk
- **Four ways to bump into people**: describe them, chat, give them their
  order, or attack, each unlocked as you need it
- **Badges** that change everything: double damage, time dilation, random
  regeneration, a Badge of peace that will not let you hurt anyone, and a
  Badge of dumbness that will not let you say a word
- **A guest list** of lumberjacks, astronauts, magicians and consultants,
  happy, impatient or furious
- **The assessment** at the end: alive or dead, items delivered, people
  killed

## Controls

- **Arrows** or **vim keys** (hjkl yubn), **Home**, **PgUp**, **End** and
  **PgDn** for the diagonals: walk; walk into someone to interact
- **.**: wait a turn
- **d**, **c**, **g**, **a**: what walking into someone does: describe,
  chat, give, attack (each appears once you need it)
- **1** to **8**: ready an item from your tray
- **R**: a new game, once the assessment is in
- **Ctrl+Q** or **Ctrl+C**: quit

## History

Ondřej Žára, the author of rot.js, made Café Havoc in seven days for the
Seven Day Roguelike Challenge in March 2016: a roguelike where the dungeon
is a café, the treasure is somebody's cappuccino, and the monsters are the
customers, but only after dark. It now plays in the terminal, its scripts
exactly as he wrote them.
