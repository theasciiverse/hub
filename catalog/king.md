**EIGHT YEARS TO GOVERN AN ISLAND.**

You are premier of Pellora, a small island of forests and farms, for an eight-year term. Every year foreign industry offers to buy land. The money is real and the factories bring work, but they also bring smoke: harvests shrink, the tourists stay away, and people fall ill or leave. Each year decide how much land to sell, how much to spend on food for the people, how many fields to plant and how much to put into cleaning the air.

- **An island map** of forest, farms and factories, with the haze to show for it.
- **A chart of your term,** year by year.
- **The verdict:** lose too many people, or sit on money while they go hungry, and the assembly ends your term early. Last the eight years and the voters decide.

## Controls

- **Tab** or **↑ ↓**: move between the orders; typing or the mouse wheel changes a number
- **F**: feed everyone; **P**: plant all you can; **Enter**: end the year
- **?**: the rules; **M**: sound on or off; **Esc**: quit
