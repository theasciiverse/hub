**THE DUNGEONS OF DOOM. FEW PEOPLE EVER MAKE IT OUT ALIVE.**

Rogue went to Bell Labs and came back bigger. Before you go down, choose
what you are: a fighter, strong enough to strike several times a turn; a
magic user with a growing book of spells; a cleric who prays, and turns the
undead; or a thief, who slinks past sleeping monsters, picks pockets and
stabs them in the back.

Then go after your quest. Deep in the dungeon, a few monsters carry unique
artifacts: the Wand of Orcus, the Rod of Asmodeus, the Flail of Yeenoghu,
the Horn of Geryon, the Mandolin of Brian. Bring one back and you are a
Complete Winner. Or keep going, as the manual dares you, "to match wits
with an arch-devil, demon prince, or even a deity found far down in the
dungeon."

- **Four classes**, each with powers of its own: spells, prayers, turning
  the undead, stealing and backstabbing
- **Over a hundred monsters**, many of them clever
- **Trading posts** down in the dungeon, to buy and sell
- **Experience, not gold, is your score**

## Controls

- **hjkl yubn**: move, the last four diagonally; with **Shift** run that
  way
- **f** and a direction: move until something turns up; **t**: throw,
  **z**: zap a wand or staff
- **i**: inventory, **d**: drop, **P**: pick up
- **e** eat, **q** quaff, **r** read, **w** wield, **W** wear, **T** take
  off
- **C**: cast a spell, **p**: pray, **a**: affect the undead, **^**:
  set a trap, **D**: dip something in a pool
- **s**: search for traps and secret doors, **.**: rest
- `>` and `<`: stairs down and up
- **?**: all the commands, **/**: what a symbol is
- **S**: save and quit, **Q**: quit

## Saving

**S** saves the game to your data folder. To carry on, start it from a
shell as `arogue -r`; a saved game is deleted once it is restored.

## History

Advanced Rogue was developed by a group at AT&T Bell Labs between 1984
and 1986, among them apparently Michael Morgan and Ken Dalka, and AT&T
distributed it for a time through its Toolchest. Version 5.8 grew from
Rogue 3.6 with parts of Super-Rogue. Nicholas J. Kisseberth (Yendor) restored
it in the Roguelike Restoration Project (5.8.1 in 2005, 5.8.2 in 2006).
This edition keeps your files in your data folder and has fixes for
64-bit machines and the score file. It is part of the Roguelike Restoration
Project in the Asciiverse catalog.
