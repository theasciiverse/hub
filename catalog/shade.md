Odd, how the light just makes your apartment gloomier. Your luggage is packed for the desert, the hyacinth by the window needs water, and morning will not come. Something about this small, familiar room is wrong, and the longer you look, the more it shows. A short game, best played without knowing more.

## How to play

Type what you want to do, in plain words, and press Enter:

- **look**, **inventory** (or **i**), **examine** something (or **x**)
- **north**, **south**, **east**, **west**, **up**, **down** (or **n**, **s**, **e**, **w**, **u**, **d**)
- **take**, **drop**, **open**, **push** things; **help** or **about** in most games
- **save** and **restore**; **quit** to stop

Your saved games are kept in `~/.local/share/shade`.
