Herb Chong's long-grown Rogue. Pick one of ten classes, from fighter,
paladin and ranger to druid, illusionist, assassin and ninja, spend
your gold at Friendly Fiend's Flea Market, and go down. The monsters
come in both cases and many kinds, and unique artifacts lie deep in the
dungeon for the bravest to bring back.

## Controls

- **hjkl yubn**: move, the last four diagonally; with **Shift** run that
  way, **m** and a direction to step without picking up
- **f** and a direction: fight; **t**: throw, **z**: zap a wand or
  staff
- **i**: inventory, **,**: pick up, **d**: drop
- **e** eat, **q** quaff, **r** read, **w** wield, **W** wear, **T** take
  off armour, **P** put on and **R** remove a ring, **A**: use an
  artifact
- **c**: cast a spell or say a prayer, **a**: affect the undead,
  **^**: set a trap, **D**: dip something in a pool
- **s**: search for traps and secret doors, **.**: rest
- `>`: stairs down
- **?**: all the commands, **/**: what a symbol is
- **S**: save and quit, **Q**: quit

## Saving

**S** saves the game to your data folder (press Return to take the name
offered). To carry on, start it from a shell as `urogue -r`; a saved
game is deleted once it is restored. Characters you choose to keep at
the start are stored there too.

## History

Herb Chong wrote UltraRogue in 1985 and 1986, from Rogue 3.6 and an
early Advanced Rogue. In 1992 he gave the source to Nicholas J.
Kisseberth (Yendor), its first restoration, and the two kept working on
it until 1995; the Roguelike Restoration Project released it as 1.0.7
in 2005. This edition keeps your files in your data folder and fixes
saving so that a saved game can be restored. It is part of the
Roguelike Restoration Project in the Asciiverse catalog.
