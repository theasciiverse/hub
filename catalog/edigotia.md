**A CITY OF YOUR OWN, IN A WORLD OF KINGDOMS.**

Generate a world: its size, climate, rainfall and mountains, and the
kingdoms that hold it, humans, elves, dwarves, orcs, goblins, ogres,
trolls, halflings, gnomes, minotaurs, rakshasa or naga. Then pick a race,
choose a spot on the map, name your town and start building.

Every tile around your city is real land with its own crops, game and
minerals. Lay out farms where the soil is good, hunting camps where the
deer and boar are, pastures, mines and housing; put your peasants to work,
attract merchants and burghers, set the taxes for each class and watch
their morale. Your ministers keep the books on finance, food, hunting,
livestock, mines and trade, and the seasons turn a day or a week at a
time.

**An unfinished game, and honest about it.** Whales called it "a game of
building, trade, diplomacy and war", and built the first half: the city
and its economy play, but the diplomacy and war never arrived, and the
other kingdoms' cities sit quietly on the map.

- **Generated worlds** with climate, terrain and twelve races of kingdom
- **A city builder** where each tile's crops, animals and ore matter
- **Three classes of citizen**, each with its own housing, taxes and morale
- **Ministers' reports** on finance, food, hunting, livestock, mines, trade

## Controls

- On the first screen: **G** generates a world, **N** founds a city in
  it, **L** loads a saved city, **H** opens the help
- **1** to **5**: the Game, Ministers, Buildings, World and Help menus
- **A**: build an area (then pick its kind and place it with the
  movement keys and **Enter**); **C**, **O** and **D**: close, reopen
  and destroy an area
- **Enter**: what is on a tile, **T**: terrain view, **R**: your city's
  reach
- **.** and **>**: let a day or a week pass
- **1** then **1**: save and quit

## Saving

**Save & Quit** in the Game menu keeps your city; **Load city** on the
first screen picks it up again. The world and your cities live in your
own folder, not beside the game.

## History

Whales, best known for the roguelike Cataclysm, worked on Edigotia in the
open on GitHub from 2014 to 2015, nearly nine hundred commits, and set it
aside in June 2015. It never had a release. This edition makes it
build on today's machines and finishes the one thing a city builder can't
do without: cities that save and load.
