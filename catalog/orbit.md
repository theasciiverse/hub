**SOMEWHERE OUT THERE, AN INVISIBLE SHIP.**

A cloaked raider circles your planet, counterclockwise, somewhere between 10,000 and 30,000 miles out, once every 12 to 36 hours. In seven hours it will have the power to escape.

Each hour you get one bomb, aimed by an angle and a distance. You can't see the raider, but your instruments tell you how far from it the bomb burst. Within 5,000 miles, and it's gone.

- **The plot from above:** the planet, the rings, every burst with the circle of places the raider might have been, and your aim with its kill radius.
- **Aim with the mouse,** or by numbers.
- **The truth at the end:** the raider's real orbit drawn in.

## Controls

- **Mouse**: aim, click to fire
- **Tab**: angle or distance; **↑ ↓**: change it; typing replaces it
- **Enter**: fire; after the hunt, a new one
- **M**: sound on or off; **Esc**: quit
