**SAVE YOUR DAUGHTER. THE CLOCK IS RUNNING.**

Your daughter has caught dianthroritis, and no doctor can help. The only
hope is a potion hidden deep in the volcano below the caverns of Larn,
where dragons and demons wait. You will not survive the volcano as you
are: first go down into the caverns, fight, gather gems and gold, and
spend them in town on the equipment that might keep you alive.

And all the while time passes. Every turn counts towards the day the
doctor can no longer help her.

NLarn is Larn, the 1986 roguelike, rewritten. The story and atmosphere
are the original's; the play is new: monsters that keep their distance
and shoot, allies that fight beside you, townsfolk who come to your aid,
weapons to enchant or poison, new spells, auto-travel, and full mouse
play. In English, German, French, Spanish and Portuguese.

## Controls

- **h j k l y u b n** or the keypad: move and attack; **H J K L Y U B N**: run
- **v**: travel to a place you name by its symbol (**O** for the caverns);
  **V**: carry on travelling
- **>** and **<**: go down, go up, or enter a building
- **i**: inventory; **,**: pick up; **W**: wear or wield; **c**: cast a spell
- **q**: drink; **r**: read; **f**: fire; **t**: throw
- **?** or **F1**: help with every key; **Ctrl+Q**: quit
- **Mouse**: click to travel or attack
