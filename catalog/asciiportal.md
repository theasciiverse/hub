**THINK WITH PORTALS. IN TEXT.**

You wake up somewhere you are not supposed to be, and a voice on the speaker would really rather you left. Doors need switches, switches need boxes, and the way out is always on the other side of something. Luckily you have picked up a portal gun.

ASCIIpOrtal is a side-on puzzle platformer. Shoot a blue portal and a yellow one onto the walls, step into one and out of the other. Use them to cross gaps, reach ledges, carry boxes to switches, dodge boulders and fling yourself across the room: whatever falls into a portal keeps its speed on the way out. You can't jump, so you'll need to think.

- **25 levels** in the main game, which teach every trick
- **14 more map packs** from the community, from gentle to evil
- **Doors, switches, conveyors, chain link, lasers, boulders** and companion cubes
- **Make your own levels**: they are plain text files

## Controls

- **Left** / **Right**: walk; **Up** / **Down**: climb ladders, or aim
- **Number pad**: aim in eight directions
- **Z**: blue portal; **X**: yellow portal; **Space**: the next portal; **C**: clear portals
- **R**: restart the level; **P**: pause; **Esc**: menu
- **+** / **-**: game speed

Holding a key to walk works best in a terminal with the kitty keyboard protocol (kitty, foot, WezTerm, Ghostty, Alacritty, iTerm2). Other terminals work too, with a short pause before a held key starts repeating.

Your progress and your own map packs are kept in `~/.asciiportal`.
