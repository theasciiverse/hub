**ONE CARD LEFT. EVERYONE IS LOOKING AT YOU.**

Seven cards each, four colours, one pile. Match the colour, the number or the symbol, and keep the table moving. Then the knives come out. A skip and the next player sits on their hands. A reverse and the whole table spins the other way. A draw two lands on the player who was one card from winning, and a wild lets you name the very colour they haven't got. Nothing left in the colour in play? That's when the Wild Draw Four comes down!

Ada, Bo and Cyd are already at the table, and they play dirty in exactly the right way: they hoard their skips and draw twos until somebody gets close, then let them fly. First to an empty hand wins, and everyone else is ranked by the cards they're still holding.

- **Up to nine computer players,** who save their worst for whoever is about to go out
- **The full deck:** numbers, skips, reverses, draw twos, wilds and wild draw fours
- **Two to ten at the table,** four to start, and a new deal the moment a game ends

**WHO'S DOWN TO ONE?**

## Controls

- **Left** / **Right**: choose a card
- **Enter**: play it (after a wild, pick the colour and Enter again)
- **D**: draw
- **Esc**: leave (asks first in a game)
- **Left** / **Right** on the setup screen: how many players; **Enter**: deal; **Q**: quit

## History

Pieczasz began tty.cards in April 2026 with one bold idea: type `ssh tty.cards` and you are sitting at a card table, with no download, no browser and no sign-up form, your SSH key your only ticket in. Over seven months and more than eight hundred commits it grew five games, lobbies, a turn clock and rated tables. Asciiverse dealt each of the five out as a title of its own and filled every table with computer players, so a game is on whenever you are. Colour Clash is the wildest of them.
