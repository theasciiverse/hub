**PULL THE PLUNGER. THE TABLE IS ALIVE.**

Real-time pinball physics, drawn in Braille vector lines right in your terminal. The ball rolls and ricochets off bumpers, slingshots and drop targets, the camera follows it up and down the table, and a dot-matrix display keeps the score.

Eight tables, each with its own rules. On Round and Round the bumpers orbit. On Eye of Horus a spinning triangle guards the pyramid at the centre. On Orbiters five planets circle a sun and tug at the ball as it passes. Ad Astra carries a map of the real night sky, 1,500 stars, and asks you to light up Orion, Leo or Ursa Major one star at a time. Pool Party racks them up and dares you to "Shoot the Eight Ball!" Ball locks, multiball and jackpots wait on every table that has them.

- **Eight tables**: Classic, Round and Round, Ringmaster, Compound Interest, Eye of Horus, Orbiters, Ad Astra and Pool Party
- **Real physics**: flippers, kickers, ramps, loops and layered mini tables
- **The original's sound**: score dings, rollover notes, and music that builds as you score
- **Faithful rules**: ball savers, halving multipliers and "Shoot Again", as Vector Pinball plays them
- **Unlimited balls** from the menu, when you just want to play

## Controls

- **Space**: launch the ball
- **Z**, **Left** or **Left Shift**: left flipper
- **X**, **Right** or **Right Shift**: right flipper (Shift works on terminals with the kitty keyboard protocol; elsewhere use the letters or arrows)
- **Enter**: carry on after a lost ball, or start again after game over
- **Esc**: back to the menu
- In the menu: **Up** or **k**, **Down** or **j**, **Enter** to choose, **Q** to quit; sound, score animations, ball trails and unlimited balls are switched there
