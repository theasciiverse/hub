**DON'T TAKE THE QUEEN.**

Thirteen cards each, and three of them go to a neighbour before the first trick: left, then right, then across, then keep. The two of clubs leads. Follow suit if you can. Every heart you take costs a point and the queen of spades costs thirteen, unless you take every last one of them and shoot the moon.

The match ends when someone reaches 100. Lowest score wins.

- **Three computer players,** who pass their most dangerous cards, duck under the winning card, and dump the queen the moment you lead their empty suit.
- **The full rules:** passing, hearts broken, no points on the first trick, shooting the moon.
- **A match to 100,** hand after hand.

Hearts is one of the five tables of Pieczasz's tty.cards, here on its own.

**WHO'S GOT HER?**

## Controls

- **Left** / **Right**: choose a card
- **Space**: mark a card to pass; **Enter**: pass the three marked
- **Enter**: play a card
- **Enter**: deal the next hand, when it's yours to deal
- **Esc**: leave (asks first in a game)
- **Enter** on the setup screen: deal; **Q**: quit
