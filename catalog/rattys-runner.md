**ONE SMALL MOUSE. ONE LONG ROAD. AND IT ONLY GETS FASTER!**

The Xfce mouse has somewhere to be, and it's running flat out to get there! The road twists and climbs through four very different places. Out along the Meadow Path between trees and toadstools, where logs lie across the way and branches hang low. Up onto the Kitchen Counter, past jars and mugs, where somebody has left out a mousetrap. Was that on purpose? Down the Night Lane under the stars and the lamplight, crates and barriers and potholes in the dark. And at last into the Moonlit Drain, pipes overhead and underfoot and channels cut across the way...

Three lanes, and trouble in all of them. Some of it is low, some is high, some is a hole where the road used to be, and the mouse can beat every one of them, if your fingers are quick enough:

- **Jump** the logs, mousetraps, crates and pipes, and leap the puddles, the sink, the potholes and the channels
- **Slide** under the branches, the rolling pins, the barriers and the overhead pipes
- **Change lanes** to slip past, or to snatch the cheese, worth five metres apiece
- **Three lives:** the mouse can take a knock or two, but not three
- **Faster and busier** the further you get, until the road is blocked two lanes wide
- **Two looks:** clean lines on black, or painted in full colour with sky, grass and trees; press **V** to switch
- **Your best run is kept,** waiting to be beaten

How far will the little mouse get? **RUN, MOUSE, RUN!**

## Controls

- **Left / Right** or **A / D**: change lane
- **Up**, **Space** or **W**: jump
- **Down** or **S**: slide
- **P** pause, **M** sound on or off, **V** lines or painted, **R** run again, **Q** or **Esc** quit

## Options

- `--mute`: start with the sound off
- `--demo`: let the computer run
- `--painted`: start in the painted look

## History

Ratty's Runner started out as ozzyocak's Ratty Runner, a three-lane runner made for Orhun Parmaksız's Ratty terminal, with a 3D mouse only that terminal could show. On 3 November 2026 the runner set off again, the same day as Ratty's Cube: its rules of lanes, jumps, slides and lives kept, everything else new. The road is drawn the old arcade way, curving and rising over hills that hide what is coming, and its runner is a familiar face to anyone who has used the Xfce desktop: the Xfce project's own mouse.
