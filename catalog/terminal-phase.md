**A SPACE SHOOTER. IN YOUR TERMINAL. OF COURSE.**

Your little **>** ship drifts through a cavern of rock while the stars twinkle past in Braille dots. Then the enemies come: squiggling ships that weave toward you, turrets that fire across your path, mines, kamikazes that turn and aim themselves at you, and cannons firing up from the ground. Keep shooting, keep moving, and don't scrape the walls.

Two levels, three lives, and a credits roll of everyone who backed it.

- **Old-school shmup**: one ship, one gun, a lot of things that want you dead
- **Terrain that fights back**: the cavern narrows and widens as you fly
- **A starfield in Braille**: smooth, tiny stars in plain text
- **Time travel**: start it with `-t` and press **t** to step back through the last moments of play

## Controls

- **Arrow keys**: move
- **Space**: fire
- **q** or **Esc**: leave the level; **q** on the title menu quits
- In the menu: **Up** and **Down** to choose, **Enter** to pick

## Options

- `-t`: allow time travel with the **t** key
- `-f`: show frames per second
- `terminal-phase LEVEL-FILE ...`: play your own level files instead of the built-in ones

## History

Terminal Phase began in 2019 as a test program for Spritely Goblins, a library for programs built from communicating objects. Christine Lemmer-Webber later moved it to Guile; this is the Racket version, with the fixes and new enemies that came after its 1.1 release.
