**PICO. FERMI. BAGELS.**

The computer thinks of a three-digit number with no digit used twice. You guess, and it gives clues without saying which digit is which:

- **Fermi:** a right digit in the right place
- **Pico:** a right digit in the wrong place
- **Bagels:** none of your digits is in it

Twenty guesses. With a good plan, eight is plenty.

- **A digit pad** that dims every digit a Bagels has ruled out.
- **How many numbers are still possible,** after every clue.
- **Your record:** solved, missed, average and best, kept between games.

## Controls

- **0** to **9**, or click the pad: type a guess; **Backspace**: fix it
- **Enter**: guess; **N**: a new number; **Tab**: the rules
- **M**: sound on or off; **Esc**: quit
