**THE ELDERS NEED SIMELLAS. THIS YEAR, THE HONOUR FELL ON YOU.**

Every year your village sends someone down into Hareka's Underground to gather simellas, the medicinal plants that grow nowhere else. This year it is you, with a robe, a dagger and the elders' word that deep below, a magical monolith will carry you home. Between you and it wait goblin bands, hounds that bark and follow your scent, tiny harpies that blink away the moment they are hurt, cyclopes throwing rocks, mirror specters that drink your magic, and earth dragons, who are said to have dug most of these tunnels in the first place.

Boohu is a roguelike about where you stand. There are no experience points and no character sheet: you grow by what you find. A spear strikes two foes in a row, so take it into a corridor; an axe hits everyone around you, so take it into the open; the dancing rapier swaps places with your foe to strike the one behind; the har-kar gauntlets carry you through a line of enemies on the wind. Doors open only while someone stands in them, blue foliage hides you, and the magical stones set into the floor turn a single blow into a teleport, a bank of fog, a confusing boom or a ring of sudden walls. Fight, or flee. Both are good answers.

Listen as you go. Somewhere below you may hear faint music, *larilon, larila*: that is Frundis, a musician and harmonist who also happens to be a two-handed staff. And if someone teleports you away for no reason at all, it is probably Marevor Helith, an ancient undead nakrus and repentant necromancer, down here looking for his old disciple Jaixel.

- **Eight levels** to the monolith, and three optional depths beyond it for the brave and the greedy
- **No grinding**: twice on the way down you simply feel different, and come away with an aptitude such as good ears, scales, fast legs or the earth throwing your foes back
- **Weapons with a trick each**: the hopeful sword that hits harder the more you bleed, the vampiric dagger, the defender flail, the final blade that kills anything below half health
- **Rods and potions** for every tight spot: fireballs, lightning, digging, swapping, sleep, walls, shadows, dreams
- **A coffee break long**, every dungeon new, and every game kept as a replay to watch back

## Controls

- **h j k l y u b n**, the **arrow keys** or the **number pad**: move; walk into a creature to attack it
- **H J K L Y U B N**: travel in a direction
- **o**: explore automatically; **G**: go to the nearest stairs; **>**: descend
- **.** or **5**: wait a turn; **r**: rest
- **e** or **g**: equip what you stand on
- **q**: quaff a potion; **t**: throw; **v**: evoke a rod; **i**: inventory
- **x**: examine; **%**: your character and quest; **m**: messages
- **Mouse**: click to move, and the buttons along the bottom of the map
- **?**: help; **=**: settings and key bindings; **S**: save and quit; **Q**: quit without saving

## Options

- `-s`: the 16 colour solarized palette
- `-o`: 8 colours only
- `-c`: keep the camera centred on you
- `-n`: no animations
- `-r _`: watch a replay of your last game

## History

anaseto (Yon) made his first public commit of Boohu in September 2017 and released version 0.1 a week later, a short tactical roguelike shaped by Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup and the talk in its tavern, with ideas from Brogue. Releases came quickly through 2017 and 2018: dense foliage, the self-opening doors, the magical stones, mouse support. In 2019 he grew a new game out of its code, Harmonist, a stealth roguelike set in the same Underground, and in 2023 Boohu got some of it back, Harmonist's field of view among it. His third game, Shamogu, followed in 2025.
