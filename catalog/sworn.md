**SWEAR THE OATH. BREAK IT WHEN IT PAYS.**

The year 800. You are a lesser noble with one hold and a handful of
spears, and four peoples share the realm: Saxons, Danes, Bretons and
Moravians. Each has a crown seat, and the first noble to sit in theirs
as king, answering to no one, wins.

Every season all orders are secret and take effect at once. Raise
troops, march on your neighbours, take the towns that pay and the
fortresses that hold. Then the season turns and the report tells you
who kept their word.

Between seasons there are letters, and letters can say anything. Only
an offer binds: gold, land or an oath of fealty, carried out before the
marching if both sides can still pay. Promise to stand with an ally and
stay home. Buy a year's peace from the lord on your border. Kneel to the
strongest Saxon now and turn on them when you have grown. March on your
own liege and the whole realm hears of it.

- **The crown** needs your people's seat, two fifths of the realm held
  by you and your vassals, a name worth 20 prestige, and every other
  noble of your people sworn to you somewhere down the chain
- **Four peoples:** Saxon troops cost less to keep, Danes hit harder,
  Bretons hold their walls, Moravian towns pay more
- **Computer nobles** see only what you would see, take good deals,
  write to you when your lands meet, ask tribute when they are strong,
  plead when they are not, and gang up on whoever comes near the crown
- **Up to eight players** over the network, found on the local network
  or joined by an id from anywhere. A player who drops is held by the
  computer until they come back; the host can set a timer on the seasons
- Battles and clashes on the road flash on the map; at the end, the
  standings and charts of every noble's rise and fall

## Controls

- **Arrow keys** or the mouse: choose a province; **n** the next of yours
- **r**: raise troops; **m**: march (**b** to fight on someone's behalf)
- **w**: write a letter, with an offer if you like; **a**: accept an offer
- **1** to **5**: province, orders, letters, nobles, chronicle
- **e**: end the season; **u**: take your orders back; **?**: all keys

## Options

- `--host`: host a table for others
- `--join`, `--join ID`: find tables on the local network, or join one by its id
- `--seed N`: new games on realm N
