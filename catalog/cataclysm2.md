**An unfinished alpha.** Cataclysm II is the rewrite of Cataclysm that its
author, Whales, began in 2013 and stopped working on in 2016. It is
playable for a while, but much is missing, things are rough, and
characters cannot be saved yet: each game is one sitting.

Fifty years after the cataclysm that filled the world with monsters and
raised the dead, you wash up on the beach of a shipwreck with a few things
from your old life. Make a character from stats, traits and a profession,
pick through the wreck, read the world map and set out across fields,
forests, swamps and salt marsh. Towns are where the zombies are: fast
ones, brutes, spitters and smoke zombies. Every character starts with a
handful of small missions (eat this, read that) that pay experience, and
experience buys skills.

## Controls

- **hjkl yubn** or the numpad: move, the last four diagonally; walk into a
  monster to attack it, into a door to open it
- **,**: pick up, **d**: drop, **i**: inventory
- **w**: wield, **W**: wear, **T**: take off, **e**: eat, **R**: read,
  **a**: apply
- **t**: throw, **f**: fire, **r**: reload
- **o** and **C**: open and close, **s**: smash, **x**: examine,
  **;**: look
- `<` and `>`: stairs up and down
- **:** or **M**: the world map (**q** leaves it)
- **@**: your character, **K**: skills
- **Q**: end the game

On the first screen, **H** opens the manual, with every key and how the
game works; **W** makes and chooses worlds.

## Saving

Worlds you make under **W: Manage Worlds** are kept in your data folder
and can be played again with a new character. Characters themselves are
not saved: **L: Load Character** was never finished.

## History

Whales wrote Cataclysm, a post-apocalyptic roguelike, from 2010 to 2013;
its players carried it on as Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead, the big
community fork that is still developed today. Cataclysm II
is a different project: Whales' own start over, with new code, its own
interface layer and data files, set fifty years after the first game. It
was never finished, and its last change, in 2016, did not even compile.
This edition adds two contributors' unmerged fixes
and crash fixes of our own, keeps the game data beside the program and
your worlds in your data folder, and leaves the game as Whales left it.
