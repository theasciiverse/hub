You are nine months pregnant and the contractions have begun. Trapped in a castle with more enemies than friends, Queen in name but not in influence, you look into your crystal ball to see what waits for your son. You can wait in your den for his fate to arrive, or act, quickly and ruthlessly, before the waters break.

## How to play

Type what you want to do, in plain words, and press Enter:

- **look**, **inventory** (or **i**), **examine** something (or **x**)
- **north**, **south**, **east**, **west**, **up**, **down** (or **n**, **s**, **e**, **w**, **u**, **d**)
- **take**, **drop**, **open**, **push** things; **help** or **about** in most games
- **save** and **restore**; **quit** to stop

Your saved games are kept in `~/.local/share/fate`.
