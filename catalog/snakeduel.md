Your snake against the computer's. Both start side by side in the middle
of the board, yours on the right, and both leave a wall behind them
wherever they go. Run into a wall, the other snake or yourself and you
lose the round; make the computer crash first and you win it.

The computer plays to block your way and keep room for itself. The first
to five wins takes the match, and from four all it goes on until one
side is two ahead. The colours of the snakes change from round to round.

## Controls

- **Arrow keys**, **hjkl** or **wasd**: change direction (not straight back)
- **p**: pause
- **F1** or **?**: help on the controls, **F2** or **!**: how to play
- **q** or **Esc**: quit

## Options

Started from a shell, Snakeduel fits the board to the terminal, or takes
its size:

- `-w`: the width, from 40 to 127
- `-l`: the length (height), from 10 to 127
- `-h`: help on the options

## History

Snakeduel is one of the games abakh wrote for New BSD Games, a collection
of small curses games started in 2018 and named after the classic BSD
games. The collection is packaged by Debian, Ubuntu, openSUSE, Alpine,
Nix, Homebrew, FreeBSD and pkgsrc, and is in the AUR.
