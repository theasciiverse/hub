**THE CITY NEVER SLEEPS. TONIGHT IT'S JUST GETTING WET.**

Night, somewhere downtown, and it is pouring. You're hanging high above the street, looking straight down. NOODLES. KARAOKE. PACHINKO. 24H MART. The neon signs hum over the shopfronts, sputter out, lose a letter and buzz back to life, and every colour they throw runs down the wet road in long shimmering streaks.

Below you the city goes about its night. Cars and trucks swish through the puddles, headlight beams sweeping the tarmac ahead of them, scooters weave between, and now and then a police car tears past flashing red and blue. People hurry along the pavements under umbrellas of every colour, wait at the crossing while the traffic stops for them, or mill about in a glass-roofed lobby glowing in the dark. Steam curls out of the manholes and drifts off on the wind. Skylights burn on the rooftops and beacons blink. And once in a while lightning cracks over the city and lights up every roof at once.

- **A new street every time,** laid out to fit your window: its own shops, its own signs, its own alleys
- **Signs with a life of their own:** sushi bars, pawnbrokers, a net cafe, a tattoo parlour, a motel, all of them buzzing and flickering
- **Rain that catches the light,** every streak picking up the colour of whatever sign it falls past
- **Leave it running** and the city simply carries on, all night

## History

Between 2015 and 2018 a developer kept a devlog on the TIGSource forums for Terminal Rain, a cyberpunk roguelike drawn in characters, and posted a GIF of a rain-soaked street glowing with neon. The game was never released, but that one picture was enough. In October 2026 Neon Rain built the whole street from scratch, every car, umbrella and sign of it, not to play but to watch: a little window onto a city in the rain.

## Controls

- **Any key**: quit

## Options

From a terminal, `neon-rain -s 7` lays out the same street every time.
