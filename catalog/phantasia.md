**THE REALM IS ENDLESS. THE GRAIL IS OUT THERE.**

Phantasia drops you into a realm laid out on coordinates, with the Lord of the Realm's castle at its centre. Pick a character class, roll your strength, magic and luck, and set out. Every step may bring a monster: fight it, cast a spell, try your luck or run. Win, and you take its gold and grow stronger. Buy armour and weapons at the trading posts, and keep going.

Climb far enough and you can become a king, a council member, even one of the valar, and go in search of the Holy Grail. Phantasia was written for many players on one machine, who could meet, fight and rule over each other; alone, the whole realm and its monsters are yours.

## Controls

- **N**, **S**, **E**, **W** (or **K**, **J**, **L**, **H**): a step north, south, east or west
- The number keys pick from the menu on screen: **1** moves to a point, **4** shows your stats, **5** quits
- In a fight, the numbers on screen choose: fight, cast a spell, try your luck or flee
- Your character is kept with a password you choose

## History

Edward Estes wrote the first Phantasia in 1978-79 for an HP 2000 and rewrote it in C for Unix in the mid-eighties, where it became one of the BSD games. Later bsd-games collections dropped it as unplayable on single-user systems. This edition keeps the realm's files in your own data folder, so it plays alone.
