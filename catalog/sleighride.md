**SECOND DAY OF CHRISTMAS. SANTA'S ASLEEP. THE ELVES HAVE FOUND THE KEYS.**

Every present is delivered. Santa is snoring in his armchair by the workshop fire. And every last one of his helpers has grabbed anything with runners and gone for a joyride!

Bob takes the big sleigh. Holly and Juniper saddle up the reindeer. Sprocket fires up the workshop snowmobile, Pip grabs a toboggan, Nutmeg straps on racing skis, Tomte rides Julbocken, the straw Yule goat, and Befana? Befana takes her broomstick. Pick your racer and floor it!

But come lap two... SANTA'S AWAKE! He's in the one thing they left behind: the snowplough. He's got a list, he's checking it twice, and whoever is out in front is clearly the ringleader. Take the lead and he's coming for YOU, a mitten the size of a sack swinging in for a good clip round the ear. YOU'RE ON THE NAUGHTY LIST!

Drift through the bends until your sparks burn frost blue, then gold, then aurora, and let them rip for a boost! Fly off the kickers and throw a barrel roll! Smash open present boxes for snowballs, homing tinsel and peppermint boosts, or hold out a sprig of mistletoe on a stick: any rival who gets too close gets kissed and stops dead in a cloud of hearts!

- **Ten courses!** A frozen lake, a lake of eggnog flecked with nutmeg, black ice full of holes (SPLASH!), a candy cane forest, a gingerbread village with a cottage right in the road (jump it!), Santa's own workshop with its conveyor belts, a glacier split by crevasses, a birch wood where Ded Moroz lays frost on the road, the clifftops of a fjord, and a Christmas night market where the crowd throws presents.
- **Hazards straight out of Christmas folklore:** the Icelandic Yule Cat, the prankster Yule Lads, snow tornadoes, blizzards, and a line of penguins waddling across the road with a polar bear lumbering after them, never quite fast enough. Plough into a penguin and it belly-slides away across the snow. Penguins like that.
- **Real hills and jumps,** carved in voxels, right in your terminal.
- **Up to eight players** on the local network, on keyboard or gamepad.

Ten courses, eight racers, one very cross Santa. **CAN YOU WIN WITHOUT GETTING CAUGHT?**

## Controls

- **Left** and **Right** (or **A** and **D**): steer
- **Up** or **W**: gas; **Down** or **S**: brake and reverse
- **Space** or **Shift**, held while steering: drift
- **X**, **E** or **Ctrl**: use your item; with brake held, drop a present behind you as a trap
- **P**: pause, **R**: restart, **Esc**: back
- **F1** or **H**: help, with the drifting, items, Santa and the hazards
- **F8**: sound on or off, **M**: music on or off
- **Gamepad**: stick or d-pad to steer, A gas, B brake, right shoulder drift, X, Y or left shoulder item

On terminals without the kitty keyboard protocol, Up latches the gas on, Down turns it off, and Space toggles a drift.

## Options

- `--race`: straight into a race
- `--course N`: pick the course, 1 to 10
- `--as NAME`: race as Bob, Holly, Sprocket, Pip, Juniper, Nutmeg, Tomte or Befana
- `--host`, `--join`, `--name NAME`: races on the local network
- `--mute`: no sound
