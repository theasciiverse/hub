**NETHACK, BUT EVERYTHING IS OUT TO GET YOU.**

You know NetHack. You know about Elbereth, about praying when you are low, about the soldier ants. EvilHack knows you know. Its monsters have more hit points, fight in packs, wear the armour they find and zap the wands they pick up. Things that were safe are not safe any more.

EvilHack is a NetHack 3.6 variant built to be very hard and still fair. It borrows the best of GruntHack, SporkHack, UnNetHack, xNetHack and others, and adds plenty of its own: new races such as centaurs, giants, illithids and tortles, new roles, new artifacts and item properties, player-made forges and much more. Winning is possible. It is just a long way down.

- **Tougher, smarter monsters** that use items and fight together
- **New races and roles**, and a reworked balance for the old ones
- **New items, artifacts, materials and object properties**
- **The full NetHack dungeon**, with new branches and changes on the way down

## Controls

- **h j k l y u b n**: move (shift to run); **.** wait; **s** search
- **i** inventory; **,** pick up; **w** wield; **W** wear; **q** quaff; **r** read
- **<** / **>**: stairs; **#**: extended commands; **?**: help
- **S** saves and quits; **#quit** gives up (type the whole word *yes*)

Your saves, scores and dumplogs are kept in `~/.local/share/evilhack`; your options go in `~/.evilhackrc`.
