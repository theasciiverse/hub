**"Rest awhile, oh Wanderer, whilst thine Dungeon is initialising."**

So says a dusty voice in a grey, misty nowhere, and the cave will not
let you in until you have understood it. Get past that, and you are
standing at the end of a road before a small brick building, with a
forest around you and a stream running into a gully. It is the most
famous opening in adventure games, and it is only the doorway.

Adventure 770 is the Colossal Cave grown to its fullest: 478 places, more
than twelve hundred words understood, and 41 treasures to bring home for
all 770 points. Dwarves still prowl the dark, and far below the old
halls, new caverns open that no earlier version ever had.

The cave talks back. Ask the same thing twice and it rarely answers the
same way, it corrects your typos without giving away a secret, and it
lets you examine nearly anything it mentions: the bricks of the well
house, the gold veins in a wall. Commands stay short, but they chain:
**drop all but lamp and rod then exit** is one move.

## History

Will Crowther wrote Adventure in 1976 and grew it with Don Woods into
the 350-point classic of 1977. Others built on it: Peter Luckett and
Jack Pike's Adventure II in 1979, Dave Platt's Adventure 3 in 1984. In
1985 Mike Arnautov merged those two into one game, then polished it into
Adventure 4+, the 660-point version.

One thing in it nagged at him for years. He had never found a clever way
to be rid of the picnicking giant, so the Jinn simply scared it off.
In 1999 he set out to put that right, and four years later, in March
2003, the cave had grown into Adv770. He is still tending it, in his own
A-code language.

## How to play

Type a word or two and press Enter:

- **north**, **south**, **east**, **west**, **up**, **down**, **in**, **out** (or **n**, **s**, **e**, **w**, **u**, **d**)
- **look** (or **l**), **inventory**, **get** and **drop** things, **examine** them
- **help** for hints, **voc** for the words it knows, **info** for more
- **save** and **restore** (a point cost, as tradition demands), **memorize** and **recall** for a quick try
- **quit** to end the game; close the terminal instead and the game picks up where you were next time

Saved games live in `~/.local/state/adv770`.
