**STOP LOOKING AT YOUR FINGERS.**

GNU Typist is the patient teacher you never had in school. It starts with your fingers on the home row, introduces each key one at a time, and gives you a line to copy. Hit the wrong key and it marks it, quietly. Make too many mistakes and you do the line again. Before long you are typing the whole alphabet, numbers and punctuation without looking down.

It has been teaching people to type since 1998, and it still works the same: short drills, honest speed tests, and a personal best to beat.

- **Courses for QWERTY, Dvorak and Colemak**, short and long
- **Drills and speed tests**, including passages from Shakespeare
- **Number pad, symbols and programming** lessons
- **Lessons in other languages**: `gtypist esp.typ` for Spanish, plus Czech, Romanian, Russian and many KTouch keyboard courses

## Controls

- In menus: **arrow keys** and **Enter**; **Esc** goes back
- In a drill: type the line you see, then **Enter**
- After an exercise: **R** to repeat, **N** for the next one, **E** to leave the lesson

Your personal bests are kept in `~/.gtypist-bestlog`.
