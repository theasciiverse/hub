**LEAP AND TAKE, OR SWEEP WHOLE LINES AWAY.**

Points joined by lines along every row, column and diagonal. In alquerque, your men leap enemies like draughts pieces and can keep on leaping. In fanorona, the national game of Madagascar, you capture by moving towards an enemy or away from one, and the whole unbroken line of enemies in that direction is swept off the board at once!

Alquerque's first rules are in the Book of Games a Spanish king had made in 1283. Fanorona was being played "everywhere, in-doors and out of doors" in 1886, its lines scratched on rocks and on the paving outside the palace. Its board is two alquerque boards side by side, and in 2008 it was proved that fanorona, played perfectly, is a draw. Nobody plays perfectly.

- **Alquerque de doze, after Alfonso X**: 5 by 5, twelve men each, taking not compulsory
- **Alquerque after Bell**: the stricter game; taking compulsory, and no man steps backwards
- **Fanorona, today's rules**: 9 by 5, twenty-two men each, approach and withdrawal
- **Fanorona after Montgomery**: the rules as first written down, in 1886
- **Vela**: the game after a defeat; last game's winner must hand over seventeen men, one at a time, before taking a single one back
- **Fanoron-dimy**: fanorona on the little board of five by five

Play the computer at three strengths, from either side, or a friend at the same keyboard.

## Controls

- **Arrows**: move the cursor; **Enter** or **Space**: pick up a man, then put it on a lit point; a turn that takes again goes on from where the man stands; where one step could take two lines, point at the line you want swept away; **Esc**: let go of a man, or stop taking where the rules allow
- **Mouse**: click
- The men a step would take are marked ✕ when the cursor is on where it goes
- **F1** or **?**: the rules and where they come from; **M**: menu; **Q**: quit
- **O**: the braille ornament on or off

## Options

- `--demo`: the computer plays both sides
- `--seed N`: the same game every time
- `--plain`: start without the ornament, for fonts that draw braille thin
