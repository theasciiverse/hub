**THE ISLAND IS WAITING. WHO WILL RULE IT?**

Nineteen hexes of forest, hill, pasture, field and mountain rise out of
the sea, each one stamped with a number. Roll the dice, and every
settlement that touches that number gets paid. Wood! Brick! Wool!
Grain! Ore! Who will claim the best spots first?

Plant your first two settlements, lay your roads, and the race is on.
Swap sheep for brick with a rival who badly needs them, or trade four of
a kind with the bank when nobody will deal. Turn villages into cities
that bring in twice as much. And when a seven comes up, the robber
strikes: he squats on a tile, starves everyone around it and lifts a
card from a neighbour of your choice.

- **The whole race to ten**: roads, settlements, cities and
  development cards, every point counted.
- **Five development cards**: Knights to chase the robber off, Road
  Building, Monopoly, Year of Plenty, and hidden Victory Points.
- **Wheel and deal**: post an offer for everyone, or trade with the bank.
- **A new island every game**: the tiles are shuffled, and the numbers
  spiral round them the way the board game sets them out.
- **Three or four players at one keyboard**, each with a hand only they
  should look at.
- **Save and come back**: the start screen picks up where you left off.
- **Play across the room**: host a game and friends on your local network
  join by ssh, each at their own terminal.

**FIRST TO TEN POINTS TAKES THE ISLAND. WILL IT BE YOU?**

## Controls

- **Arrow keys**: move on the board and through the menus
- **Enter**: confirm; **Esc**: back out of a choice
- **1** to **4**: show that player's hand; **0**: follow whoever's turn
  it is
- **Tab**: on a narrow terminal, switch between the board and the hands
- **e**: emoji tiles or two-letter tiles (WD, BR, SH, WH, OR)
- **Save & Quit**: in the menu before you roll
- **Ctrl+C**: quit without saving

## Options

- `el_poblador new Ana Bruno Carmen`: start straight away with these
  players (three or four)
- `el_poblador serve -addr 0.0.0.0:2222`: host a game for your local
  network; friends join with `ssh -p 2222 <your address>`, pick or name a
  lobby and press Enter when ready
