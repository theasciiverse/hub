**YOU MEANT LS.**

Everyone mistypes `ls` sometimes. Since 1993, Toyoda Masashi's sl has made sure you remember it: a steam locomotive, smoke and all, rolls across your terminal from right to left, and nothing you type will hurry it along. Not even Ctrl+C.

It has become one of the best-known jokes in Unix, and it is still a pleasure to watch.

- **sl**: the D51 steam locomotive with its coal car and carriages
- **sl -a**: an accident, with people calling for help
- **sl -F**: the train flies
- **sl -l**: a little one
- **sl -c**: the C51 instead

## Controls

None. That is the point. Wait for the train to pass.
