**FIFTEEN PUZZLES. ONE DAILY. NO TWO ALIKE.**

A whole shelf of logic puzzles in one place, every one of them generated
fresh: pick the puzzles you feel like, set how hard you want them, and
PuzzleTea makes one. Or share a seed, and a friend gets the very same
grid.

- **Nonogram**: fill cells to match the row and column clues
- **Sudoku** and **Sudoku RGB**, the second with three symbols instead of nine
- **Netwalk**: turn the tiles until every computer reaches the server
- **Hashiwokakero**: join the islands with bridges
- **Nurikabe**, **Hitori**, **Shikaku**, **Fillomino** and **Ripple Effect**
- **Takuzu** and **Takuzu+**, the second with `=` and `x` clues between cells
- **Lights Out**, **Word Search** and **Spell Puzzle**

Every day brings one puzzle that is the same for everyone, with a streak
to keep. Every week brings a ladder of 99, unlocked one rung at a time.
Solving earns XP and levels in each kind of puzzle, the stats screen
keeps score, and 365 colour themes let it look the way you like.

## Controls

- **Arrow keys** and **Enter**: through the menus; **Esc**: back
- **Each puzzle shows its own keys** under the board: **z** and **x**
  fill and mark in Nonogram, **1** to **9** fill a Sudoku, **Space** turns
  a Netwalk tile and **Enter** locks it
- **Mouse**: drag in Nonogram, Nurikabe, Shikaku, Spell Puzzle and Word
  Search; click to pick a cell, turn a tile or toggle a light
- **Ctrl+C**: quit

## Options

From a terminal: `puzzletea new sudoku hard` or `puzzletea new nonogram
classic` starts a puzzle at once, `--difficulty 1200` asks for a
difficulty from 0 to 3000, `--with-seed myseed` makes the same puzzle every
time, and `puzzletea continue <name>` takes up a saved one.
`puzzletea --help` lists them all.
