**FURS TO MARKET BY CANOE.**

Eight seasons as an independent fur trader on the north shore of Lake Ontario in the 1700s. Each autumn buy bales of trade goods and hire your voyageurs. Over the winter trade with the Anishinaabe, Odawa, Wendat and Haudenosaunee trappers who bring in the beaver, otter, marten, fox and mink. In spring choose where to sell: Fort Frontenac close by, Montreal down the rapids of the St. Lawrence, or Albany on the Hudson by way of Oswego, the Great Carrying Place and the Mohawk River.

- **A map of the routes** with your canoe on its way.
- **Storms, rapids, wet carries, rival traders** and the price of beaver in Europe.
- **A ledger and a season log,** and your fortune season by season; the best is kept.

## Controls

- **Tab** or **↑ ↓**: move between fields; typing or **↑ ↓** sets them
- **1** to **3**, the arrows or a click: choose the post; **Enter**: set out, go on
- **?**: the rules; **M**: sound on or off; **Esc**: quit
