**THREE LAPS. THREE PICKUPS. ONE TANK OF FUEL.**

The track is drawn in slashes, bars and underscores, and your car is a
single bright block screaming round it. Put your foot down, ease off
before the hairpins, and pick your next turn early, because a car in
ZRacer only turns at forty-five degrees and a corner taken too fast puts
you into the wall.

Every track asks the same of you: cross the line three times and
collect all three pickups, the **X** marks, before the clock runs out.
Fuel burns as you drive; run dry and your top speed sags, so grab an
**F** on the way past. Win a track and the next one unlocks, along with
new ways to race the old one:

- **Time Stop**: grab as many pickups as you can, each one buys more time
- **Count Down**: pickups tick down from 9 to 0 and vanish
- **Search**: find the pickups on a track you can only partly see

There are thirty tracks and a bonus level, a level editor for your own,
and options for every key and colour. Too hard? Turn the speed down, or
switch off real time and race it turn by turn.

## Controls

- **Space**: accelerate, and start the race; **b**: brake
- **Left** and **Right**: turn; **Up** and **Down**: turn upwards or downwards; **Enter**: go straight
- **g**: grab a pickup or fuel beside you; **v**: reverse (not in Laps)
- **p**: pause; **Esc**: the pause menu
- On a track's screen, **Left** and **Right** pick the track, **Up** and **Down** the mode
- Main menu: **h** help, **o** options, **l** load, **s** save, **e** level editor, **c** credits, **q** quit

## History

In 2007 the C-40 ZCode competition set one rule: the game had to fit in
40K. David Fisher wrote ZRacer in Inform 6 without any library and made
it exactly 40,960 bytes, a full racing game with thirty tracks, four
modes and an editor, on a virtual machine built for text adventures.
W. Doug Bolden, David Schweinsberg, Eric Forgeot and Michael Martin
tested it.

Saved games live in `~/.local/share/zracer-c40`.
