**TYPE FAST. TYPE COZY.**

Pick a test from the menu and start typing. The words wait in a few calm lines; what you've typed turns white, mistakes turn red. When you're done typioca shows your words per minute, your accuracy, your raw speed and a chart of your pace through the test, and keeps your results so you can see whether you're getting faster.

- **Three kinds of test**: against the clock (15, 30, 60 or 120 seconds), a word count (10 to 100 words) or a sentence count (1 to 30 sentences)
- **Classic books**: word and sentence lists from Frankenstein, Dracula, Pride and Prejudice, Sherlock Holmes, Treasure Island and more
- **Other keyboards**: Dvorak, Colemak DH and Gallium layouts
- **Your own words**: add word lists in the config file

## Controls

- **Up** and **Down**: choose a test in the menu; **Left** and **Right** move to its settings, where **Up** and **Down** change them
- **Enter**: start the test, or open Config
- Type the words; **Space** moves to the next one, **Backspace** fixes the current one, **Ctrl+W** deletes the last word
- **Ctrl+R**: restart the test; **Ctrl+Q**: back to the menu
- **Esc** or **Ctrl+C**: quit

## Options

- In the menu's Config screen, **e** turns a word list or layout on or off and **s** downloads or deletes it; lists beyond the two built in download from typioca's own repository
- `typioca serve`: run typioca as an SSH server on port 2229
