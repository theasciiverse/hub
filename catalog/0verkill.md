A bloody 2D deathmatch drawn entirely in ASCII art. Run, jump, creep and
climb through the levels, pick up weapons, armor and grenades, and frag
everyone else in sight. Started from Asciiverse, it opens a game on your
machine against two bots; friends on your network can join the same game.

## Controls

- **Left** and **Right**: run
- **Up**: jump (with a direction, jump aside)
- **Down**: creep; **C** toggles creeping
- **D**: climb down ladders and drop through platforms
- **Ctrl** or **Z**: fire
- **1** to **5**: change weapon
- **Enter**: chat

## Playing with friends

One player starts the game; everyone else joins it with
`0verkill -i <that player's address>`.

## History

Brainsoft, a Czech group at Charles University in Prague, released 0verkill
in 2000. Its code has been kept alive since: hackndev fixed it for
newer compilers (2008 to 2010), and ravener's revival (2021 to 2026) fixed
its network checksum and a memory bug.
