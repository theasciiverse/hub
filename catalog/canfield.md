**THE DEAL COSTS $13. THE CLOCK IS RUNNING.**

Canfield is solitaire with money on the table, and this one keeps the
books. The deal costs $13. A look at the game before you commit, another
$13. The rest of the game, $26 more. Every card you get onto a foundation
pays $5. Each extra run through the hand costs $5, card counting costs $1 a
card, and your think time is billed at $1 a minute. Your account carries
over from game to game, so the question is not whether you beat the hand.
It is whether you are up.

The rules are classic Canfield: thirteen cards in the stock, the first card
on the foundation setting the base rank for all four, the foundations
building up by suit and round the corner from king to ace, the tableau
building down in alternate colours and moving only as whole piles. Deal the
hand three at a time and look for the opening.

And from the manual's list of bugs: "It is impossible to cheat."

- **A running betting account**: costs, winnings, net worth and return,
  one keypress away
- **Card counting** on demand, at a price
- **A Berkeley classic** from 1980, with Kirk McKusick, Eric Allman and
  friends among its authors

## Controls

Each move is two letters or digits, typed at the Move prompt:

- **s#** and **sf**: stock to tableau pile #, or to the foundation
- **t#** and **tf**: talon to a tableau pile, or to the foundation
- **##**: tableau pile to tableau pile
- **#f**: tableau pile to the foundation
- **ht**: deal the hand to the talon
- **c**: card counting on or off
- **b**: the betting account, **i**: the instructions, **x**: hide the box
- **q**: give up this game

## History

Steve Levine wrote Canfield at UC Berkeley (the copyright is from 1980).
Steve Feldman moved it to curses and debugged it, Kirk McKusick and
Mikey Olson added card counting, McKusick the betting, and Eric Allman
and McKusick cleaned up the interface. This edition keeps the betting account
in the player's own data folder rather than a system file. It is part
of BSD Games in the Asciiverse catalog, under the BSD licence.
