**TWENTY PILES OF MONEY. ONE M.**

One screen, scattered with money `$`, mines `*` and crates `#`, and you
`@` somewhere in the middle of it. Collect every pile. The trouble is the
`M`: every step you take, it takes one towards you.

Mines finish you, and so does walking into the `M`. The crates are your
friends: push one into its path and it has to find a way round. Every
game deals a new screen, and at the end it tells you how much you
collected and how many steps it took.

## Controls

- **Arrow keys**: walk, and push a crate
- **q** or **Esc**: quit
