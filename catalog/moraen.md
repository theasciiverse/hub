**THE ICE IS GONE. THE TROLLS ARE STIRRING.**

When the great ice came down from the north, the dwarves sealed their treasure halls and fled. For ten thousand years the glacier lay over the mountain, and the stone trolls who guard its depths froze where they stood.

Now the ice has gone. All that's left is the moraine: rock, rubble, and somewhere underneath, the gems of the old halls. You're the youngest of the clan, sent back to bring the treasure home. And deep in the mountain, the trolls are starting to thaw...

Dig through the rubble and gather the gems. Collect enough and the old hall doors swing open. Mind the boulders: the moraine is still settling, and one careless dig brings a hillside down on your head. Drop a stone on a firefly and it bursts into flames; crush a butterfly and it scatters into gems. The old dwarf rune-stone still works after ten thousand years, turning every boulder that falls through it into treasure. And something green is creeping through the halls, filling every gap: trap it and it turns to gems, let it grow and it turns to stone.

But watch the boulders closely. Some of them blink.

A sleeping stone troll looks like any other rock, until you get close and it starts to blink. Come any closer, or let the thaw reach it, and it wakes: too heavy to climb, it stomps along the ground hurling stones at you, down from above or straight across the cave. Its touch is death. But drop a boulder on a troll and... OOF! Just another rock. And when the hall doors open, daylight floods in, and every troll turns back to stone.

- **Twenty caves and five bonus intermissions** across the land the ice left behind, from First Thaw to the troll-haunted Bergschrund.
- **Five levels of difficulty,** every cave reshuffled for each.
- **Faithful to the 1984 cave-digging classic,** down to the tick, plus trolls of our own.
- **Chunky pixel art or crisp character graphics,** keyboard or gamepad.

The clock is the thaw, and the trolls are waking. **DIG FAST!**

## Controls

- **Arrow keys** or **W A S D**: move and dig
- **Shift** with a direction, or **Space** / **Enter** held: grab or dig the next cell without moving
- **P**: pause, **Esc**: back
- **G**: pixel art or character graphics, **+** and **-**: zoom, **0**: automatic zoom
- **Gamepad**: stick or d-pad moves, any face button grabs, Start pauses

## Options

- `--cave N`: start at cave N (1 to 25)
- `--level N`: difficulty 1 to 5
- `--glyphs` or `--pixels`: the look to start in
