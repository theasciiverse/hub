**YOUR CALL, COACH.**

Two twenty-minute halves against Cedar State, with five-minute overtimes while the score is level. With the ball, call the shot: a long jump shot for three, a short jump shot, a layup, a set shot, or work it around for a better look. Without it, call the defence: a full-court press, man-to-man, a zone, or sag off and rest. Every call changes the odds both ways, and the other bench learns your habits.

- **A court that moves:** both teams in formation, the ball in flight.
- **Steals, fouls, free throws and rebounds,** with play-by-play.
- **Your record:** wins, losses and the biggest margin.

## Controls

- **1** to **5**, **↑ ↓** and **Enter**, or a click: call a play or a defence
- **Enter**: go on after the break and the final whistle
- **N**: a new game; **Tab**: the rules; **M**: sound on or off; **Esc**: quit
