**A PATCH OF WILD LAND. A HANDFUL OF PEASANTS. BUILD THEM A CITY.**

First, conjure a world. Choose its size, its climate, its rainfall and its mountains, and watch the kingdoms rise to claim it: humans and elves, dwarves and orcs, goblins, ogres, trolls, halflings, gnomes, minotaurs, rakshasa and naga. Then pick your people, choose your spot on the map, name your town, and begin.

Every tile around your walls is real land. Here the soil is good for wheat; there the forest hides deer and boar; up in the hills lie tin, copper and iron. Lay out farms and pastures, set up hunting camps, sink mines, raise hovels and houses. Wild things roam the wider world too: wolves and bears, lions and tigers, wyverns, manticores and basilisks, and the unicorn, which, the game's own notes say, is notoriously difficult to find.

Then make it thrive. Put your peasants to work, draw in merchants and burghers, and set the taxes for each class without souring their morale. Build the smeltery, the sawmill, the tailor and the mint, and turn ore into housewares, fur into coats and gold into coin. Your ministers bring their reports on finance, food, hunting, livestock, mines and trade, and the seasons turn a day or a week at a time.

The city is the heart of Edigotia, and it is all yours to grow.

- **Generated worlds** with climate, terrain and twelve races of kingdom
- **Land that matters**: every tile's crops, animals and ore shape what you can build
- **Peasants, merchants and burghers**, each with their own homes, taxes and moods
- **Workshops and trades**, from salt and furniture to jewellery and haute couture
- **Ministers' reports** to keep the whole thing in balance

## Controls

- On the first screen: **G** generates a world, **N** founds a city in
  it, **L** loads a saved city, **H** opens the help
- **1** to **5**: the Game, Ministers, Buildings, World and Help menus
- **A**: build an area (then pick its kind and place it with the
  movement keys and **Enter**); **C**, **O** and **D**: close, reopen
  and destroy an area
- **Enter**: what is on a tile, **T**: terrain view, **R**: your city's
  reach
- **.** and **>**: let a day or a week pass
- **1** then **1**: save and quit; **Load city** on the first screen
  picks it up again

## History

Whales, the creator of the roguelike Cataclysm, set out in 2014 to make
"a game of building, trade, diplomacy and war" in a fantasy world of
rival kingdoms. He built it in the open on GitHub, nearly nine hundred
commits over fourteen months, and poured most of them into the city: its
land, its trades, its people and their moods. In June 2015 he set it
aside. Edigotia lives on here, its cities founded, saved and returned to
once more.
