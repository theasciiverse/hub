**EVERY CELL A QUESTION. EVERY WORLD AN ANSWER.**

The window fills with question marks, and a world settles out of them.
Wave Function Collapse picks the most certain cell, decides it, and lets
the decision ripple to its neighbours, until oceans, forests, mountain
ridges and lava flows have found their places. Then a city does the same:
roads that join up, blocks of houses, shops, parks and ponds. Then
another landscape, and so on, each one new.

## Controls

- **Space**: the next world now
- **Any other key**: quit
