A dead-end alley in a foreign capital, and a plain metal door with no handle. You are a tourist, or so you say. Your interrogator has other ideas, and the story of how you got past that door is told, and retold, under questioning. Pay attention to what you say, and to what you do not.

## How to play

Type what you want to do, in plain words, and press Enter:

- **look**, **inventory** (or **i**), **examine** something (or **x**)
- **north**, **south**, **east**, **west**, **up**, **down** (or **n**, **s**, **e**, **w**, **u**, **d**)
- **take**, **drop**, **open**, **push** things; **help** or **about** in most games
- **save** and **restore**; **quit** to stop

Your saved games are kept in `~/.local/share/spider-and-web`.
