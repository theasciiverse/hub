**THE DUNGEONS OF DOOM, ONE YEAR DEEPER.**

A year after 3.6, the Berkeley team had another go at their dungeon. The
task has not changed: the local guildmasters send you down with an
enchanted mace, a bow and a quiver of arrows to fetch the Amulet of
Yendor from 26 levels below, and you only win if you carry it back up.
Neither have the monsters, twenty-six of them from the giant ant to the
zombie, with the rust monster, the nymph, the umber hulk and the dragon
on the way.

What changed is how much the game tells you. Press **D** and it lists
every potion, scroll, ring and wand you have identified so far. Point
**^** at a trap and it tells you what kind it is, before you step on it
twice.

- **A new map on every level**, drawn as you explore
- **Magic you learn by trying it**, and paying for it when it goes wrong
- **Death for good**, and a top ten for the gold you carry out
- **The same reward for the few who make it:** "Congratulations, you
  have made it to the light of day!"

**One more year of polish. The same appetite for adventurers.**

## Controls

- **hjkl yubn**: move, the last four diagonally; with **Shift** run that
  way
- **f** and a direction: move until something turns up; **t**: throw,
  **z**: zap a wand in a direction, **^** and a direction: say what a trap is
- **i**: inventory, **d**: drop, **D**: what you have discovered
- **e** eat, **q** quaff, **r** read, **w** wield, **W** wear, **T** take
  off armour, **P** put on and **R** remove a ring
- **s**: search for traps and secret doors, **.**: rest
- `>` and `<`: stairs down, and up once you carry the Amulet
- **?**: all the commands, **/**: what a symbol is
- **S**: save and quit, **Q**: quit

## Saving

**S** saves the game to your data folder. To carry on, start it from a
shell as `rogue52 -r`; a saved game is deleted once it is restored.

## History

Rogue 5.2 is Michael Toy, Glenn Wichman and Ken Arnold's Berkeley
version of 1982, between the 3.6 of BSD and the 5.4 that most later
ports grew from. Nicholas J. Kisseberth (Yendor) restored it in the
Roguelike Restoration Project, releases 5.2.1 to 5.2.3, and keeps it at
rogueforge. This edition keeps its files in your data folder and fixes an overflow in the top
ten. It is part of the Roguelike Restoration Project in the Asciiverse
catalog.
