The classic game of interstellar commerce for one to four players, taking
turns at one keyboard. Each turn you pick one of five sectors on the star
map. Land next to a star or an infant company and you found a shipping
company, with five free shares for the founder; land next to a company and
it grows. Then you trade: buy stock in the companies you think will
prosper, sell what looks doomed.

A company is worth $100 a share for every sector it holds, plus $500 for
every sector next to a star and minus $500 for every sector next to a black
hole. Push a rival's company into a black hole and its stock is gone. When
two companies touch, the bigger swallows the smaller: shares in the loser
turn into half as many in the winner, and its shareholders get a cash bonus.
Prices over $3,000 split two for one. Every turn pays you 5% of your
holdings in cash. The game ends when companies fill 54% of the map, and the
richest player wins.

## Controls

- **1** to **5**: take one of the five sectors numbered on the map
- **Up** and **Down** (or **k** and **j**): choose a company while trading
- **Enter**: trade in that company; type a number of shares (negative to
  sell), **m** for as many as you can afford, **n** to sell them all
- **Esc**: end your trading and pass to the next player
- **s**: the standings; **c**: company details (size and total worth)
- **q** or **Ctrl+C**: quit, after showing the final standings
- **Ctrl+L**: redraw the screen

## Options

- `-c`: force colour; `-m`: force black and white

## History

Steven Faber's Star Lanes was printed as a BASIC listing in Interface Age
in June 1977 and went round many home computers from there (Brian "Beej"
Hall traced it in his starlanes-info notes). Beej played it from a
First Osborne Group disk in about 1982 and wrote this curses version in C
in 1995 to 1997, releasing 1.2.2 in 1997. His version has black holes,
which the original did not, "to give players who have fallen behind a
chance to shaft the leaders". In 2026 he took it up again, broke the one big source file
into modules and released 1.4.0.
