**THE ALPHABET IS LOOSE. NAIL IT TO THE BULLSEYE.**

Twenty-six letters fly around the screen, bouncing off the edges, swooping
over a round target. Press a letter's key and that letter is yours: it
keeps going for a moment, slows, and stops dead wherever it lands. You get
ten shots. Make them count!

The trick is the drift. A letter doesn't stop when you press, it stops a
little later, so you are not aiming at where it is but at where it is
going. Every ring closer to the middle doubles the score, a letter that
misses the target costs you points, and one that stops exactly in the
centre is worth **a million**. Land close and the game says "Very
close..."; land perfect and it says "Bravo!"

- **Ten shots, twenty-six targets**: pick the letter in the best spot, or
  gamble on the one heading for the middle
- **Doubling rings**: every step inwards is worth twice the last
- **A high score list of eleven**: beat the record and the game names the
  champion you just knocked off

Needs an 80 by 24 terminal. **Steady hands. Ten darts. Go.**

## Controls

- **A** to **Z**: stop that letter
- **F1** or **?**: how to play
- **Esc**: end the round; at the end of a round, **n** quits and **y**
  plays again (pressing **Q** a second time in a round also ends it)
- **Ctrl-C**: quit at once

## History

Darrt is one of the games abakh wrote for New BSD Games, a collection of
small curses games started in 2018 and named after the classic BSD games.
The collection is packaged by Debian, Ubuntu, openSUSE, Alpine, Nix,
Homebrew, FreeBSD and pkgsrc, and is in the AUR.
