You boasted, after too much to drink, that you could take on Malygris single-handed, and the prince's mage shoved you through a portal to prove it. Now you are in the dungeon of Kerkerkruip, a new one every game, with a few powers, a few items and a tension meter that keeps rising. A roguelike played by typing commands, with tactical fights and permanent death.

## How to play

Type what you want to do, in plain words, and press Enter:

- **look**, **inventory** (or **i**), **examine** something (or **x**)
- **north**, **south**, **east**, **west**, **up**, **down** (or **n**, **s**, **e**, **w**, **u**, **d**)
- **take**, **drop**, **open**, **push** things; **help** or **about** in most games
- **save** and **restore**; **quit** to stop

Your saved games are kept in `~/.local/share/kerkerkruip`.
