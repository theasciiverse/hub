**BREAK THEM ALL.**

A wall of coloured bricks, a ball, and your paddle at the bottom. Keep
the ball in play and knock out every brick to move on. Seven levels, each
with more bricks hanging lower, and three lives for each one.

Where you catch the ball matters: the ends of the paddle send it off to
the side, the middle maybe straight up, and a paddle on the move whacks it
the way it is going. Some bricks drop a charm as they break; catch it:

- **%**: a thousand points
- **@**: an extra life
- **#**: ten seconds of slow ball
- **^**: three seconds of nuclear ball, straight through everything

Points for every brick, a bonus for every level, and a few more just for
staying alive.

## Controls

- **← / →**: move the paddle
- **L**: launch the ball
- **Space**: pause
- **s**: save the game; **o**: load it
- **Esc**, then **y**: quit

## Options

From a terminal: `bricktick --level=4` starts at that level,
`bricktick --nocolor` plays without colour.
