Slide the blocks around the tray until they stand where the puzzle
wants them. Blocks only slide into empty space; they cannot be lifted
or turned. Each puzzle has a known shortest solution, counted in steps
(a step is any number of slides of one block), and the game shows how
close you came.

Twelve classic puzzles are included, among them H. E. Dudeney's
Motor-Car Problem (1903), Sam Loyd's Moving Day Puzzle (1914) and John
Conway's Century-and-a-Half (1982), and more can be added as text files.

## Controls

- **Arrow keys**: move the selection square by square
- **h j k l**: select the next block in that direction
- **H J K L**: slide the selected block; or drag a block with the mouse
- **g**: show the goal
- **x** / **z**: undo / redo a slide; **X** / **Z**: undo / redo a step
- **R**: start again; **s** / **r**: save / restore a position
- **N** / **P**: next / previous puzzle
- **?**: help; **q**: quit
