**THE ALIENS ARE ALREADY HERE.**

It started with a message carried by hand from Titan Base: something alien is out in the Saturn system. Then the alien fighters came for Titan Base itself.

You fly a small starfighter, and out here nothing is faked: real Newtonian physics, real gravity, real planets at their real distances. Point your nose at a target and you'll sail straight past it! Learn to thrust, brake and coast, swing into orbit, match speeds and dock, and then do it all again with alien fighters on your tail.

Learn to fly out to the Moon and to Mars and back. Defend Titan Base. Hunt a hidden communications station in the Cassini Division of Saturn's rings. Pull the truth out of an alien data store: the whole alien presence at Saturn was a deception, and the real invasion is heading for Jupiter! Then chase the war out to Uranus and Neptune, back in to Mars, where a vast alien energy collector waits for a slow, mighty gun called Bertha, and finally to a strange portal orbiting Pluto.

- **Steve Belczyk's whole 1999 ORBIT campaign,** 28 missions from Earth orbit to Pluto, ported from his own C source.
- **Episode Two: Open Skies,** a new campaign: the aliens are gone and the science missions fly again, with salvager pirates and the breakaway Jovian Combine in the way. Its first part is here.
- **Space drawn in Braille:** ships, planets with real surface maps (press T), 2000 real stars from the Yale Bright Star Catalogue, and constellation lines (press B).
- **An orbit autopilot** (press G) that swings you into a perfect equatorial orbit, with the classic starship-in-orbit camera shots.

The solar system needs a pilot who understands orbits. **ARE YOU THAT PILOT?**

## Controls

- **W** / **S** or **Up** / **Down**: thrust forward and back
- **A** / **D** or **Left** / **Right**: yaw, **I** / **K**: pitch, **J** / **L**: roll
- **X**: warp engines, **H**: hold still (needed to dock)
- **Space**: fire, **U**: lock the nearest enemy, **Y**: next target, **F**: next weapon
- **G**: orbit autopilot, **C**: camera (in orbit: the two orbit shots and the cockpit)
- **T**: planet surfaces, **B**: constellation lines, **N**: names, **O**: the orrery, **Z**: dense star field
- **M**: recall the last mission message, **P**: pause, **Q** or **Esc**: back to the title (progress is saved)
